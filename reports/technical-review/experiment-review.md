## Scientific Decisions

1. **Registration Scope:** A timestamped analysis plan makes changes auditable; it cannot prevent amendments or guarantee sound inference. Preserve original plans, dated amendments, outcome-access state, predeclared contingencies, exploratory analyses and unfavorable outcomes. An inspected pilot is not untouched confirmation. Protocol completeness alone does not qualify an instrument or study.
2. **Measurement and Identifiability:** Calibrated wrenches, reconstructed power, stiffness, muscle force and model counterfactuals have different observational requirements. A split-grip transducer does not directly measure every individual hand-wrench component. Timing uncertainty includes clock offset/drift, latency, resampling and event localization; covariance assumptions matter. Provider sampling rates and tolerances are protocol-specific. Preserve trial dispositions and differential quality failures; missing data are not measured zeros.
3. **Filtering and Closure:** Even two zero-phase filters can attenuate a shared signal differently. In the constant-mass illustration, residual r = H_F F - m H_a a = m(H_F-H_a)a. Matching the linear filters closes this particular example, not arbitrary nonlinear multibody dynamics. The periodic test excludes finite-record boundary effects; the prose separately explains acausal event/edge contamination. Sidestep and landing studies do not establish a universal golf cutoff.
4. **Control and Null Boundaries:** Frame/reference invariance, physical interventions and deliberately corrupted channels test different propositions. For opposite forces, M = (r_L-r_R) cross f + C_L + C_R. Zero separation removes only the separated-force couple; coincident hands with direct z-moment projections -0.3 and -0.2 N·m retain -0.5 N·m. Human geometry changes can change force/control as well. Temporal surrogates and label permutations must respect dependence and the actual randomization.
5. **Causal Mediation:** Randomizing an instruction does not randomize a measured mediator. With independent centered unit-variance T, U and E, M=T+U+E and Y=U yield a naive regression product 1 times 1/2 despite zero structural effect through M. U is a pretreatment common cause; E is independent variation in M, not a separate observation-error model. Adjustment for observed U removes this association in the toy. Hierarchical modeling alone does not identify mediation. A failed manipulation check does not erase an instruction's possible total effect through other pathways.
6. **Participant Inference:** Separate prediction for new golfers from later swings of known golfers, and prescribed baseline calibration from test-outcome leakage. For a balanced random-intercept grand mean, variance is sigma_u^2/n + sigma_e^2/(nm). Twelve people, ten swings each, between-person variance 4 and within-person variance 1 give 0.3417 versus a naive 0.0417, ratio 8.2. This is not a universal design effect for paired contrasts, where stable intercepts may cancel. Plan the actual estimand, covariance, missingness and multiplicity.
7. **Decision Regions:** Nonsignificance is not equivalence. In the idealized normal known-standard-error example, estimate zero with s=0.2 N·m gives a 90% interval approximately [-0.329,0.329] within illustrative bounds +/-0.5 N·m; s=0.5 gives approximately [-0.822,0.822], inconclusive. The interval corresponds to alpha=.05 TOST under the stated model, not a universal interval level, golf threshold, exact-zero finding or exemption from multiplicity/cluster inference.
8. **Evidence and Readiness:** Distinguish bounded support, contradiction, inconclusive results and unexecuted tests; update the figure accordingly. A synthetic intake dry run and computational qualification do not establish human evidence or laboratory readiness. The pinned provider's missing governed full-contract dataset does not mean no relevant study exists anywhere. State staged testing, consent/data authority, model fitting versus prediction and hypotheses with outcomes that remain open.
