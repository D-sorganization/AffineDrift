The adjacent chapters, notebook scaffold, full equilibrium simulation, parameter fitting and human validation are outside this correction. Source checkpoint precedes public-link and audit completion; do not infer publication from a rebuilt PDF.

## Findings and Decisions

1. The Gaussian gives 0.573753 at normalized lengths 0.5 and 1.5, not nearly zero. Its half-height interval is approximately (0.441505, 1.558495), a curve property rather than an anatomical range.
2. The original passive implementation had an extra negative shape-factor offset in the exponent. It jumped negative just above normalized length one, was zero at 1.6 and reached one at 2.2. The corrected law is nonnegative, continuous and normalized to one at 1.6. Thelen's shape factor is five. Derivative discontinuity at slack and unbounded exponential extrapolation are explicit.
3. The original velocity law and units did not match the attributed formulation. The replacement is the algebraic inverse of Thelen's interior velocity-as-force equation: c(a)=0.25+0.75a; shortening ends at -c(a)\*v0, the eccentric multiplier approaches 1.4, and the one-sided slopes at zero differ by a factor of two. It rejects inadmissible shortening; it does not implement OpenSim's regularized continuations.
4. The listing evaluates a prescribed fiber state. It does not enforce series equilibrium or infer fiber states from joint motion. The stored tendon slack length is explicitly unused until tendon/path equations are supplied. BIClong parameters are traced to one immutable Arm26 asset, not both biceps heads or an individual golfer. Impossible pennation is rejected, not clipped.
5. Fiber length requires initialization. An initially isometric root needs an interval, residual and root-selection policy; existence and uniqueness are not guaranteed. The undamped force inversion requires positive activation/active-length multiplier/cosine and an attainable ratio. Slack tendon with positive passive fiber tension cannot satisfy its nonnegative active balance. The declared state space reflects these restrictions.
6. The single-exponential tendon remains a declared teaching choice. Its reference force cannot identify its shape factor; slope or another measurement is needed. The exercises now supply shape, scale, bounded length domains and comparison conditions. Force evaluation, equilibrium simulation, damped simulation and rigid-tendon models answer different questions.
7. Constant-thickness geometry gives dx/dl=1/cos(alpha), not cos(alpha). Together with axial force projection, this preserves power. Virtual work gives Q=-FT\*gradient(lMT); skeletal power equals active fiber output less the rates of passive-fiber and tendon storage. Mechanical output does not determine metabolic cost, neural policy or the cause of segment deceleration.
8. Joint tangent stiffness includes both force-length response and changing moment arms: sum(k*lprime^2+F*lsecond). Equal excitation of unequal antagonists does not preserve equilibrium in general. Fixed-state response, quasistatic equilibration and dynamic impedance must be distinguished.
9. The activation vector field is continuous at excitation=activation, although derivatives generally jump. Analytic step times are 68.496968 ms to 0.95 and 174.919985 ms from one to 0.05 for the stated 15/50 ms base constants. These are activation-state calculations, not measured force-rise or conduction delays.

## Primary Evidence and Reading Scope

- [Thelen 2003 author PDF](https://uwnmbl.engr.wisc.edu/pubs/jbme03.pdf): methods, parameters and Appendix; physical pages 6–7 visually inspected. The methods explicitly give activation/deactivation constants 15/50 ms; Table 1 gives young-adult eccentric limit 1.4 and passive strain 0.6. Appendix provides the constitutive equations. No new biological experiment or full-paper simulation replication.
- [Millard et al. 2013 author PDF](https://mmillard.com/pdf/MillardUchidaSethDelp2013.pdf): pages 1–4 derivations and selected page 8 benchmark discussion. The paper distinguishes equilibrium, damped equilibrium and rigid-tendon models with separately constructed smooth curves. Rat/cat soleus comparisons do not establish a universal human or golf ranking. No full-paper visual audit claimed.
- [OpenSim Thelen model documentation](https://opensimconfluence.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/OpenSim/pages/53089814/Thelen+2003+Muscle+Model): introductory implementation description; equation extraction was incomplete, so source and Appendix pixels supply the equation evidence.
- [OpenSim source](https://github.com/opensim-org/opensim-core/blob/2aaa04b33463076edb06625693ad9bf98cf3bea4/OpenSim/Actuators/Thelen2003Muscle.cpp): defaults, passive curve and force-velocity routines (lines 179–190, 1440–1466, 1505–1576 at that revision). This is implementation provenance, not a runtime dependency.
- [Arm26 asset](https://github.com/opensim-org/opensim-models/blob/d9b05d470b1a481c222372c85b75772faf8f7792/Models/Arm26/arm26.osim): BIClong values 624.3 N, 0.1157 m fiber length, 0.2723 m tendon slack length and zero pennation. SHA256 e2224d0044eb393b05d64926c3fa1682c451a9adc7f510e5517ef9958d3d41b9. This does not reproduce original anatomical calibration.
- [Hill 1938 original paper](https://math.nyu.edu/~peskin/eb_lecture_notes/Hill_AV_1938.pdf): introduction and selected force-velocity passages, including physical page 26/printed page 161. It motivates the shortening relation; the whole paper was not read or visually audited.
- [Gordon, Huxley and Julian 1966](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/5921536/): primary-paper metadata and indexed abstract concerning single frog fibers, plateau and length dependence. Full text was inaccessible through the PMC challenge; no full-paper reading claim.
- [Alexander 2002](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/12485689/): metadata and abstract on tendon elasticity in locomotion. The chapter makes no golf-specific metabolic or recoil-strategy claim from it.
- [Hill 1950 metadata](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/15430325/): corrected title to The series elastic component of muscle and DOI 10.1098/rspb.1950.0035. This was a metadata correction, not a new full-paper reading. The unsupported Winters1984 record was removed after checking its sole citation was in this chapter; no replacement metadata was invented.
