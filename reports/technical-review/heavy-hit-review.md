## Argument and Corrections

The article asks how delivered motion, local shaft response, grip loading,
stored elastic energy, contact traction, structural vibration, radiation, and
perception can interact. These are connected mechanisms with different
observables. A possible path through the model does not establish a measurable
effect in a particular golfer or an improvement in perceived sweetness.

1. **Consistent Closing Summary.** Related Concepts had restored categorical
   physical decoupling and a ground-force cutoff after the body withdrew them.
   The corrected summary distinguishes a contact reference calculation,
   continuing boundary loads, delivered state, and finite-duration transmission.
   Software staging alone says nothing about physical isolation. Engine
   interchange requires checked states, contact laws, and event conventions.
2. **Declare the Impedance Port.** The frequency-domain mobility now states a
   stationary support, a frozen linear reduction, colocated power-conjugate
   quantities, and the wrench sign. Club-to-boundary wrench is positive into
   the eliminated subsystem; its structural reaction is negative. Elimination
   therefore adds positive `i omega L^T Z L` to dynamic stiffness. The same hand
   inertia, stiffness, or damping must not also appear in the retained matrices.
   Poles and transient initial conditions remain explicit limits.
3. **Retain Moving-Support Forcing.** A scalar two-spring/damper boundary with an
   intervening mass gives `F_g = D_cc x + D_cb b`. Direct elimination from the
   hand equation gives the displayed coefficients. At nonzero mass,
   `D_cc + D_cb = -D_g m_g omega^2 / D_h`, so substituting relative velocity into
   the fixed-support impedance loses an acceleration-dependent force. Equal
   endpoint displacements do not force zero internal inertia. The massless
   case is explicitly distinguished. Both driven-port powers enter the energy
   balance; a force's sign alone cannot identify its work.
4. **Acoustic Evidence Limits.** Delaye's plate study used an uncalibrated
   microphone and adjusted structural parameters. Its relative spectra and
   decay comparisons, including discrepancies, are a modeling precedent; they
   do not validate absolute pressure or a new club/grip prediction. Retain the
   existing separation of contact spectrum, structural response, radiation,
   microphone/room transfer, and listener judgments.
5. **Hypotheses Are Proposals.** The research table describes candidate
   hypotheses for preregistered studies, not completed experiments or existing
   public preregistrations. Preserve the old section anchor; require declared
   interventions, outcomes, uncertainty, and equivalence margins before data.
6. **Stable Evidence Links.** Pin the historical review/inventory to existing
