# Ground-Reaction Chapter Scientific Review

## Scope and Findings

Issue [#4415](https://github.com/D-sorganization/AffineDrift/issues/4415) continues
Physics #4054, corpus #4021 and epic #4009. Both complete Chapter 15 sources were
read, including every original print subsection and exercise. The prior web-only
acceptance did not cover the much less accurate print edition. Its route was
reopened while the paired correction and rendering checks were performed.

Six findings organize the correction:

1. **P1 — System boundary and momentum.** Weight and normal reaction on one body
   are not a third-law pair. Include the club or its external hand wrench; add
   ball impulse at impact. Correct the vertical sign and 90 kg/1200 N example
   from 23.1 to 3.52 m/s² upward. Distinguish velocity from acceleration and
   impulse from energy or a prescribed segment sequence.
2. **P1 — Wrenches and work.** Derive COP for an offset surface and retain its
   free couple. Explain consistent wrench/velocity transport, material versus
   COP-location velocity, deformable contact, ideal rolling and moving supports.
   Distinguish translational COM kinetic energy from actual contact work.
3. **P1 — Reaction and input attribution.** Derive the Schur reaction under
   explicit rank, state, load and contact assumptions. Replace the unsupported
   shoulder-to-GRF shortcut with an independently checked coupled model whose
   reaction depends on input while contact power is zero. Handle redundant
   constraints, unilateral feasibility, friction and moving-support bias.
4. **P1 — Inference and human evidence.** Net inverse joint moments are not
   unique muscle forces or activations. Neither force peaks nor COP identify
   universal swing faults. Separate held-out prediction from fitted consistency
   and use the exact COM balance fairly as a baseline. Bound observational
   associations and constructed transfer indices without assuming causality.
5. **P2 — Bibliography.** Correct three author lists in both the textbook and
   shared proximal-distal bibliography using primary publication records.
6. **P2 — Paired pedagogy and presentation.** Give both editions eight worked
   solutions. Preserve the public route, original equation targets and print
   chapter/section targets. Split four wide display groups for mobile reading,
   supply the browser page title and fix print heading breaks.

## Argument and Source Boundaries

The revision connects external support, internally generated motion, energy
accounting and input attribution through one declared model. Its algebraic
examples prove identities and counterexamples; they are not measured golfer
parameters or calibrated physiological predictions.

Primary records and the following accessible material were checked:

- [Han and colleagues](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31042142/): abstract and
  bibliographic record; 63 skilled male golfers, selected force/moment
  associations with speed, not an intervention prescribing maximal force.
- [Ball and Best](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/17454544/): abstract and record;
  multiple COP patterns across skill levels, not one optimal template.
- [Worsfold and colleagues](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/24149910/): abstract;
  shoe/skill/club conditions limit the interpretation of shoe-ground torque.
- [Joo, Oh and Mun](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/27515436/): abstract and
  record; pressure-derived predictions of unmeasured wrench components.
  Correct names are Su-Bin Joo, Seung Eel Oh and Joung Hwan Mun. The abstract's
  cross-validation statement does not establish participant-independent folds.
- [Sturdy, Silverman and Pickle](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35460934/):
  abstract and record; automated RRA weight selection reduces manual tuning,
  not independent golf-specific attribution validation. Those three authors
  replace the unrelated authors formerly attached to this citation.
- [AddBiomechanics](https://journals.plos.org/plosone/article?id=10.1371/journal.pone.0295152):
  publisher author list and methods; measured forces enter dynamic-consistency
  optimization. Correct Jon Stingel and restore Steven H. Collins and C. Karen
  Liu. Andrea Tigrini is the editor, not an additional author.
- [Rachnavy and colleagues](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/sports-and-active-living/articles/10.3389/fspor.2026.1790645/full):
  methods, regression, mediation and limitations sections. The initial block
  includes skill group; cross-sectional association and a constructed transfer
  factor do not establish causal energy transmission or a thermodynamic
  efficiency. No new effect-size claim is introduced.
- [Watson and colleagues](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s40279-025-02391-3):
  publisher abstract, introduction and methods; varied methods/terminology
  constrain synthesis. This is a systematic review, not an independent
  controlled intervention supporting a universal waveform.
- [Gatt and colleagues](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/9548125/): abstract
  confirms modeled knee kinetics from motion capture and plates; no clinical
  return-to-sport prescription is adopted. The
  [Nesbit primary paper](https://www.jssm.org/volume04/iss4/cap/jssm-04-499.pdf)
  is retained as the established model-based golf-kinetics reference.

The bibliography changes alter only author fields for joo2016, sturdy2022 and
werling2023. Chapters 16 and 22 do not cite these keys. Their unchanged content
and other evidence must be verified before carrying forward the dependency
digests; metadata correction is not a new scientific review of those chapters.

## Verification and Publication Boundary

Eleven independent numerical cases test force/impulse signs, system-boundary
cancellation, offset COP, wrench-power invariance, direct KKT versus Schur
solutions, input-induced reaction, infeasible zero-input contact, bilateral
nonuniqueness and moving-support work. All pass, as do five existing chapter
contract checks and 54 LaTeX structure/environment tests. Black100, Ruff,
the 638-source title audit and citation audit pass.

The isolated chapter compiles with the actual book preamble and bibliography
to 13 pages. Initial inspection found awkward heading breaks; these were fixed.
The standard public verifier passes four viewport/theme cases with zero serious
or critical axe violations. Settled scrolling renders all 118 expressions in
each case. Visual inspection exposed clipped mobile displays missed by the
page-level gate; the final line breaks remove display and page overflow while
preserving all checked equation anchors. No MathJax error or lazy placeholder
remains. The companion JSON records the final verification scope.

Review evidence must be committed and bound before route acceptance. Protected
merge and exact-main live deployment are separate gates. Neither this chapter
review nor its isolated PDF establishes whole-book or whole-corpus acceptance.
