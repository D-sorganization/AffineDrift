## Findings and Corrections

1. **Exact Doppler Ambiguity.** Replace the supposed six-unknown per-frame solve with the single-origin factorization and exact three-dimensional null family. Distinguish rank loss from weak remaining sight-line geometry, and additional positional/array information from Doppler-only rows.
2. **Consistent Coordinates.** Keep linear-first ordering throughout; distinguish body-reference world velocity from body and spatial twists. Declare the reference point, adjoint interpretation, wedge convention, and multiplication side for integration.
3. **Axis and Plane Limits.** Derive the nonzero-angular-speed screw axis and pitch; handle pure translation, rest, and low-speed sensitivity. An axis trajectory is not a uniquely defined plane or evidence of a hand-centered pivot.
4. **Conditional Covariance.** State the full-rank fixed-design GLS conditions; reject inverse-normal covariance for the singular problem and zero-uncertainty interpretations of pseudoinverse null modes. Include scaling, position errors, correlations, robust fitting, and model discrepancy.
5. **Temporal Inference.** Give a smooth Doppler-history ambiguity; require an augmented multi-time measurement/process model. A smooth prior can determine an unobserved direction without measuring it.
6. **Sensor Envelope.** Distinguish OPS243-A from C; do not treat spectral peaks/width/midpoints as uniquely calibrated point speed or angular speed. K-LD7 speed is radial and one angle is not 3D localization; aliasing does not automatically preserve a valid ball-angle estimate.
7. **Coupled Waveform Arithmetic.** Correct per-transmitter repetition, sample counts, TDM speed limit/resolution/acquisition time, sampled bandwidth, and range migration. Explicitly identify the conflict between conventional three-TX >50 m/s unambiguous Doppler and a 4-GHz ramp at the specified maximum slope. No firmware capability is claimed from arithmetic alone.
8. **Geometric and Temporal Data.** Convert spherical measurements to Cartesian correctly; preserve correlations and acquisition intervals. A changing weighted glint centroid is not a body-fixed point. Speed-only clutter or shaft rejection is not guaranteed.
9. **Impact Boundary.** Separate last pre-contact estimate, first contact, maximum compression, and separation; disclose extrapolation, trigger and event-time errors. No universal 500-microsecond rigid/nonrigid switch.
10. **Parameter Definitions.** Declare a right-handed target frame and golfer-facing path sign. Derive face-normal azimuth rate rather than substitute a shaft-axis component. Require independent trajectory-plane and low-point definitions; a vertical-velocity zero may be a maximum.
11. **Mechanics Attribution.** A pre/post angular change is net kinematics, not uniquely gear effect; motion does not identify golfer torques or intention. Representation alone never promotes an estimated parameter to a measured one.
12. **Validation Adequacy.** Explicitly proposed synthetic/rig/comparator checks must test null cases, coverage, calibration, rejected cases, and independent reference validity. A pendulum's angular history is not analytically known just because its axis is fixed; identities satisfied by construction are not independent validation.
13. **Primary-Study Scope.** Vena's segment ISA study is not club-radar validation or a universal golfer sequence. Correct Part 1 year/pages while retaining the permanent key; make publisher-preview scope explicit.
14. **Bibliography and Claims.** Remove the obsolete free-book-availability promise; add author robotics and TI primary references; update inspected datasheet revisions. Remove unsupported vendor-algorithm, novelty, exclusivity, and freedom-to-operate implications.
15. **Dependent Summaries.** Replace the README's Appendix E summary and stale artifact counts. Add only a qualification notice to the historical research outline; native epic child #4719 retains its complete-review scope.
