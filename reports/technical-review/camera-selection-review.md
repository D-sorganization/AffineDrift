# Markerless Camera Selection Technical Review

Issue: [#4471](https://github.com/D-sorganization/AffineDrift/issues/4471), under corpus #4021 and epic #4009. Reviewed 2026-09-27.

## Scope and Findings

The complete article and all 57 registry claims were inspected. This review changes public evidence and explanation, not Tools/UpstreamDrift capture contracts or runtime. No camera was connected and no physical experiment was performed.

1. **Model and mode identity.** LUCID TRT016S-CC uses IMX273 and lists 166.3 fps at 1440 by 1080. The previous IMX296/226 combination was wrong. Allied's exact color C-mount datasheet lists 1632 by 1248 and 225 fps, explicitly at 525 MByte/s in Mono8; its RGB8 default cannot inherit that rate. Basler's current documentation distinguishes a 1936-by-1216 full region from the 1920-by-1200 default and lists 164 fps at default settings. Mode conditions now travel with every rate.
2. **Sensor depth and transport.** A 10/12-bit ADC is not the delivered bits per pixel. Packed samples, container sizes and RGB channels differ. FLIR's full-resolution BayerRG8 table changes from 226 to 165 fps between 10-bit and 12-bit ADC settings. Payload calculations use image bytes and explicit hypothetical mode screens.
3. **Capacity versus traffic.** The old LUCID 2.812-Gbit/s payload exceeds its 2.5-Gbit/s interface before overhead. Corrected eight-bit arithmetic gives 2.069 Gbit/s. Nominal interface capacity does not determine payload or host CPU use. Eight cameras do not mathematically require Ethernet; shared uplinks, host buses, burst buffering and storage must be sized and measured for the chosen topology.
4. **Clock versus exposure.** PTP aligns clocks; trigger scheduling and latency govern acquisition. Exposure duration changes effective image time. Packet arrival is separate. Vendor timing figures are not reconstructed-position bounds. The LUCID tutorial demonstrates separate clock, trigger and transfer configuration on other models, so its exact capabilities are not silently transferred to Triton2.
5. **Stereo and multi-view uncertainty.** The depth expression is derived for rectified pinhole stereo with fixed calibration. Disparity covariance includes left/right correlation. The general local weighted least-squares covariance needs identifiable geometry and an appropriate image-noise model; calibration and timing uncertainty remain additional inputs. Conditioning, bias and detector confidence are distinct.
6. **Connection to swing mechanics.** Independent-noise central-difference examples demonstrate amplification in derivatives. Smooth pose does not establish acceleration accuracy. Inverse dynamics additionally requires external loads, inertial properties and model assumptions; camera selection cannot validate muscle-force or neural-control conclusions.
7. **Research transfer.** Nakano's two-person experiment changed rate and resolution together across walking, jumping and throwing. OpenCap temporally aligned keypoint trajectories in software and estimated dynamics through models. Song's abstract reports joint/movement-dependent agreement and larger hip differences, including running; it does not establish absolute anatomical truth or a universal speed law.
8. **Purchasing and license evidence.** Historical prices remain historical, with current comparable regional prices unavailable. The FLIR page changed its displayed dollar amount; the ZED store redirects without the earlier configured quote. Generic lens-cost ranges were removed. GS optics are narrow/wide; the current fisheye option belongs to the S model. Arena's product page is not a license agreement, and GenICam access alone does not establish an intended GenTL producer. Missing exact-version license terms remain unavailable. Vendor agreements and separately licensed components do not change the optional-adapter boundary.
9. **Authority limits.** Registry schema validation can catch missing fields and inconsistent status but cannot prove source support. The article now says this explicitly. All recommendations remain provisional/evaluation-only and procurement remains denied. Driver capability, calibration, exposure, reconstruction and downstream output each require their own evidence.
