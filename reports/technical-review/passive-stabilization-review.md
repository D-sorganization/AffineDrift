## Argument and Technical Changes

The chapter asks how geometry, muscle activation, elasticity and feedback jointly reject a specified disturbance while carrying energetic and loading costs. Its existing distinction between repeatability, Lyapunov stability, attraction and a physiological mechanism is essential. A golf impact configuration can be repeatable without being an autonomous attractor. A numerical zero-input trajectory can retain activation and stored energy, and does not identify metabolic effort. Those qualifications remain intact.

The added bridge makes the existing preload caveat computable. At a smooth conservative static equilibrium with regular stationary holonomic constraints and the chapter's positive reaction convention, `grad U = A^T lambda`. For a tangent basis `N`, the restoring stiffness is `N^T (U_qq - sum lambda_i phi_i,qq) N`. The multiplier is held fixed in the ambient Hessian. Differentiating a feasible curve twice independently recovers the reaction-curvature term; projecting only the ambient potential Hessian loses it. This is a geometric consequence of the applied preload, consistent with zero net work by ideal stationary reactions.

The manufactured circle example uses metres, a stated potential and fixed spring parameters. With radius 1 m, spring constants 100 and 40 N/m, and horizontal spring offset 0.8 m, the tangent stiffness is 20 N/m and stable static compliance is 0.05 m/N. Offset 0.5 m gives -10 N/m and an unstable conservative positive-mass equilibrium despite a positive ambient Hessian. Constraint rank remains unchanged. The example is not a human tissue measurement, a golf posture recommendation, or a general treatment of nonconservative follower loads. Angular energy curvature equals radius squared times tangent-displacement stiffness; division converts back.

The existing moment-arm chain rule is retained with an explicit quasistatic constitutive approximation. Force-velocity and internal-state variations require additional derivatives; fixed activation alone does not erase them. The original unloaded compliance formula, oscillator poles, scheduled-spring energy identity and UCM variance decomposition are also retained. Constant state scaling is now explicit in the UCM Jacobian/covariance, and the optimization specifies its task error and sampled rate. Equivalent Quarto reflow introduces only a local stage-cost abbreviation. Print title wrapping preserves the original short title and label.
