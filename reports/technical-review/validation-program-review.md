## Technical Reasoning

Same-shot subtraction removes the shared physical quantity only when the two outputs represent that same quantity in the same condition. Reference bias and noise remain; positive shared error covariance can reduce apparent disagreement. This is an identifiability issue, not a claim about any named vendor. Conversely, dispersed shot outcomes do not isolate either motor variability or sensor error. Golf-swing explanations must connect delivered impact state, physical ball flight, instrument inference and the chosen statistical estimand.

Agreement limits concern the distribution of individual differences. A confidence interval for a mean shrinks as independent information increases; the underlying individual spread does not. The repeated-block equation follows by summing all covariance entries in the mean. Its independence/equal-block assumptions are stated, and it is explicitly illustrative. It does not claim to fit, validate, or replace the campaign's eventual hierarchical model.

For independent same-instrument errors at a fixed underlying quantity, difference variance is twice the within-device error variance. A device–reference difference instead combines two unknown variances and possibly their covariance. Multiple decompositions give the same observed difference variance, so the misleading software field is explained rather than silently treated as an instrument property. No upstream implementation is modified by this publication review.

The sample-size arithmetic is one normal-approximation power calculation for detecting a specified mean difference. Practical agreement, simultaneous endpoint claims, missingness, repeated blocks and uncertainty of the limits are separate design requirements. The accepted-set gate is unchanged. The text requests a frozen acquisition policy; it does not fabricate collection, authorize funding, or declare the external physical campaign complete.
