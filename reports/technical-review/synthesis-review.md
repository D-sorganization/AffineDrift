## Scientific Decisions

1. **System and Outcome:** Define the system boundary, outcome, comparison and event before causal interpretation. Hand-to-club transfer is internal to an arm-plus-club system; muscle action can convert chemical to mechanical energy, and stationary ideal ground contact can exchange momentum without work. A fixed-time delivery event is not a resolved collision. Wrench, velocity, work and biological demand are separate observables.

2. **Wrench and Power:** Use the rigid-club identity P = F dot v_C + M_C dot omega, including force-generated and direct moments about the same point. The manufactured planar example produces resultant (0,50) N, moment -2 Nm and positive signed angular velocity 3 rad/s: COM velocity(2,1)m/s gives50W translational and-6W rotational power, total44W. Application-point powers37W and7W sum consistently. Torque direction alone does not identify power, passivity, muscle work or later benefit. Flexible and slipping interfaces require their own kinematics/storage/dissipation.

3. **Allocation and Feasibility:** Collinear equal/opposite added hand forces leave the net wrench and total44W invariant while changing individual powers to137W and-93W. A manufactured50N per-hand bound admits the first allocation and excludes the second. This illustrates a wrench-map null direction, not an empirical grip limit or complete trajectory equivalence. Contact, joint, actuator and state-history restrictions remain part of feasibility; generalized actuators are not identified muscle groups.

4. **Energy and Task Velocity:** With mass1kg, central inertia0.25kg m^2, COM velocity(0,2)m/s and head offset(0.5,0)m, angular velocities+/-2rad/s have equal2.5J kinetic energy but head speeds3/1m/s. These same-pose instantaneous states disprove unique task-speed inference from scalar energy, without proving joint reachability from matched initial conditions. A useful strategy must create a feasible task state while respecting orientation, loading and impact conditions.

5. **Causal Comparisons and Transmission:** Separate fixed-complete-state attribution, evolving copied-state branches, changed preparation and retuned policies. Recompute reactions, retain controller/actuator/flexible states and declare prospective information. Matching peak torque, impulse or work imposes different controls. Force-direction algebra with forces fixed is not a re-solved trajectory. Preload, low force, low tangent stiffness, clearance, separation and delay differ; opposing moments do not automatically stabilize or improve delivery. Preserve both proposed strategy families as nonexhaustive testable hypotheses.

6. **Finite Simulation Evidence:** Bound planar, moving-base/shaft, prescribed-force spatial and shared-contact carriage comparisons separately. Preserve failed matching conditions and anatomical placement limitations. Saved uncertainty summaries comprise8programs,6training and6held-out draws; independent nondominance recomputation gives7/8 candidates, not a global Pareto frontier. Objective directions are max speed q10 and min mean absolute planar face/path angle, force q90, squared-torque effort and speed standard deviation. The effort integral has units(Nm)^2s, despite the saved `effort_proxy_mean_nms` field name; it is not work/metabolic cost. Six-draw tails and engineering ranges are not calibrated human quantiles. The toy two-objective dominating alternative explains finite-set inference limits.

7. **Measurement and Falsification:** Net inverse estimates depend on external forces, inertial assumptions, synchronized frames and differentiated kinematics. Bilateral wrench measurements can distinguish allocations that a net wrench cannot; shaft strain and perturbation impedance require observation models. The EMG methodological review supports excitation-related interpretation, not direct activation/force/work. Synthetic protocol checks do not constitute human observations. A failed anatomical mapping can reject a model application without rejecting mechanics laws. Holdouts must be named as synthetic draws, conditions or participants and kept separate from fitted data.

8. **Publication and Reader Action:** Treat desktop/React guide catalog instructions as documentation and model selection at the pinned revision, not execution evidence for every listed experiment. Do not extrapolate these selected components to every frontend capability. Make claims traceable to versioned sources, preserve adverse results and distinguish technical completion from biological proof. The synthesis diagram is explicitly a conceptual review map; arrows do not assign causal effects or equal evidence strength. Chapter28 source is revised; only make_synthesis changes within the shared generator. Other chapter sources and other generator AST nodes must remain intact.
