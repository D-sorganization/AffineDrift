## Corrected Findings

1. **Signed and vector slip.** Replace always-positive spin acceleration with contact-point slip, signed friction and the vector moment equation. State the fixed-plane, continuous-contact and inertia assumptions; derive and test both zero-spin and overspin transitions. Restrict the five-sevenths result to its level, spinless homogeneous-sphere case.
2. **Rolling inertia and rest.** Restore the gravitational factor 1/(1+alpha), derive an explicitly assumed resisting-couple law, check power balance and distinguish its moving-downhill threshold from static equilibrium. Sliding friction alone does not supply a static rolling-resistance couple. A 5% slope at conventional Stimp 12 is a counterexample to the old acceleration threshold.
3. **Events and capture.** Separate event localization from step adaptation; identify the unsigned-norm zero and two-crossings-within-a-step failure modes. Root tolerance is not physical trajectory accuracy. Remove the stale point-chord bound, reconcile the finite-ball companion and distinguish a capture proxy from rim dynamics or universal conservative classification. Treat rest, reversal and time-limit termination separately.
4. **Surface geometry and numerical resolution.** Qualify the small-slope/local-plane reduction, changing contact frame, curvature and radius offset. Correct automatic bicubic smoothness and interpolation-as-denoising claims. Demonstrate continuous heights with discontinuous slopes; discuss slope-noise amplification and convergence without prescribing a universal grid spacing.
5. **Maps and probability.** Local downhill acceleration is not global reachability. Aim/pace area depends on units and measure. Define the capture indicator integral over a joint distribution and give an exact zero-mean-noise counterexample to the universal probability-ceiling claim. Distinguish sampling error, shared parameters, shot noise and model uncertainty.
6. **Perturbation timing.** Correct the reversed remaining-time argument with a fixed-time double-integrator calculation: equal impulses at 0.5 and 3.9 s produce a 35:1 displacement ratio at 4 s. Qualify transfer to dissipative trajectories, event times and spatial defects. Remove universal noise-exposure and optimum-pace claims.
7. **Directional resistance.** A heading-dependent scalar resistance has no instantaneous transverse component. Supply a synthetic positive-semidefinite tensor example with negative power, explicitly noting its reversal symmetry and inability to model with/against-grain asymmetry alone. Paired distances do not identify every directional effect; no literature-wide absence claim is made.
8. **Implementation authority.** Pin sixteen provider source files at Tools 96ab281ef0af185640e93571dd4bf2b94ff761a5 and explorer af7f2682213f57bb2e95052fed6d5b0740596cbd. The actual loops use fixed 2 ms steps, full slope gravity, between-step mode switches, low-speed termination and sampled-position capture. Record the 0.87 groove factor, bilinear surfaces and out-of-domain policy. Python input dispersion exists; browser report statistics do not independently execute that sampler. Name the code/article coordinate conversion. Selected reference fixtures are reproducibility checks, not physical validation or an all-version guarantee.
9. **Inference and interconnected decisions.** Remove solved-physics, universal wind-negligibility, unsupported real-make-rate fidelity, absent-data and unconditional one-degree-miss claims. Separate source inspection from live-provider qualification. Connect every layer to the next and define the measurements and convergence evidence needed for a real prediction.
