## Scientific Decisions

1. **Coordinate-Compatible Loads and Power.** Declare both link angles absolute and the wrist angle relative. An internal wrist actuator exerts equal-and-opposite couples, so the absolute-coordinate load is (tau_s-tau_w,tau_w), whereas the relative-coordinate load is (tau_s,tau_w). Transform the inertia and bias consistently through theta=Aq. The manufactured rates (2,5) rad/s and actuator torques (3,4) N m give 18 W, not the naive 26 W. Numerical checks independently verify power, kinetic energy and acceleration equivalence; the omitted arm-side wrist contribution is -8 W.
2. **Configuration-Dependent Coupling.** Give the physically parameterized absolute-coordinate inertia and velocity bias, including transported distal mass. Set delta=theta1-theta2=-phi explicitly. At delta=+pi/2, inertia coefficients (2,1,1) and absolute rates (2,0) produce distal acceleration 4 rad/s² without gravity or actuation despite an instantaneous diagonal inertia. A second nonzero-rate fixture verifies cancellation of the inertial work term and the mass-matrix derivative term. Deleting one off-diagonal entry without the associated energy and force structure is not physical decoupling.
3. **Transport, Relative Motion and Energy.** Distinguish absolute club rotation from relative wrist rotation and vector clubhead velocity from an invalid scalar speed sum. Carrying can change distal motion before rapid relative release, but energy gain requires the work balance. Rigid-link mechanical storage is kinetic and gravitational potential energy; shaft strain requires deformation state. Internal exchanges redistribute energy, while boundary work changes its total. A geometric sketch supplies no measured velocities, forces or energy history.
4. **Moving-Hand Reactions and Contact Power.** Both force components applied at the hand have zero moment about that hand, although a transverse force can have a moment about the club COM. For a body-fixed hand point, include m r_G/H cross a_H in the planar angular balance. The manufactured m=2 kg, I_G=0.5 kg m², r=(1,0) m and a_H=(0,1) m/s² state has angular acceleration -0.8 rad/s² and hand force (0,0.4) N without a couple. Its COM moment is -0.4 N m and its power at hand velocity (0,3) m/s is 1.2 W. The test reconciles COM and hand-reference balances and kinetic-energy rate. Club-side couple power uses absolute club rate; whole-system wrist-actuator power uses the relative rate.
5. **Singular Limits and Locking Events.** A vanishing distal inertia does not universally remove reactions: with mass and COM inertia proportional to epsilon, unit offset and finite unit couple, angular acceleration is 1/(2epsilon) and tangential hand force stays 0.5 N. The support reaction couple also persists. Bounded-acceleration families behave differently; the singular endpoint requires a reduced model. Zero length need not remove rotary inertia. Distinguish an already locked wrist from imposing an inelastic lock. For diagonal generalized inertia (2,1), rates (1,0), wrist constraint and no other generalized impulse, projection gives (2/3,2/3) and kinetic energy falls from 1 to 2/3. Smooth conservative energy checks do not forbid physically dissipative events or gravity/preload-driven motion from rest.
6. **Actuator Rank and Intervention Meaning.** Zero command is an input value, not a change in the rank of available actuator directions. Separate actuator command from net torque even for a single pendulum. A copied-state acceleration comparison differs from an integrated branch whose state subsequently diverges. Setting velocities to zero removes all velocity-dependent effects present, including damping when modeled; it does not isolate inertial coupling by definition. Preserve joint modes and internal actuator or elastic state where relevant.
7. **Conditional Timing and Evidence.** Torque timing comparisons depend on work, rise time, initial state and the output event; peak speed anywhere is not impact speed. Sharp's three-link optimization supports a conditional timing result, with net wrist torque distinct from parameterized actuator input and limit-stop reactions. Putnam's kicking/throwing synthesis is not a golf intervention study. Neither fitted kinematics nor manufactured checks identify human muscle strategy. Added relevant physics can reverse an intervention's benefit without violating mechanics; accounting identities survive extensions, while timing and sign claims require retesting.
8. **Figure Geometry and Scope.** The old three panels retained the same relative angle despite a carry/reorient/handoff narrative and cropped distal endpoints. Change the relative angles to -1.6, -0.8 and -0.1 radians, show both complete connected links, and derive common bounds from all endpoints.
