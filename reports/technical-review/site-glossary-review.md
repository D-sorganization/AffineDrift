## Scientific Decisions

- **Plant, Input and Time:** Drift is the complete zero-declared-input field, including retained states and compatible constraints. Control-affinity is pointwise at fixed state and settings; it does not make trajectories additive or identify biological passivity. Input level, internal state and frozen parameters must be declared. The ZTCF family retains pointwise, stitched, forward and branched constructions; only the latter two integrate the dynamics.
- **Capacity and Geometry:** DCR uses bounded control capacity in one acceleration space and metric, with a reported regularizer. It can exceed one and is not a fraction of motion or physiological effort. Local authority, finite-horizon reachability, constraint-compatible velocity and task-preserving velocity are distinct. Underactuation uses the reduced mechanical input-map rank; state dimension need not be 2n. Coordinates may be local or redundant, with explicit constraints.
- **Loads and Inference:** Generalized forces are virtual-work coefficients. Constraint multipliers require the transpose-Jacobian map and declared constraint scaling. Workless total reactions can exchange power between subsystems; moving constraints and friction require separate treatment. Forward dynamics solves the complete declared model. Inverse dynamics and net joint moments do not uniquely identify muscle forces or unmeasured contacts.
- **Energy and Measurement:** Angular momentum includes translation about the chosen origin; inertia depends on reference point and axis. Distinguish force and couple work, kinetic and potential energy, and applied loads already represented in a potential. Smash factor is a speed ratio, with mass-weighted squared speed entering a translational-energy ratio. Clubhead speed requires a declared tracked point. COR uses relative normal contact velocity; impulse and duration yield mean rather than peak force. Compression ratings and COP have protocol/contact assumptions.
- **Anatomy and Behavior:** Wrist coaching labels do not uniquely define anatomical coordinates or a three-dimensional club release. Forearm pronation/supination is separated from wrist motion and global palm direction. Proximal speed-peak order does not identify torque order or energy flow. Feedforward and feedback can coexist; ideomotor and motor-program accounts do not establish one identified neural implementation of a golf swing.
- **Evidence Language:** Governance, provenance, protection and qualification describe declared processes and evidence scope. A hash, review label or named model alone does not establish scientific truth. Aerodynamic definitions use air-relative velocity and conditional force directions; launch angle uses a declared horizontal plane.
