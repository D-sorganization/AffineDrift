## Scientific Decisions

1. **State and Event Definition.** Distinguish the complete predictive state from a scalar event condition, which can depend on many state variables. A threshold in one coordinate does not match full state. Specify the first admissible directed root, detector memory, hysteresis and absent or repeated events. Sign-change detection can miss multiple crossings within a step and tangential roots; ODE residual checks alone do not validate event localization.
2. **Transversality.** Derive the local event-time perturbation as minus the event-function perturbation divided by its approach-side derivative. A positive 0.002 rad error gives -0.5 ms at 4 rad/s or -50 ms at 0.04 rad/s for a linear trace. A curved trace over the longer interval needs explicit root evaluation. The dimensionless grazing example `(s-0.2)^2+epsilon` has no, two, or one double real root as epsilon is positive, negative or zero; the double root is at s=0.2. A confidence interval conditional on one root cannot represent all event outcomes.
3. **Actuator History.** Use an explicitly illustrative first-order torque response, not a universal muscle law. A 35 ms time constant gives 63.2% response after one constant and 76.9 ms of 10–90% rise. A -5 to +15 N m target change crosses zero after 10.1 ms with that time constant. Distinguish pure delay, lag, rate limits, preload and transmitted load. A finite torque step does not instantaneously change velocity. Complete-state branching and finite preparation answer different causal questions; equal command is not equal impulse or mechanical work.
4. **Provider and Literature Scope.** The pinned filter audit attempts 92 programs per filter, 368 rows across four filter constants and two shoulder commands (60/100 N m). Lead reconstruction reproduces eight ordering summaries and acceptance counts 63/59/59/56. Initial command is copied directly into the filter state; only subsequent changes are smoothed. The first upward club-angle-zero event, with arm angle at most 2 rad, is a geometric proxy without ball collision. Selected onset may change across filters; finite-grid reselection is not continuous optimization or fixed-policy robustness. The separate moving-base/two-hand/flexible comparison has eight schedules, six held-out draws and endpoint 0.24 s. Nominal early_restrain (-2 N m before 0.10 s, +4 after) and late_drive (zero before 0.14 s, +5 after) change early torque, switch time and late amplitude together. All eight candidates are nondominated across five objectives; neither this comparison nor the filter audit tests a general feedback advantage. Sprigings2002 publisher abstract supports a bounded three-condition planar simulation, not measured human command histories or this audit's filter parameters.
5. **Alignment and Work.** Distinguish translation from duration normalization. The angular derivative with respect to normalized phase is D times angular velocity and has units rad per unit phase. Preserve the time Jacobian in work integration. Identical phase power traces `10+5*phase` W over durations 0.2/0.4 s give 2.5/5 J. Nonlinear warps need their local Jacobian; averaging phase traces before integration need not recover mean trial work. Sharpness at an alignment event is partly constructed, not causal evidence.
6. **Prospective Information.** Actual future impact and actual completed duration are retrospective quantities. A real-time policy needs available measurements or predictions with evaluated error. Adding a clock to the mathematical state adds no information about unobserved deviations. Compare sensors, estimator memory, latency, actuator bounds and preparation explicitly; an internal simulation diagnostic is not automatically an experimentally available control signal.
7. **Causal Design and Feasible Regions.** A sign crossing need not begin an interaction. Define body, moment point, projection and power-conjugate rates. Distinguish measured ordering from causation, copied-state intervention from preparation, and fixed-policy effects from reoptimization. Each branch evolves its own reactions. Preserve the loading-history and arm/wrist allocation hypotheses without equating roles with net sign. A broad plateau, narrow peak, boundary optimum, disconnected regions or no feasible benefit are possible; report failures and the actual response rather than asserting a robust plateau.
8. **Figure Contract.** Three artificial logistic curves have different offsets and slopes; exact threshold markers share scalar value 0.6, not complete state. Labels declare schematic curves and arbitrary units. Only `make_clock_state` changes in the generator; all other top-level AST nodes match baseline. Manufactured checks test algebra and figure geometry. No new provider dynamics rollout or human experiment was performed.
