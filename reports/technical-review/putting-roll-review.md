## Findings and Corrections

1. **P1 — Slope inertia and thresholds.** Derive rolling acceleration from
   separate Newton and Euler balances with an explicitly calibrated resisting
   couple. The gravity contribution carries 1/(1+alpha). Distinguish a0/g from
   the displaced-normal lever coefficient; state normal-load scaling and
   traction feasibility. Moving thresholds become 9.80/7.84/6.53 percent for
   nominal stimp8/10/12. The stimp12 5-percent example decelerates. These are
   neither measured initiation thresholds nor course-design standards.
2. **P1 — Break approximation.** Lateral resistance is first order in lateral
   velocity. Solve its linearized ODE including this term; provide a modest
   finite-speed example and explain nonuniformity near rest. Remove the
   unsupported dying-putt upper-bound and large-deflection aiming claims.
3. **P1 — Ground-entry spin and skid.** Derive signed-slip transition time,
   speed, displacement and heat for arbitrary initial spin in a straight
   level-contact model. Cover over-spin and distinguish skid ratio from total
   share. Demonstrate topspin from tangential face impulse; remove unsupported
   launch-angle/loft equality and arm-mass coupling claims. Explain how launch,
   hopping and landing establish the state that the ground model actually uses.
4. **P1 — Instrument geometry and identification.** Derive the V-groove lever
   from its published 145-degree opening, giving rc/R=0.9537. The approximate
   ideal ramp predicts1.885m/s, not an exact1.83. Distinguish geometry from
   release/transition calibration, and state the nominal flat-rolling convention.
   An explicit alternate a(v) gives the same stimp but a different shorter
   rollout, proving that a single distance cannot identify an entire loss law.
5. **P1 — Capture mechanisms.** Replace full-diameter point-chord geometry with
   finite-ball free-fall construction (D-R) and its domain-limited off-centre
   form. Attribute Penner's separate historical capture fit explicitly; remove
   universal1.64m/s claims and recompute the fitted widths. Rim, spin, slope and
   flagstick effects require their own qualification; neither formula is a
   universal probability or speed bound.
6. **P1 — Regime consistency.** Use vector spin and contact slip in planar
   sliding; add traction checks, rest-law choices, normal-spin limitations and
   fixed-plane scope. Remove unverified claims of conformance by the Tools
   putting module. The independent check suite is not a software qualification.
7. **P2 — Reader interpretation.** Rewrite the accessible overview and preserve
   the chain linking delivery, time on slope, arrival state and capture. Reuse
   the existing force-measurement scoped stylesheet for readable math and a
   fully expandable overview without altering its frozen predecessor evidence.
