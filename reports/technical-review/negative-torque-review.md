## Scientific Decisions

1. **Power Conjugacy.** Separate a moment on the club from its action/reaction joint pair. Club-side power uses absolute club angular velocity; rotational joint power uses club-minus-arm rate. Manufactured torque -2 N m with rates 3 and 5 rad/s gives -6 W club, +10 W arm, +4 W pair. Relative coordinates `(theta_arm, beta)` and absolute `(theta_arm, theta_club)` preserve total generalized power. Three-dimensional dot products and coordinate-rate mappings replace one projected-component inference.
2. **Energy Boundary.** Rigid-club kinetic-energy change equals physical hand-wrench power plus gravitational power and power from any other declared external loads in one inertial observer. A boundary enclosing a flexible shaft includes its strain storage and dissipation; shaft release and inertial coupling must not be counted as extra external supplies. Spring/damper examples distinguish recoverable storage from dissipation. Instantaneous negative power does not imply negative cumulative work or energy loss for the full system.
3. **Moment Transport.** With H-to-C displacement `(0, 1, 0)`, absolute omega `(0, 0, 3)`, vH `(2, 0, 0)`, F `(10, 0, 0)` and MH `(0, 0, -2)` in SI, vC = (-1, 0, 0), MC = (0, 0, 8). Both wrench reductions give 14 W although the moment component changes sign. Reference-point transport is different from changing observer. A seeded 32-case vector identity verifies transport without claiming human validation.
4. **Reaction Geometry.** Preserve the provider convention d = trail-minus-lead and Fd = (lead-minus-trail)/2; midpoint force moment = -d cross Fd. Fixed-force reversal/collapse are algebraic controls, not predictions of re-solved reactions. Free moments survive collapse. Co-rotation includes the measuring normal. Ideal two-sided constraint power can vanish while one body receives nonzero contact power.
5. **Three Study Scopes.** Separate archived pointwise zero-command reactions, a distinct finite forward constrained branch, and the finite eight-schedule uncertain-control comparison. Reintegrated saved contact power is -149.417052 J over 0.088121744 s. About 85% is negative, not a positive-power boost. The forward representative branch copies state exactly and all saved couple values stay negative for 50 ms, minimum -7.43245166 Nm. The later 37.25 ms persistence is read from provider JSON/text, not independently rerun. Eight candidates are nondominated; early_restrain q10 = 4.705878716 m/s, mean face/path 5.439869 deg versus late_drive q10 = 4.417288309 m/s, mean 1.576101 deg. Five-objective arithmetic matches archived arrays at relative and absolute tolerances of 1e-14. Endpoint 0.24 s, no ball collision, no population bound or continuous optimum.
6. **Empirical Attribution.** Remove unsupported wrist-EMG attribution to Morrison2016. Its primary abstract concerns UCM kinematics. Read Nesbit2005 abstract and methods, including kinematically prescribed wrists and simplified hands; describe inferred kinetics without claiming individual-muscle measurement. No full-paper methodological endorsement. Sprigings2000 publisher access failed, so specific claims attributed to it were not added; its old trailing citation is removed from this chapter, bibliography retained unchanged.
7. **Causal Design.** Declare complete copied state, finite torque versus command removal, controller/actuator memory, fixed constraints and event definitions. Reactions evolve on each branch. Contact-mode changes can require state compatibility or reset; pointwise splits are not independently evolved trajectory partitions. Equal torque is not equal work when velocity differs. Reoptimization asks substitution, not the same fixed-policy effect. Remove unsupported necessary interior optimum. Preserve the arms/wrists role-reversal and loading-history hypothesis with measurable boundaries.
8. **Figure Contract.** Retain mathematically correct quadrants while requiring conjugate rate and distinguishing absolute club versus relative joint rate. Only make_sign_quadrants changes in the shared generator. Initial default-style generation revealed footer overlap; corrected footer offset and regenerated with the generator's standard \_style().
