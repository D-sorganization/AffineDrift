## What the Evidence Can Establish

The appendix was an annotated argument, not just a collection of links. It placed patent embodiments, manufacturer claims, regulatory records and independent comparisons under headings that could imply equal evidential strength. The revision explains their distinct roles and connects the result to practical interpretation: a measurement is meaningful only with its measurand, reference point, event time, uncertainty, conditions and return rate.

1. **Centered Agreement Is Not Zero-Error Accuracy.** Leach et al. (2017), Table 6, uses bands centered on the median difference. The 45% path and 66% face-angle figures therefore cannot be read as the fraction meeting a one-degree absolute-error specification. A synthetic counterexample with errors 2.5, 3 and 3.5 degrees puts all three within one degree of their median and none within one degree of zero. Bias and dispersion need separate reporting.
2. **Missing Outputs Change the Interpretation.** The same study returned TrackMan club velocity on 98% of trials; the remaining four club parameters returned on 62% overall and 19% for the utility wedge. The previous prose made 62% sound applicable to every clubhead output. Identify the Pro IIIe, eight golfers, 240 trials, indoor setup, installation compromises and differences in reference definitions. Calibration of a reference system does not make all compared outputs the same measurand or validate newer models.
3. **Historical Specifications Are Conditional.** Newsletter 9 separates absolute accuracy from repeatability, specifies geometric/contact and successful-output conditions, and qualifies its maximum-compression reference with temporal averaging. Its confidence terminology is the vendor's historical convention, not independent coverage validation. The 2017 definition article distinguishes preimpact processing from averaging face orientation during contact; no undocumented algorithm is inferred.
4. **Availability Claims Need a Date and Scope.** Remove unsupported claims that every official copy returns 404, archives contain only redirects, the mirror is complete, the series ended, or these are the only published specifications. Two newsletter retrievals initially returned 503 and later succeeded. Preserve historical indexing, distinguish an access observation from permanent absence, and identify the three issues actually retrieved.
5. **Curvature Controls Local Face Orientation.** Newsletter 8 really does present its half-inch/two-degree example with universal-driver wording. That is an accurately identified vendor assertion, not a general geometric law. For a circular cross-section and lateral displacement x, the change in normal is asin(x/R). The same 12.7 mm displacement gives about 2.91 degrees at R=250 mm and 1.46 degrees at R=500 mm. These are ideal geometric examples, not equipment measurements.
6. **Launch Weights Belong to a Study.** Wood, Henrikson and Broadie (2018) report means after both central-strike and face-to-path filters. The retained horizontal samples are 89 driver, 172 iron and 28 wedge shots; the 1,575 shots describe the original pool. Their means are not universal coefficients or individual-shot tolerance bands. Replace the incorrect TrackMan attribution, the claim of canonical weights, and the categorical vertical-pass/horizontal-fail comparison with the actual study scope. Retain protocol and measurement differences.
7. **Correct the Inverse Sensitivity.** With path and weight fixed, inferred face angle has launch-direction derivative 1/w. The factors for 0.76 and 0.85 are 1.315789 and 1.176471: an 11.8421% increase, not roughly double. The second copy in the design-implication callout is corrected as well. This derivative is not a full error budget; path error and uncertainty in the weight contribute separately.
8. **Patent Disclosure Does Not Verify a Product.** Move US6186002 to its original USGA assignee grouping. Narrow several annotations to what the inspected abstracts disclose. Remove unverified expiry/clearance language and the markerless implementation-blueprint implication. Retain the distinction between an embodiment, shipped architecture, independent performance and legal claim scope. No freedom-to-operate opinion or patent-status audit is made.
9. **Repair Concrete References.** Replace two observed 404 paths with verified SkyTrak and Uneekor product descriptions; label the Canadian Uneekor page. Point the Full Swing application at its inspected patent record. Label the TrackMan historical explainer's generic-blog redirect. Add Wood's authors, journal, volume, issue, article number and DOI under the existing bibliography key. Keep all 83 entries.
10. **Make the Reference Pages Readable.** Long URLs overflowed the original PDF margins. Local appendix URL-break opportunities and ragged alignment preserve clickable destinations without changing typography rules in the other chapters.
