## Scientific Decisions

1. **Model Families and Figure Meaning.** The earlier caption described models while its actual graphic ranked equation identity through replicated outcomes. Replace that mismatch with six model families connected through an explicit comparison contract. A flexible shaft, moving base, spatial contact and biological allocation are branches with capability memberships, not guaranteed nested additions or a monotonic confidence ranking. Mark the family descriptions as modeling questions rather than claims of execution.
2. **Available Outputs and Compatible Power.** Define each model's output map on full state, input and parameters, including units, frame, reference, port, event and error scope. Unsupported quantities remain unavailable, not measured zeros. A resultant wrench paired with one rigid twist only captures compatible rigid motion. Two outward 10 N endpoint forces with outward 0.2 m/s endpoint velocities give 4 W external input despite zero resultant force and moment. The example illustrates deformation power omitted by rigid reduction; it does not claim that all input instantly becomes stored strain energy.
3. **Full State and Intervention Identity.** Equal reduced motion can hide elastic or activation state. In a two-mass spring fixture with displacements from equilibrium, k=100 N/m and observed mass1=1 kg, z=q2-q1=±0.01 m gives a1=±1 m/s² while q1=v1=0 initially. The unobserved mass2=2 kg has opposite momentum-rate contribution, preserving total linear momentum. Keep prescribed inverse comparisons, dynamic forward evolution, instantaneous command deletion and altered input histories distinct; register lifts and initial energy.
4. **Complexity and Identifiability.** Added state does not universally increase all uncertainty or destroy identifiability. Separate approximation bias, numerical error, parameter uncertainty and ambiguous internal variables. Data, known parameters and justified constitutive restrictions determine whether additional structure is informative. Training fit and anatomical appearance alone do not establish predictive improvement.
5. **The Independent Part of an Implementation.** Pin the reviewed upstream source. Its common-state MuJoCo/Lagrange--Christoffel comparison is distinct from its reduced forward MuJoCo/Pinocchio comparison. The latter uses native inertia-and-bias operators but shared projected compliance and a shared semi-implicit state update. This is bounded operator transport, not independent native contact-solver or integrator parity. Compare physical reactions with matched coordinates and boundary laws; redundant multipliers need not match. Package capabilities are not executed project evidence.
6. **Failure Status and Capability Scope.** Failed closure prevents numerical qualification; a resolved falsifier may contradict a registered prediction; insufficient information is inconclusive; unrun comparisons are untested. Changed assumptions can reverse a conditional result without refuting mechanics. The pinned finite-base study reports 576 numerical passes but zero eligible total-work-matched comparison cells, which supplies no delivery-benefit estimate. Articulated short-horizon successes do not imply unrestricted anatomical transport; completed documentation does not imply completed experiments.
7. **Ensemble Fractions and Statistical Inference.** A chosen 95% sign rule is conditional on scenario design and weights. Identical outcomes produce 75% with equal weights and 30% with weights (0.1,0.1,0.1,0.7). For 95/100 independent Bernoulli successes, SciPy's independent Wilson calculation gives [0.888249530768,0.978456320846] at approximate 95% confidence. It is not an exact-coverage guarantee, a population lower bound of 0.95, or a justified interval for an arbitrary correlated grid.
8. **Measurements and Published Golf Evidence.** Instrument selection follows the estimand and alternatives; EMG is not direct muscle force. The absence of a qualifying governed deposit in the pinned project is not global absence of golf measurements. McNally/McPhee 2018 provides a six-DOF carry-distance optimization example with constrained timing, candidate center-face impacts, a torso-before-pelvis solution and grip-kinematic discrepancies. Those limits illustrate conditional model evaluation; neither visual resemblance nor the optimized torque generators identify human muscle control.
