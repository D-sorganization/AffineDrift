## Scientific Corrections

The chapter connects swing delivery to local contact state, coupled impulse, outgoing ball motion and instrument inference. A technique change can affect face pose, contact-point velocity and strike geometry together; compatible joint predictions are stronger evidence than independently fitted scalar rules.

1. **Contact Geometry and Reference Points.** Declare a right-handed forward/up/right frame and distinguish contact-point incident velocity from reported club speed at another point or time. State the symmetric, initially stationary and nonspinning ball assumptions behind the D-plane. Correct the illustrated weighted launch from −2.6 to −2.96 degrees and preserve the earlier study-specific horizontal weights and inverse sensitivity.
2. **Obliquity and Historical Launch Maps.** Replace the incomplete trigonometric identity with the full dot product retaining both elevations. Keep Tutelman's historical coordinates, units and approximation distinct from exact target-frame geometry; its later comparison is not evidence that the earlier model was fitted to that comparison.
3. **Spin Direction and Tilt.** Derive spin from the tangential impulse moment and define signed tilt in an outgoing-flight frame. State parallel-vector, vertical-launch, zero-spin and purely axial-spin degeneracies. Total spin and one tilt do not describe every three-dimensional spin vector.
4. **Normal Impulse and Smash Factor.** Include rotational head effective mass and the restriction to uncoupled normal restitution. Separate normal ball velocity from speed norm and local incident speed from a device's reported denominator. The illustrative table is not a conformance test or universal rejection ceiling.
5. **Joint Launch and Spin.** Use the same tangential impulse for translation and rotation. Bound the empirical spin rule by its mph/rpm units and model conventions. Remove unsupported universal loft-to-spin, saturation and contamination rules; retain the former vendor citation as historical background.
6. **Gear Scaling and Coupled Recoil.** Remove the hundredfold scaling error: 150 mph and 0.25 inch give 615 rpm under the cited heuristic. Its historical 2.5% ratio spread is not prediction accuracy. Derive a two-by-two contact mobility that includes ball translation/spin and head translation/rotation; final sticking is a friction-constrained candidate, not a complete compliant collision solution.
7. **Inclined-Plane Geometry.** Derive the conditional attack/path relationship, its branch, sign and domain. A −5-degree attack in the declared 60-degree plane gives +2.8953 degrees of path; it does not prove any product algorithm or a fixed-plane human swing.
8. **Evidence and Inference.** Remove universal architecture claims and avoid validating an inferred face angle with its own launch input. Distinguish measured data, manufactured examples, author approximations and sensor uncertainty. Correct the friction paper's four-author attribution and journal.
