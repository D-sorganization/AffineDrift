\chapter{Glossary of Important Concepts}

\begin{description}[leftmargin=!,labelwidth=\widthof{\bfseries Configuration Space}]
\item[Articulated Body Algorithm] A recursive $O(N)$ algorithm for computing the forward dynamics of a kinematic chain, widely used in physics engines.
\item[Configuration Space] The space (often a manifold) of all possible positions of a system.
\item[Contraction Analysis] A framework for analyzing the stability of trajectories with respect to each other, rather than with respect to an equilibrium point.
\item[Control Contraction Metric (CCM)] A Riemannian metric that can be used to synthesize stabilizing feedback controllers for nonlinear systems along arbitrary feasible trajectories.
\item[Degrees of Freedom (DOF)] The minimum number of independent coordinates required to completely specify the configuration of a system.
\item[Exponential Map] The operation that relates a Lie Algebra (representing velocity) to its corresponding Lie Group (representing position/orientation).
\item[Euler Angles] A 3-parameter representation of orientation that is subject to gimbal lock.
\item[Flow] The solution map of a differential equation smoothly moving points along vector fields in state space.
\item[Gimbal Lock] A coordinate singularity where a 3D rotation parameterization loses a degree of freedom.
\item[Jacobian] The matrix of partial derivatives relating joint velocities to task-space velocities.
\item[Kinematics] The study of motion geometry without considering the forces that cause it.
\item[Lagrangian Mechanics] An energy-based formulation of mechanics utilizing kinetic and potential energy to strictly determine the equations of motion.
\item[Lie Algebra] The mathematical space of velocities associated with a Lie Group, corresponding to its tangent space at the identity.
\item[Lie Group] A smooth manifold that is also a mathematical group, typically describing continuous symmetries or rigid body transformations (e.g., $SO(3)$, $SE(3)$).
\item[Lyapunov Stability] The classical definition of stability describing whether a system returns to an equilibrium point when perturbed.
\item[Manifold] A topological space that locally resembles Euclidean space, crucial for rigorous formulation of non-trivial state spaces.
\item[State Space] The space of all possible states of a dynamical system, usually comprising both configuration and momentum/velocity.
\item[Screw Axis] A geometric representation of spatial motion combining a rotation axis and a translation along that axis, foundational for modern rigid body mathematics.
\item[Tangent Space] The vector space of all possible velocities at a specific point on a manifold.
\item[Tangent Bundle] The manifold consisting of all tangent spaces glued together, typically representing the full state space of mechanical systems.
\item[Twist] The spatial velocity of a rigid body, composed of angular and linear velocity components, residing in the Lie Algebra.
\item[Wrench] The spatial force acting on a rigid body, combining torque and linear force.
\end{description}
