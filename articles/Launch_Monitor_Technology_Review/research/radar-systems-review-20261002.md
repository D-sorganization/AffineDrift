## Argument and Scientific Decisions

The chapter connects observations to geometry, identifiability, estimation and validation. The golf-swing consequence is that reported parameters may share measurements and model assumptions. Their correlation cannot automatically validate a biomechanical explanation. Independent reference measurements and the estimator's covariance structure matter before interpreting differences between swings or instruments.

1. **Doppler and Resolution.** Use two-way monostatic range rate with an explicit mixing-sign convention. Exact SI conversion gives 71.9487079291 Hz per mph at 24.125 GHz and 31.3144635547 at 10.5 GHz. Fourier-bin spacing, estimation error, bandwidth-based range resolution, angular geometry and detection range are different quantities. Remove unverified product-band assignments and the universal 24 GHz thirty-yard limit.
2. **Phase Geometry.** Retain every physically admissible integer phase branch. At one wavelength spacing, direction cosines 0.2 and -0.8 alias. Half-wavelength endpoints coincide even though interior aliases are excluded. Restricted fields of view and multiple baselines can resolve particular ambiguities; antenna count alone cannot. Define broadside angle, far-field approximation, calibration and channel association.
3. **Hardware Envelope.** K-LD7 raw export is useful evidence of an interface, not launch-monitor performance. Distinguish stock speed/frame settings, configurable range and target detection. Full Swing's patent discloses alternative waveform and antenna arrangements; its line-swing example and proposed range are not independent golf-shot tests.
4. **Rotating Returns.** Derive feature velocity from center translation plus angular velocity crossed with feature offset, projected onto the line of sight. Rotational velocity is invisible in that projection when spin is parallel to the view. Reflectivity, visibility and coherent scattering also shape the return. Missing harmonic orders defeat a universal adjacent-peak spin rule: 200/400 Hz offsets admit 100 Hz and 200 Hz fundamentals under different order assignments.
5. **Trajectory Axis.** Separate gravity, drag and lift as accelerations, using air-relative velocity. Projection does not ensure physically opposing drag. A constant-axis homogeneous fit needs unit normalization: rank two leaves an axis line, rank one a continuum, and noisy data need weighted fitting and singular-value separation. A signed, adequate Magnus model can add orientation information. Instantaneous insensitivity to axial spin does not prove unobservability across every changing trajectory.
6. **Delay Disclosure.** US10775492B2's printed delay relationships are dimensionless if its stated lengths are used, and its subsequent angle ratio reverses baseline factors. Conditional elimination of those printed equations gives `atan2(T_H/S_H, T_V/S_V)`. With baselines 0.1/0.2, distance 3 and angle 30 degrees, the printed ratio gives 66.5868 degrees. This checks the displayed algebra; it neither supplies a validated delay estimator nor interprets legal claim scope.
7. **Operating Modes.** Separate observed flight, extrapolation and normalization. TM4's 4.7 m is device-to-net distance. Garmin R10 standard-ball guidance must be qualified by its RCT mode and fallback behavior. Rapsodo MLM2PRO uses optically printed RPT; RCT and RPT are different aids. X3C setup statements are generation-specific.
8. **Club Inference.** Declare tracked point, coordinate frame, event time and local face orientation. Radar returns need not come from a fixed or prescribed club point. The local face-angle inverse has sensitivities to launch, path and weighting, including their correlations. A shared yaw-reference shift cancels in face-to-path in the stated approximation. This is neither a universal collision law nor evidence that all radar products use that inverse.
9. **Validation and Fusion.** Pickup rate measures availability, not accuracy. The Leach study concerns its tested systems, clubs and protocol. Additional sensors should address an explicit ambiguity; camera presence or a patent does not prove a deployed fused estimator or independently establish its accuracy.
