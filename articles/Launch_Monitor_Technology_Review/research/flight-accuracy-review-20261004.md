## Technical Plan

Read and correct the complete connected argument: launch-state uncertainty, flight-model discrepancy and observation geometry all contribute to displayed carry and club data. A plausible filter result does not identify spin, nor does agreement among model-dependent outputs validate their shared assumptions.

Flight: preserve eq:eom and other public labels, but define the coefficient convention and singular limits. For a force-magnitude coefficient, lift direction is normalized omega cross air-relative velocity; its dependence on spin inclination must be stated. Retaining an unnormalized unit-vector cross product is a different coefficient convention, not inherently a sign error. In this book x is forward, y upward, z right; positive z backspin crossed with forward x gives upward y. The helper assumed another frame and incorrectly assigned backspin direction. At zero cross product a symmetric lift model needs a limiting definition. Drag must oppose air-relative velocity; its ground-frame power can have either sign in wind. Avoid forcing all empirical fits to a zero-spin continuation they did not establish.

Spin ratio is a derived dimensionless quantity R\*norm(omega)/airspeed. Its fractional derivative is fractional spin change minus fractional airspeed change; a constant spin-decay law does not imply the same decay for spin ratio. A three-component spin vector avoids silently assuming one angle represents a general axis. General model coefficients require a declared ball, calibration domain, atmospheric state and inclination; the asserted Smits sine formula and universal constants have not been verified. Do not relabel invented coefficients as literature. The already-reviewed impact-mechanics-and-ball-flight.qmd contains a sourced, domain-bounded Naruo example and distinguishes signed lift, zero limits and spin dynamics; recheck primary sources before reusing it.

For a landing event g(p,t)=0 and parameter sensitivity Y=partial p/partial a at fixed t, event-time sensitivity follows dt/da=-(g_p Y+g_a)/(g_p v+g_t). Evaluate output sensitivity including the changed landing time; a fixed-time covariance is not automatically carry covariance. Near tangency the denominator makes the event ill-conditioned. Numerical order alone is insufficient: test step/tolerance convergence and event interpolation separately from coefficient validity. Reference height and ground surface must be explicit.

Accuracy: separate repeatability, consistency ICC, absolute-agreement ICC, bias, spread, reference uncertainty and return rate. A study of repeated human swings does not isolate instrument noise. A small median difference can coexist with wide differences or missing measurements. An estimated-spin exclusion changes the population to which reported agreement applies. Avoid universal technology rankings and per-club corrections that merely force agreement. RPT optical markings and RCT construction are distinct.

Design: replace universal performance, cheapest/best-upgrade, commercial-algorithm and market-cost assertions with observable inputs and acceptance conditions. A radar spectral percentile is not automatically speed at a defined head point. The proposed single-radar twist inverse has geometric ambiguities already explained in Appendix E. Adding a sensor or filter state does not remove them by itself. Cumulative tiers are design proposals, not delivered products. Preserve accepted patent-boundary corrections and public labels.
