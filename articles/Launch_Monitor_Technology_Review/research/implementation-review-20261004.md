## Reasoning and Corrections

The central distinction is between a sensor observation, an identifiable physical quantity and a model-dependent prediction. A plausible output or a small filter covariance is not an independent validation. Reference point, event time, sensor geometry and uncertainty connect every stage.

1. Doppler arithmetic uses the declared 24.125 GHz carrier and exact speed of light: 71.9487 Hz/mph, ideal complex-sampling half-span 208.482 mph, 128-point bin 234.375 Hz or 3.25753 mph. A 200 mph carrier already occupies 14.3897 kHz of the 15 kHz half-band. No analog/firmware/detection guarantee follows. A 20 ms grid versus 40 ms grid illustrates observation length, not a universal resolution threshold. Coherent phase integration removes a time-varying modeled carrier; per-frame recentering alone does not remove intraframe chirp. Missing harmonic orders and correlated overlapping frames invalidate blanket robustness/three-independent-frame claims.
2. A trimmed Doppler median is an explicitly defined statistic, not an identified geometric-center speed. Rigid-body point velocity, line-of-sight projection, scattering and contact reference point all matter. A smash-factor discrepancy is not a universal toe-lock signature or physical impossibility; the already-reviewed impact chapter states the conditional normal-component model.
3. The K-LD7 is bounded by its actual FSK interface, speed setting and cadence. A generic sensor measurement vector is not a claim that every component is available. The appendix supplies fixed-sensor transforms and range/radial-speed/angle functions with their coordinate singularities. The radial-speed Jacobian is obtained by differentiating u=(p-s)/norm(p-s): du/dp=(I-uu^T)/rho. One radial observation has velocity sensitivity u^T and a two-dimensional instantaneous kernel, even with position known. Temporal dynamics can add information but do not guarantee conditioning or spin/wind/coefficient identifiability.
4. EKF/extended RTS are approximate nonlinear estimators. Missing spin effects remain model uncertainty. Innovation gates require calibrated assumptions and cannot classify returns infallibly. Acoustic propagation, electronic delay, source-event/face-separation offset and clock calibration remain distinct. Back-extrapolating a post-impact trajectory does not permit propagating free flight through impact.
5. Inverting b=wF+(1-w)P gives F=P+(b-P)/w, with explicit sensitivities and model discrepancy. Club-category weights and gear-effect coefficients are not universal inverse laws. Priors must remain visible and cannot validate their own outputs.
6. For constant-depth transverse geometry, image separation is v*dt, blur is v*tau, and the ideal footprint of N pulse centers is (N-1)*v*dt+D. The original 300–500 microsecond spacing produces overlapping ball silhouettes. A finite rectangular light pulse adds v\*tau to the footprint and clearance diameter. Global shutter does not eliminate integration blur; IR illumination requires a verified optical configuration. Texture ambiguity and optimization convergence do not follow just from a 7.2-degree increment.
7. A literature aerodynamic model is a hypothesis with a stated domain. Carry alone cannot identify a full aerodynamic law. Calibration, replay and held-out validation are distinct; paired devices establish agreement subject to reference uncertainty, not absolute truth. RPT optical markings and RCT radar-ball construction are not interchangeable for MLM2PRO spin measurement.
