## Reasoning

The central correction is the separation of acquired information from a finer numerical representation. Padding a short radar record changes the frequency grid, not its duration. A speed peak is a projection from an identified scatterer at a particular time, not automatically a ball or club-center speed. The same observation time controls the temporal averaging that matters near impact; it also constrains spectral separation and coherent Doppler estimation. Hardware timing, geometry and association therefore must be specified together before feeding an impact or flight model.

Independent checks are recorded in `reports/technical-review/hardware-appendix-independent-checks.json`. Lead-authored rational arithmetic and symbolic integration/differentiation verify the sampling scales, acoustic budget, optical displacement, CA-CFAR null-model identity and cosine sensitivity. A manufactured DFT identity verifies shared spectral nodes after zero-padding. No numerical result is presented as a measured product accuracy.

The new CA-CFAR derivation declares an independent exponential test cell and training cells. Its threshold factor follows from the product of their Laplace transforms. Correlated samples, target leakage or nonuniform clutter need different calibration; source text does not assert that an advertised false-alarm target survives those violations.
