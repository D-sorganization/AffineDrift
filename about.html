About

Thanks for taking the time to visit my site. This is a collection of my thoughts on the biomechanics of the golf swing and random other topics organized in a place I can manage and update.

My name is Dieter Olson and I am a chemical engineer by trade and by day I manage an R & D facility that specializes in plasma arc gasification and thermal treatment / remediation of various waste materials. We produce hydrogen rich gas from waste materials using extreme heat and electric arcs into a pool of molten glass. Not much like golf.

I have always been very much interested in golf instruction and fascinated by the complexities of the golf swing. I am an avid collector of golf related instructional materials and have a library that takes up more of the house than the wife would like. Through my interest in golf instruction I have become more engaged in the science behind the golf swing and the application of engineering principles to see what we can figure out. Most of all I like a good puzzle and the golf swing has many great ones.

My research interests are robotics control, modelling, parallel mechanisms, and above all the interaction of the past with the present, which is the namesake of this website. How much can we separate and parse out the effects of the past from what we see in the present? This website is a collection of my thought on the topic.

Feel free to shoot me an email if you want to get in touch. I am always looking for a good riddle to think about. Feedback is appreciated and I will do my best to respond and address any comments. The best way to get in touch is via email at dieterolson@AffineDrift.com
