Golf Biomechanics Tools

Wrist Universal Joint Model

Interactive application modeling the wrist as a universal joint with proper kinematics. Explore how grip angle and wrist deviation affect torque transmission to the club's shaft axis and high-inertia axis, with real-time visualization and calculations.

Wrist Universal Joint Model (PyQt6)

Enhanced PyQt6 desktop application with advanced GUI features. Download and run locally for full interactive control over grip angle, wrist angle, club properties, and signal generation. Includes comprehensive visualization of torque transmission, angular acceleration, and universal joint mechanics.

Grip Angle Torque Transmission Calculator

Compare how different grip angles modulate transmission of forearm axis noise to the club's shaft axis and high inertia axis. Visualize torque distribution with customizable input signals.
