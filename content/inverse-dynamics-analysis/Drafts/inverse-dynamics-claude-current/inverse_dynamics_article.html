What the Revision Clarifies

Standard inverse dynamics already accounts for the declared gravity and inertial coupling. A reported moment is real but does not identify a unique hand strategy. Changing the reporting point, changing the actual wrench, and redistributing contacts while preserving that wrench are distinct experiments. Mechanical power and radial contact loading also require separate interpretation from perceived or metabolic effort.

The original approximately forty-percent aerodynamic moment reduction is possible for its assumed geometry and force direction. It remains a hypothetical calculation, not a measured seven-iron correction.
