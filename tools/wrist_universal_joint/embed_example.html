Grip Angle Torque Transmission Simulator

This interactive tool visualizes how grip angle modulates transmission of forearm axis torque noise to the club's shaft axis (local alpha) and high inertia axis (local gamma), and calculates the resulting angular acceleration based on club inertial properties.

Features

  • Adjustable club properties (clubhead weight, shaft weight, length, CG distance)
  • Real-time moment of inertia calculations
  • Grip angle slider (0° to 90°)
  • Multiple noise input types (golf-like random, burst, step, sinusoidal)
  • Dual plots: transmitted torque and angular acceleration
  • Side-by-side comparison of different grip angles

Physics

The tool calculates angular acceleration using Newton's second law for rotation: $\alpha = \tau / I$, where $\tau$ is the transmitted torque and $I$ is the moment of inertia. Higher inertia means lower acceleration for the same torque input.
