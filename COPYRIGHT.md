# Copyright and Reuse

AffineDrift contains three kinds of material with different terms: software,
written text and data, and figures. This page states which is which, because
[LICENSE](LICENSE) alone does not distinguish them.

## Software: MIT

The source code in this repository is released under the MIT License in
[LICENSE](LICENSE). This covers:

- Python modules under `src/` and the test suite under `tests/`
- JavaScript modules under `js/`
- Stylesheets, Quarto configuration, templates, and includes
- Build scripts, container definitions, and GitHub Actions workflows

You may use, modify, and redistribute this code, including commercially, subject
to the attribution requirement in the MIT terms.

## Written Content and Data: CC BY 4.0

The written text and the authored data are Copyright (c) 2026 D-Sorganization,
licensed under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International License. The
full notice is in [LICENSE-CONTENT](LICENSE-CONTENT). This covers:

- Articles and textbook volumes under `articles/` and `books/`
- Critique responses under `critiques/`
- Standalone pages under `pages/`, and reference pages under `resources/`
- Authored datasets under `data/`, excluding third-party data
- The rendered equivalents of all of the above at
  [affinedrift.com](https://affinedrift.com)

You may share and adapt this material, including commercially, provided you
credit the author, link to the licence, and say if you changed it.

## Figures and Illustrations: Reserved

Figures, diagrams, and illustrations authored for those pages are Copyright
(c) 2026 D-Sorganization and are not covered by CC BY 4.0. Ask before reusing
them in substantial part, or in any republication.

## Citation

To cite the site or an article on it:

```bibtex
@misc{affinedrift,
  title        = {AffineDrift},
  author       = {Dieter Olson},
  year         = {2026},
  howpublished = {\url{https://affinedrift.com}},
  note         = {Accessed: YYYY-MM-DD}
}
```

Cite an individual article by its own title, with the same site as the
publication venue.

## Third-party material

Sources quoted or cited within the written content remain subject to their own
terms. Where a figure is redrawn from a published source, the page carries the
attribution. Vendored third-party code carries its own license file in its own
directory.

## Questions

Open an issue for anything ambiguous about reuse, or for permission requests
that do not fit the cases above.
