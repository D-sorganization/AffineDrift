states which is which, because [LICENSE](LICENSE) alone does not distinguish
them.

under normal academic practice needs no permission. Reproducing it in
substantial part, mirroring it, or republishing it elsewhere does.

## Citation

To cite the site or an article on it:

```bibtex
@misc{affinedrift,
  title        = {AffineDrift},
  author       = {Dieter Olson},
  year         = {2026},
  howpublished = {\url{https://affinedrift.com}},
  note         = {Accessed: YYYY-MM-DD}
}
```

Cite an individual article by its own title, with the same site as the
publication venue.

## Third-party material

Sources quoted or cited within the written content remain subject to their own
terms. Where a figure is redrawn from a published source, the page carries the
attribution. Vendored third-party code carries its own license file in its own
directory.

## Questions

Open an issue for anything ambiguous about reuse, or for permission requests
that do not fit the cases above.
