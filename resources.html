Sasho MacKenzie

Dr. Sasho MacKenzie is a biomechanics researcher and golf instructor known for his scientific approach to golf swing analysis. His research focuses on the physics and biomechanics of the golf swing, with particular emphasis on how forces, torques, and kinematics interact to produce clubhead speed and ball flight. His Vimeo channel features detailed video analyses of professional swings, research presentations, and educational content that bridges the gap between academic biomechanics research and practical golf instruction. His work has contributed significantly to understanding how ground reaction forces, segment sequencing, and kinematic chain efficiency affect golf performance.

Dave Tutelman Golf Website

Dave Tutelman's comprehensive website provides in-depth technical analysis of golf swing mechanics, equipment design, and the physics of golf. His work offers detailed mathematical treatments of club dynamics, ball flight, and swing biomechanics, making it an invaluable resource for understanding the engineering principles behind golf performance.
