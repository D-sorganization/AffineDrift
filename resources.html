Academic Resources

Books

  • "Nonlinear Control Systems" by Alberto Isidori

    The definitive text on geometric nonlinear control theory

  • "Applied Nonlinear Control" by Jean-Jacques Slotine & Weiping Li

    Practical approaches to nonlinear control design

  • "Mathematical Control Theory" by Eduardo Sontag

    Comprehensive treatment of control-affine systems and controllability

  • "Control Theory from the Geometric Viewpoint" by Agrachev & Sachkov

    Advanced geometric control theory and sub-Riemannian geometry

Key Papers

  • "Controllability Properties of Affine Systems" - SIAM Journal on Control

    Foundational results on controllability of affine systems

  • "Control Lyapunov and Barrier Functions for Nonlinear-Affine Systems"

    Modern survey of CLF and CBF methods (IEEE-JAS 2023)

  • "Biomechanical Analysis of the Golf Swing"

    Kinematic and kinetic studies of swing mechanics
