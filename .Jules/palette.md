## 2024-05-19 - Accessible Sidebar Toggle Menu
**Learning:** Found that sidebar collapsible sections (`.sidebar-section-toggle`) are missing `aria-controls` linked to their content containers. Also discovered that `aria-hidden` attributes on the containers are not dynamically updating to match the toggle's state. This makes it difficult for screen reader users to understand the relationship between the toggle button and the content it reveals/hides, and whether the content is currently visible.
**Action:** Always ensure that expand/collapse widgets (like `.sidebar-section-toggle`) have their interactive toggle element use `aria-controls` linked to the content container's `id`. Additionally, the container's `aria-hidden` attribute must dynamically match the inverse of the toggle's `aria-expanded` state.
