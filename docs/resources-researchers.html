Researchers

Profiles of researchers and experts in golf biomechanics, robotics, and control theory

Sasho MacKenzie

Dr. Sasho MacKenzie is a biomechanics researcher and golf instructor known for his scientific approach to golf swing analysis. His research focuses on the physics and biomechanics of the golf swing, with particular emphasis on how forces, torques, and kinematics interact to produce clubhead speed and ball flight. He has developed the Stack Training System, a comprehensive approach to improving golf performance through biomechanical analysis and training. His work has contributed significantly to understanding how ground reaction forces, segment sequencing, and kinematic chain efficiency affect golf performance. Dr. MacKenzie also teaches a Forces and Motion course that provides deep insights into the physics underlying golf swing mechanics.

Robert Groeber

Dr. Robert D. Groeber is a Professor of Applied Physics and Physics at Yale University who has combined his expertise in physics with his passion for golf to develop innovative tools and models for understanding and improving the golf swing. He developed Sonic Golf, a real-time audio biofeedback device that helps golfers improve their swing tempo and rhythm. His research focuses on using accelerometer-based instrumentation to analyze golf swing mechanics, tempo, and timing.

Dr. Young-Hoo Kwon

Dr. Young-Hoo Kwon is a Professor of Kinesiology and Director of the Biomechanics & Motor Behavior Laboratory at Texas Woman's University (TWU). He is a leading researcher in golf biomechanics, offering comprehensive swing analysis and reprogramming services to golfers at all levels using advanced 3D motion capture technology. Dr. Kwon conducts certification programs for golf instructors, focusing on biomechanics and evidence-based practices to improve golf instruction. His work at TWU's Biomechanics & Motor Behavior Laboratory helps golfers master their swings through scientific analysis and reprogramming techniques.

Dr. Rob Neal

Dr. Rob Neal is a world-renowned biomechanist and co-founder of WedgeCraft, a wedge play data collective committed to developing a comprehensive model for learning the short game through coaching and science-based methods. He is also the owner of Golf BioDynamics, where he applies biomechanical principles to golf instruction and analysis. Dr. Neal's research focuses on golf biomechanics, with particular emphasis on short game mechanics and wedge play dynamics.

Visit WedgeCraft → Visit Golf BioDynamics → Google Scholar →

Dr. Steven Nesbit

Dr. Steven M. Nesbit is a Professor of Mechanical Engineering and Head of the Department of Mechanical Engineering at Lafayette College. He has extensive research in golf biomechanics, including kinematic and kinetic studies of the golf swing. His work focuses on understanding the mechanics of the golf swing through detailed biomechanical analysis, collaborating with Jacobs 3D Golf on advanced swing analysis techniques. Dr. Nesbit's research contributes to understanding how forces, torques, and motion patterns interact in the golf swing.
