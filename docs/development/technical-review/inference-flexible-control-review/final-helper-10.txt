### 1. Mathematical Derivations & Analytical Formulas

#### A. Dynamics & Exact Solutions
* **Equations evaluated:**
  $$\dot q = v, \quad J\dot v = rF_0(a_1 - a_2) - bv - \gamma\sin q, \quad \dot a_i = \frac{u_i - a_i}{\tau_a}$$
  For constant inputs $\bar u_i$ and initial conditions $a_i(0)$:
  $$a_i(s) = \bar u_i + (a_i(0) - \bar u_i)e^{-s/\tau_a}$$
  Net activation difference:
  $$a_1(s) - a_2(s) = (\bar u_1 - \bar u_2) + \left[(a_1(0) - a_2(0)) - (\bar u_1 - \bar u_2)\right]e^{-s/\tau_a} = d + c\,e^{-s/\tau_a}$$
  where $d = \bar u_1 - \bar u_2$ and $c = a_1(0) - a_2(0) - d$.
* **Velocity integration ($b = \gamma = 0$):**
  $$\dot v(s) = \frac{rF_0}{J}(d + c\,e^{-s/\tau_a}) = A(d + c\,e^{-s/\tau_a})$$
  $$v(s) = v_0 + \int_0^s A(d + c\,e^{-\sigma/\tau_a})\,d\sigma = v_0 + A\left[d s + c\tau_a(1 - e^{-s/\tau_a})\right]$$
  *(Verified: Matches the text exactly).*
* **Position integration:**
  $$q(s) = q_0 + \int_0^s v(\sigma)\,d\sigma = q_0 + v_0 s + A \left[ \frac{1}{2}d s^2 + c\tau_a\int_0^s (1 - e^{-\sigma/\tau_a})\,d\sigma \right]$$
  $$\int_0^s (1 - e^{-\sigma/\tau_a})\,d\sigma = s - \tau_a(1 - e^{-s/\tau_a})$$
  $$q(s) = q_0 + v_0 s + A\left[\frac{1}{2}d s^2 + c\tau_a\left\{s - \tau_a(1 - e^{-s/\tau_a})\right\}\right]$$
  *(Verified: Matches the text exactly).*

#### B. Energy and Mechanical Power Balance
* Mechanical energy:
  $$E = \frac{1}{2}J v^2 + \gamma(1 - \cos q)$$
* Time derivative along trajectories:
  $$\dot E = J v \dot v + \gamma \dot q \sin q = v\left[J\dot v + \gamma \sin q\right]$$
  Substituting $J\dot v = rF_0(a_1 - a_2) - bv - \gamma\sin q$:
  $$\dot E = v\left[rF_0(a_1 - a_2) - bv\right] = rF_0(a_1 - a_2)v - bv^2$$
  *(Verified: Matches Eq.~\eqref{eq:ch10:power} exactly).*

### 4. Review of Claims & Modeling Limits

* **Modeling Scope vs. Unsupported Claims:**
  * The text explicitly states: *"It does not implement a complete muscle model, validate a golfer, or prescribe an assistive or clinical controller."*
  * It correctly distinguishes between rigid tendon vs. compliant tendon assumptions, and command-filter activation vs. energetic storage.
* **Co-contraction & Stiffness Claim:**
  * The claim: *"Equal antagonist activation gives zero net torque here. It does not increase mechanical stiffness, because force scales and moment arms have no position dependence"* is mechanically sound:
    $$\tau_{\mathrm{net}}(q) = r F_0 (a_1 - a_2) \implies K_{\mathrm{joint}} = -\frac{\partial \tau_{\mathrm{net}}}{\partial q} = 0$$
  * Co-contraction without nonlinear tendon/muscle stiffness or position-dependent moment arms cannot modulate joint stiffness. The disclaimer accurately delineates this limitation.
* **Optimal Control & Golf-Swing Inference:**
  * The discussion of Pontryagin's Minimum/Maximum Principle correctly notes that standard stationarity conditions ($\dot x = H_p, \dot p = -H_x$) and Hamiltonian maximization are necessary, not sufficient for existence or global optimality without convexity/regularity conditions.
  * The inverse dynamics / EMG critique appropriately notes that inverse dynamics yields net generalized torque, whereas muscle redundancy (force allocation) and EMG-to-force translation are conditional modeling choices rather than direct kinematic/kinetic measurements.
