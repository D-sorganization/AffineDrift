## Intertwined Argument

Geometry, external loading, elastic deformation, actuator state and parameter inference share the same motion and force conventions. A fitted swing does not identify every cause of that swing. Chapter 8 now distinguishes structural ambiguity, practical sensitivity, likelihood assumptions, prior information, parameterization and posterior uncertainty. The scalar posterior and scale-invariant free decay are manufactured, reproducible counterexamples to overclaiming identification. Fixed covariance normalization may be omitted only for the relevant point optimization; correlated samples and estimated covariance require their actual likelihood.

Chapter 9 derives the stationary planar small-deflection beam from energy, retains variable rigidity inside both spatial derivatives, and derives the tip-mass boundary sign from the weak form. The boundary coefficient is minus (B w'')' plus tip_mass times w_ddot. Its zero therefore gives derivative = tip_mass times w_ddot and derivative = minus tip_mass times omega squared times phi for a harmonic mode. The finite element model preserves the correct original Hermite matrices; it makes midpoint rigidity an explicit approximation, incorporates a point tip mass, includes clamped zero mode entries and rejects nonpositive eigenvalues. A point tip mass is not a full clubhead. Moving-reference coupling, grip impedance, torsion, head offsets, material inference and load causation remain necessary for swing interpretation.

Chapter 10 replaces a purported generic muscle simulator that ignored its force callback and fiber states with an explicitly declared antagonist rotor. It returns torque, checks actual input and solver contracts, and verifies nonzero-initial-activation solutions. Equal antagonist activation does not add stiffness to this particular model. Energy accounting distinguishes mechanical actuator work from metabolic expenditure. General theory retains q_dot = N v and solved contact reactions. PMP supplies necessary candidate conditions, not existence or a universal numerical solution method. EMG, inverse dynamics, muscle allocation and fitted forward reproduction remain distinct evidential steps.
