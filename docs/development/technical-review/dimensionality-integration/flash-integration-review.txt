#### 3. Mathematical Notation & Precision
* **Quote:**
  > `$$\rho(x,u)=\frac{\|f(x)\|_M}{\|G(x)u\|_M},\qquad \|z\|_M=\sqrt{z^T Mz}.$$`
  * **Reason:** In standard control-affine dynamics $\dot{x} = f(x) + G(x)u$, if $x \in \mathbb{R}^n$ and $u \in \mathbb{R}^m$, the vector $z$ is in the tangent/velocity space (or state-derivative space $\mathbb{R}^n$). Using simple matrix multiplication $z^T M z$ assumes $z$ is treated as a flat column vector and $M \in \mathbb{R}^{n \times n}$. However, the text later notes: *"A ratio of acceleration contributions is a different definition from a ratio of full state derivatives."* If $f(x)$ and $G(x)u$ represent acceleration components rather than full state derivatives, the dimensions of $M$ and the space of $z$ are undefined in the formula.
