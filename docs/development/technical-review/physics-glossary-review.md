## Findings and Correction Scope

- **P1 — Mechanics and intervention definitions:** outward centrifugal acceleration is confused with inertial acceleration and attributed to angular acceleration; a holonomic constraint is said not to restrict velocity; generalized momentum is incorrectly conserved whenever generalized force vanishes. Forward dynamics is confused with integration; inverse dynamics with unique feasible actuator recovery. Drift is identified with biological passivity, and late dominance/control-authority loss is asserted universally. Correct the named input port, retained state, constraints, task direction, norms and power conventions while preserving the already qualified DCR, ZTCF and ZVCF definitions.
- **P1 — Energy and impact:** COR is misidentified as a 0.83 golf-ball limit and laboratory ball-speed ratio. Conservation ignores external work; shaft bending, wrist lag and recoverable tissue energy are conflated. Proximal slowing, kinetic sequence and a shaft straight at impact do not establish energy transfer or maximal distance. Supply the one-dimensional collision assumptions and energy balance, distinguish relative and laboratory speeds, and bind equipment claims to governing test protocols.
- **P1 — Anatomy and clinical claims:** wrist cock/release is incorrectly equated to flexion/extension; ulnar deviation is assigned to backswing cock without conventions. A pain threshold is said to indicate tissue damage. Sarcomere shortening is asserted for all contraction; conscious control, reflex feedback and neural delay are conflated. Correct anatomical axes, contraction modes and pain/nociception distinctions; remove unsupported universal activation timing, injury causation and prevention guarantees.
- **P2 — Tissue and shaft mechanics:** unqualified fascia/tendon stiffness and strength rankings, water/elastin percentages, elastic recovery, modal dominance and shaft frequencies lack specimen, loading and boundary conditions. Explain modulus versus structural stiffness, viscoelastic dissipation, constrained mode definitions and force transmission without claiming established whole-body energy meridians.
- **P2 — Geometry, golf and measurement:** two-plane motion is wrongly defined as two-dimensional; a shaft line does not uniquely determine a plane; tangent lines may cross curves. Correct task-space, symmetry, velocity/speed, clubface/stance alignment, flight conditions, force-plate interpretation, COM/COP/foot-load distinctions, ball compression and unsupported equipment/performance claims.
- **P2 — Editorial and paired-source integrity:** remove duplicate self-referencing Wrapping entry, repair circular Kinetic Chain and missing Facet Joint targets, malformed print emphasis, undefined web control macro and inappropriate description metadata. Inspect every entry, preserve useful term coverage, synchronize both editions, rebuild the Physics of Golf PDF and inspect the entire changed glossary in print and browser modes.
