## Findings and Correction Scope

- **P1 — First variation and residual:** the integral Taylor remainder has an extra factor1/2; the mixed state/input remainder disappears without a joint-smallness assumption. An undefined tensor contraction replaces the controlled Jacobian. The later residual analysis drops nominal input sensitivity, confuses derivative error with nonlinear forcing, omits the linear error term and asserts cubic bounds from insufficient smoothness. Derive the epsilon-family first variation and an exact finite-error identity with bounded state/input remainder and finite-horizon amplification.
- **P1 — Paired mathematical disagreement:** the web pendulum uses a positive gravity sign at a stated downward equilibrium while retaining imaginary eigenvalues and oscillatory STM. Its Jacobi proof incorrectly uses inverse transpose and calls the derivative identity the matrix determinant lemma. Its adjoint terminal sign disagrees with its gradient convention and with the corrected print edition. Reconcile both editions through independently verified identities.
- **P1 — Discrete flow and adjoints:** the alleged nonlinear ZOH flow omits drift and substitutes an STM-weighted input formula for the nonlinear solution. The matrix-inverse ZOH formula lacks nonsingularity conditions. Parameter/state initialization and parameterized controls are omitted from adjoint gradients; arbitrary maximum/minimum observables need differentiability conditions. Derive exact flow sensitivities, singular-safe block exponential, fixed and moving endpoint terms, and distinguish continuous from discrete differentiation.
- **P1 — Numerical examples and QR:** pendulum cosine and impulse matrices are wrong; the initial angle response is off by a factor of ten, torque-impulse units omit seconds and0.25s is called a quarter period. The QR algorithm accumulates triangular factors in reverse order and promises bounded conditioning for arbitrarily long propagation. Replace with checked examples, correct left accumulation and explicit finite-precision limits.
- **P1 — Stability, volume and authority:** divergence omits the controlled field; negative trace is equated with contraction and uniform exponential shrinkage. Frozen eigenvalues are treated as decisive LTV stability evidence. Input-matrix rank is confused with mechanical underactuation, a planar quadrotor is said not to generate torque, and the condition-number formula misses a square. Give counterexamples, metric/units-aware growth and finite-horizon output sensitivity with feasible input bounds.
- **P2 — Numerical and geometric claims:** unsupported default Padé7/8 and guaranteed unit-roundoff/spectral accuracy, blanket cost/efficiency claims, stiffness versus conditioning, absolute epsilon singularity test, and 'early inputs most influential' need correction. STM is a flow derivative, not generally Levi-Civita parallel transport. Finite Taylor error need not mean manifold curvature. Quadratic DDP/SQP convergence does not follow from first-order remainder alone. Symplectic schemes do not universally bound energy for every step/time; not all Runge-Kutta schemes break symplectic structure; group updates need angular-velocity hat/frame conventions and floating-point limits. State variational-integrator regularity/symmetry/forcing conditions and repair ambiguous exercises.
