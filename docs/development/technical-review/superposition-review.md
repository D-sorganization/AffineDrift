## Findings Addressed

- Separate tangent-vector addition, exact fixed-state input increments and finite trajectory effects. The affine anchor is counted once. The final chapter summary incorrectly calls the total acceleration map linear. The QMD worked example substitutes zero for a nonzero zero-torque acceleration and then declares the correct difference identity false. A gain is the derivative or incremental ratio, not total acceleration divided by torque. Finite response comparisons need the evolving baseline, a shared chart and common initial state; exceptional symmetric pendulum inputs do not prove universal trajectory superposition or its failure.
- Declare model/input/state assumptions and retained loads. The first-order drift silently omits external loads present in the preceding equation. The body's COM and common expression frame must be specified in its kinetic energy. Body count and configuration dimension need not agree. Positive inertia is conditional on a regular independent inertial model, not a theorem covering massless freedoms, redundant charts and every configuration representation.
- Distinguish Christoffel symbols of the first kind (force-level velocity products) from the second kind (acceleration-level connection). The present equation adds the latter directly to M times acceleration. Geodesic motion also requires no potential gradient, damping or applied load.
- Repair the Newton–Euler body-frame force balance with transport terms, force moment arms and defined state coordinates. The planar arm uses q1 measured from horizontal and q2 relative, not both absolute. Its informal gravity equation has the wrong trigonometric function/sign and suppresses coupled acceleration. I1 is reused inconsistently for total effective inertia and individual COM inertia. Derive the same uniform-rod arm in all formulations.
- The QMD arm Coriolis vector differs from the TEX: it has a doubled coefficient and reversed first-component sign. The QMD energy identity also has the wrong sign and falsely invokes orthogonality to velocity. Reconcile both editions to the Cartesian kinetic-energy derivation and canonical C matrix, with independent body force/moment checks.
- A stack of free-body spatial inertias cannot be inverted as a complete connected-chain forward-dynamics algorithm while omitting joint constraints and reactions. Declare body twists/origins, kinematic map, bias acceleration and dual virtual-work map. Generalized effort does not uniquely specify an arbitrary unconstrained collection of body wrenches.
- Input power is u-transpose B-transpose v for the general actuation map. Power summation does not imply independent finite work contributions or prove acceleration affinity. The velocity-product power cancels the mass-derivative term; it is not dissipation. Joint reaction transfer and actuator power must be reconciled with body balances.
- Distinguish a regular configuration constraint manifold from each state's tangent velocity space; rolling without slip is not generally holonomic. Derive input-dependent multipliers, curvature offsets and reduced dynamics including the derivative of the tangent basis. Contact mode and friction feasibility remain necessary.
- Replace all unsupported trajectory tables. The purported pendulum accelerations disagree with -9.81 sin(q)+tau and even exceed the maximum gravity magnitude 9.81. The plus/minus input trajectories must be exact sign reflections under the odd dynamics and zero initial state. Use reproducible integration, a nonlinear non-symmetric input comparison, explicit tolerances/convergence and work-energy balance. A zero-reference relative error is undefined; it cannot be reported as zero percent by default.
- State passivity as a storage inequality with a correctly paired port and storage bounded below. Lossless equality is allowed away from equilibrium. Bounded input alone does not ensure bounded energy or trajectories; a free mass is a counterexample. Well-posed passive feedback gives an energy inequality, not automatic asymptotic stability, detectability or coercivity. A second-law assertion cannot verify a chosen mechanical damping matrix. Port-Hamiltonian structure requires a suitable representation, not arbitrary control-affine dynamics.
- Dissipation and passivity are not contraction. Positive R alone does not give a uniform exponential energy bound; even an energy decay result is not a differential contraction certificate. A damped pendulum has multiple equilibria and an indefinite energy Hessian near its upright posture. The full differential metric inequality, its domain and uniform bounds must be checked. Remove the unsupported categorical preference for contraction over feedback linearization.
- State-dependent feasible inputs change the reachable acceleration set without breaking affinity at a fixed state. Minkowski sums require independent channel sets. Derive an electric motor's voltage/current/torque relations with both torque and back-EMF constants and appropriate asymmetric voltage-limited bounds. Expanded actuator state can preserve command affinity. Distinguish nonsmooth friction from input nonlinearity; static friction at zero velocity is a reaction interval, not sign(0)=0.
- Use a consistent Lie-bracket convention and lowercase single field g. Separate accessibility, fixed-time accessibility, reversible driftless Chow conditions and controllability with drift/input bounds. Drift cannot generally be reversed as a physical maneuver. For second-order mechanics, [f,g] has a configuration component and is not simply an unactuated acceleration; full actuation also has fewer immediate input directions than total state dimension. Demonstrate actual coupling rather than treating a named bracket as evidence for a golf sequencing mechanism.
- Feedback linearization is generally local and requires neighborhood relative-degree conditions, admissible effort and a valid coordinate map. Input-output linearization need not linearize internal dynamics. Nonminimum-phase behavior limits exact tracking, but does not imply every trajectory diverges or every finite-horizon use is categorically unsafe. Contraction robustness also requires an explicit perturbation margin.
- Repair exercise assumptions: mass parameters and a chosen coordinate scale are required for a condition-number maximum; B alone does not determine underactuated bracket rank; an energy storage offset may be needed; zero-dimensional internal dynamics are vacuous rather than a hidden unstable subsystem. Preserve cross-chapter references and reconcile web/print formulas, tables and explanations.
