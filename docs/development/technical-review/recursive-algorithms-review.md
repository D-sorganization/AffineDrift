## Independent Derivations and Numerical Checks

### Motion, Force, and Acceleration

Starting from u_B(x_B)=v_B+ω_B×x_B, substitute x_B=R^T(x_A-p) and rotate the field to obtain ω_A=Rω_B and v_A=Rv_B+p×Rω_B. The inverse block is [R^T,0;-R^T p×,R^T]. Enforcing f_A^T X V_B=f_B^T V_B for all V_B yields X^T f_A=f_B. Thus the reverse load transport in the tree is a transpose. The numeric test includes a 90-degree z rotation, origin shift(.7,-.2,.4), a general twist and a wrench with both force and couple; it verifies moment shift and power, and verifies the erroneous inverse-transpose reverse route disagrees.

For constant local screw S, dotX=-ad_(S qdot) X. Differentiating V=X Vp+S qdot then yields a=X ap+S qddot+ad_V(S qdot). Antisymmetry changes the sign when the order is reversed. A body-coordinate twist derivative's linear part is dotv; ordinary origin acceleration in the same axes is dotv+ω×v. At fixed point r, add dotω×r+ω×(ω×r). Effective gravity-shifted arrays need the gravity contribution restored before interpretation as physical acceleration.

For V=(0,0,.5,.4,0,0), VJ=(0,0,.2,0,0,0), the bracket is(0,0,0,0,-.08,0). The inherited linear velocity at link2 of the specified mechanism is(.35341259,.35369413,0), giving bias(.10610824,-.10602378,0). Link3 bias is(.19873883,-.07068252,0). Only angular components vanish. The old angular-only test is replaced, not preserved as evidence of a full spatial identity.

### Inertia, Generalized Force, and Energy

COM velocity is v+ω×c=v-[c]×ω. Expanding its squared norm gives the cross block m[c]×, transpose -m[c]× and rotational block I_C-m[c]×². The quadratic form is strictly positive for nonzero twists if m>0 and I_C is positive definite; degenerate ideal bodies require a separate admissible-motion analysis. At a COM origin Newton--Euler gives moment I_C dotω+ω×I_Cω and force m(dotv+ω×v), which match the spatial formula and its dual frame transformation.

For V_i=J_i v, M=ΣJ_i^T I_i J_i follows directly from kinetic energy. Internal interface virtual powers cancel when expressed in compatible frames, leaving joint effort paired with relative admissible motion; ideal constraint reactions annihilate that motion. This supplies the full force equivalence to Lagrange under the declared assumptions. Affinity in qdd alone is insufficient. The matrix-column test subtracts the same nonzero gravity/velocity bias from each coordinate response and compares with the independent Cartesian kinetic-energy mass matrix.

The branched prismatic test uses A=[[1,0,0],[1,1,0],[1,0,1]], masses(2,1.2,.6), parent array(-1,0,0), and external x-forces(.4,-.3,1.1) on the parent and both children. Cartesian Newton equations independently give τ=A^T(diag(m)A a-Fext). The test also checks external virtual power. This catches missing nonleaf loads and omitted sibling accumulation.

### Constraints and Geometric Differentials

With M a-J^Tλ=r and J a=-dotJ v, substitute a=M^-1(r+J^Tλ) to obtain Wλ=-dotJ v-JM^-1r, W=JM^-1J^T. Full row rank and M positive definite imply W positive definite. Redundant rows can leave reactions nonunique; underactuation or bounds can make prescribed acceleration infeasible. The impact momentum jump uses impulse, not smooth-force substitution. Normal acceleration complementarity applies at closed contact with zero normal velocity; it is not a universal impact law.

## Exercise Solution and Scope Notes

1. Invertible frame changes: substitute T^-1=(R^T,-R^Tp) into Ad. For pure translation, the off-diagonal block squares to zero, so inverse changes its sign; determinant remains1.
2. Two-link tree: one-based parents(0,1), identical local z rotation subspaces at proximal origins. With R_i=Rot_z(-q_i), r_1=0, r_2=(L1,0,0), X_i=[R_i,0;-R_i[r_i]×,R_i]. Python parents(-1,0).
3. Planar bracket: the only nonzero component is linear y acceleration -.08m/s² for the given SI inputs. The angular three-vector computation discards the responsible v×ωJ block.
4. Power: f_A=X^-T f_B and f_B=X^T f_A. A pure translation with force not parallel to its offset supplies a direct nonzero moment counterexample to the wrong reverse transform.
5. Planar inertia with ordering(ωz,vx,vy) and COM on local x is [[I_C+mc²,0,mc],[0,m,0],[mc,0,m]]. Each input body's values follow directly. A point mass has a rank-deficient unrestricted spatial energy, while independent allowed joint velocities can still move sufficient mass for M to be positive definite; verify the actual matrix rather than infer this universally.
6. Rigid-body balance: differentiate linear/angular momentum at the COM in rotating axes, then use wrench and inertia coordinate transformations. The virtual-power argument in the chapter establishes generalized equivalence with ideal constraints.
7. Four-body branch: f4=f4own; τ4=S4^Tf4; f3=f3own+X4^Tf4; τ3=S3^Tf3; f2=f2own; τ2=S2^Tf2; f1=f1own+X2^Tf2+X3^Tf3; τ1=S1^Tf1; support=X1^Tf1. Each own term includes its external subtraction. Leaf2 affects joint2 and joint1; leaf4 affects joints4,3,1. No leaf2 influence on branch3's inverse effort at fixed prescribed motion and known loads.
8. Dual operators: transpose the block matrix to obtain force cross; test arbitrary twists and full origin shifts. The three analytic two-rod configurations are covered by the new tests.
9. Timing exercise: distinguish precomputed input matrices from preparation/factorization, count all work required by the requested output, compare equal physical models and report numerical agreement. No hardware benchmark or performance ratio is fabricated for this review.
10. Cut loop: restore closure position, velocity and acceleration constraints and solve the coupled system. Redundant rows may require independent-row selection or a declared minimum-norm reaction convention; inconsistent prescribed motion has no ideal constrained inverse solution.
11. Composition: conjugating successively by T2 and T1 equals conjugating by T1T2. For motion X12=X1X2, the same-direction force transform is X12^-T=X1^-T X2^-T. Verify the reversal inside the inverse and again in its transpose; do not reverse the final order twice.
12. Exponential derivative: differentiate the series or solve the first variation of dotY=AY on unit time. The noncommuting integral follows; right/left multiplications give the explicitly ordered integrals above. H commuting with A is a sufficient special case for e^A H.
13. Humanoid exercise is a model-design task, not a claim of an anatomical dataset. One admissible count is floating base6 plus24 articulated velocity DOFs: each leg6 (hip3,knee1,ankle2), each arm5 (shoulder3,elbow1,forearm1), trunk2. Bodies/subspaces can be represented by multi-DOF joints or ordered scalar joints with suitable inertias. A complete submitted model must supply its own segment dimensions/inertias and axes; no measured anatomical numbers are invented here. Contact constraints and unactuated six-dimensional base balance restrict which accelerations/forces are feasible.
14. CRBA: accumulate composite inertias inward, form F_i=I_i^C S_i and M_ii=S_i^TF_i, transport F_i up its ancestors and project at each ancestor to fill M_ji, then reflect symmetry. Branches without an ancestor relation have zero cross terms in a fixed-base tree's joint-space M; floating-base elimination and constraints can introduce coupling. Linear composite pass plus O(sum of depths) assembly, worst-case O(N²), must be separated from dense output storage.
15. Compliant contact: specify penetration convention, nonadhesive release, damping and state. A spring-damper normal mode has frequency sqrt(k/m_eff); numerical stability depends on the integrator and step. Compare refined trajectories, work and peak forces, and evaluate the defined delivery event rather than treating the initial inverse torque as an outcome.
