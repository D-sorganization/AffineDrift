1. **Sliding Energy Dissipation Rate Formula:**

     $$\dot{E} = -\mu_k m g |s|$$
   * *Derivation / Verification:*

     Differentiating with respect to time:
     $$\dot{E} = mv\dot{v} + I\omega\dot{\omega} = mv\bigl(-\mu_k g \operatorname{sgn}(s)\bigr) + (\kappa m R^2)\omega\left(\frac{\mu_k g}{\kappa R}\operatorname{sgn}(s)\right) = -\mu_k m g \operatorname{sgn}(s)(v - R\omega) = -\mu_k m g |s|.$$
     This expression is exact and correct.

2. **Energy Lost During Sliding:**
   * *Text expression:*
     $$E_0 - E_r = \frac{m\kappa}{2(1+\kappa)}s_0^2$$
   * *Derivation / Verification:*
     $s_0 = v_0 - R\omega_0$. At the transition, $s(t_r) = 0 \implies v_r = R\omega_r$.
     $$v_r = \frac{v_0 + \kappa R\omega_0}{1+\kappa} = v_0 - \frac{\kappa}{1+\kappa}s_0, \qquad R\omega_r = v_r = R\omega_0 + \frac{1}{1+\kappa}s_0.$$
     Evaluating initial vs final energy:
     $$E_0 - E_r = \frac{1}{2}m(v_0^2 - v_r^2) + \frac{1}{2}\kappa m (R^2\omega_0^2 - v_r^2) = \frac{m\kappa}{2(1+\kappa)}(v_0 - R\omega_0)^2 = \frac{m\kappa}{2(1+\kappa)}s_0^2.$$
     This formula is exact and correct.

3. **Compound Pendulum Equation of Motion & Equivalent Length:**

4. **Rolling Resistance Acceleration and Contact Law:**
