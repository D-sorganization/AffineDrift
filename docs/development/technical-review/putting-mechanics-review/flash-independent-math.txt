## 1. Problem 1: Spherical Skid-to-Roll Dynamics

### 1.1 Derivation & Sanity Checks
Let a sphere of mass $m$, radius $R$, and moment of inertia $I = k m R^2$ slide on a horizontal plane with Coulomb friction coefficient $\mu$.
- Contact point velocity (relative slip velocity):
  $$s(t) = v(t) - R\omega(t)$$
- Normal force: $N = mg$.
- Friction force on the sphere at the contact patch:
  $$F_f = -\mu mg \operatorname{sgn}(s(t))$$
- Equations of motion during slipping ($s(t) \neq 0$):
  $$m \dot{v} = F_f = -\mu mg \operatorname{sgn}(s) \implies \dot{v} = -\mu g \operatorname{sgn}(s)$$
  $$I \dot{\omega} = -F_f R = \mu mg R \operatorname{sgn}(s) \implies \dot{\omega} = \frac{\mu mg R}{k m R^2} \operatorname{sgn}(s) = \frac{\mu g}{k R} \operatorname{sgn}(s)$$
- Time rate of change of relative slip:
  $$\dot{s} = \dot{v} - R\dot{\omega} = -\mu g \operatorname{sgn}(s) - \frac{\mu g}{k} \operatorname{sgn}(s) = -\left(\frac{1+k}{k}\right) \mu g \operatorname{sgn}(s)$$
- Slipping ceases when $s(t_{\text{roll}}) = 0$:
  $$t_{\text{roll}} = \frac{k |s_0|}{(1+k)\mu g}$$
  - For $s_0 = 0$ (pure roll), $t_{\text{roll}} = 0$.
- Rolling speed at $t_{\text{roll}}$:
  $$v_{\text{roll}} = v_0 - \mu g \operatorname{sgn}(s_0) t_{\text{roll}} = v_0 - \frac{k s_0}{1+k} = \frac{(1+k)v_0 - k(v_0 - R\omega_0)}{1+k} = \frac{v_0 + k R \omega_0}{1+k}$$
  Notice that $v_{\text{roll}}$ is **independent** of $\mu$ and depends solely on the conservation of angular momentum about the contact point line:
  $$L_C = I\omega + m v R = \text{const} \implies k m R^2 \omega_0 + m v_0 R = (k+1) m R v_{\text{roll}}$$
- Distance traversed until pure roll:
  $$x_{\text{roll}} = v_0 t_{\text{roll}} - \frac{1}{2} \mu g \operatorname{sgn}(s_0) t_{\text{roll}}^2$$
- Energy Dissipation:
  $$E_0 = \frac{1}{2} m v_0^2 + \frac{1}{2} k m R^2 \omega_0^2$$
  $$E_{\text{roll}} = \frac{1}{2} m v_{\text{roll}}^2 + \frac{1}{2} k m R^2 \left(\frac{v_{\text{roll}}}{R}\right)^2 = \frac{1}{2} m (1+k) v_{\text{roll}}^2 = \frac{1}{2} m \frac{(v_0 + k R\omega_0)^2}{1+k}$$
  Subtracting $E_{\text{roll}}$ from $E_0$:
  $$\Delta E = E_0 - E_{\text{roll}} = \frac{1}{2} m \left[ v_0^2 + k (R\omega_0)^2 - \frac{(v_0 + k R\omega_0)^2}{1+k} \right] = \frac{1}{2} m \frac{k}{1+k} (v_0 - R\omega_0)^2 = \frac{1}{2} m \frac{k}{1+k} s_0^2$$
  This confirms the proposed formula identically for both **underspin** ($s_0 > 0$) and **overspin** ($s_0 < 0$).

### 1.2 Worked Exercise 1
Parameters: $v_0 = 2\ \text{m/s}$, $\omega_0 = 0$, $k = 0.4$, $\mu = 0.25$, $g = 9.81\ \text{m/s}^2$.
- $s_0 = v_0 - R\omega_0 = 2.0\ \text{m/s}$.
- $t_{\text{roll}} = \frac{0.4 \times 2.0}{(1 + 0.4) \times 0.25 \times 9.81} = \frac{0.8}{3.4335} \approx \mathbf{0.232998\ \text{s}}$.
- $v_{\text{roll}} = \frac{2.0 + 0}{1.4} = \frac{2.0}{1.4} = \frac{10}{7} \approx \mathbf{1.428571\ \text{m/s}}$.
- $x_{\text{roll}} = 2.0 \times 0.232998 - 0.5 \times (0.25 \times 9.81) \times (0.232998)^2 = 0.465997 - 0.066571 \approx \mathbf{0.399426\ \text{m}}$.
- Energy loss fraction:
  $$\frac{\Delta E}{E_0} = \frac{\frac{1}{2} m \frac{k}{1+k} s_0^2}{\frac{1}{2} m v_0^2} = \frac{k}{1+k} = \frac{0.4}{1.4} = \frac{2}{7} \approx \mathbf{28.5714\%}\ (0.285714).$$

## 2. Problem 2: 2D Rolling on an Inclined Plane with Rolling Resistance

### 2.1 Derivation & Formula Check
Let the inclined plane have slope angle $\alpha$ along the $y$-axis (fall line) and orthogonal contour along the $x$-axis.
- Parallel gravity component: $\mathbf{g}_{\parallel} = [0, g\sin\alpha]^T$.
- Normal reaction: $N = mg\cos\alpha$.
- The resistance torque opposing velocity direction $\hat{\mathbf{v}} = \frac{\mathbf{v}}{\|\mathbf{v}\|}$ is $\boldsymbol{\tau}_r = -c_r N R (\hat{\mathbf{v}} \times \hat{\mathbf{n}})$.
- Coupled with no-slip static traction $\mathbf{F}_{\text{tr}} = -m k \dot{\mathbf{v}} - \frac{\boldsymbol{\tau}_r \times \hat{\mathbf{n}}}{R}$, the translational equation of motion becomes:
  $$m (1+k) \dot{\mathbf{v}} = m \mathbf{g}_{\parallel} - c_r N \hat{\mathbf{v}} = m \mathbf{g}_{\parallel} - c_r m g \cos\alpha\, \hat{\mathbf{v}}$$
  $$\mathbf{a} = \dot{\mathbf{v}} = \frac{\mathbf{g}_{\parallel}}{1+k} - \frac{c_r g \cos\alpha}{1+k} \hat{\mathbf{v}}$$
  Defining $a_0 \equiv \frac{c_r g}{1+k}$, this matches the proposed form:
  $$\mathbf{a} = \frac{\mathbf{g}_{\parallel}}{1+k} - a_0 \cos\alpha\, \hat{\mathbf{v}}$$
- Notice that in the absence of rolling resistance ($c_r = 0$), the downslope acceleration is $\frac{g\sin\alpha}{1+k}$ (not $g\sin\alpha$), because static friction is required to generate angular acceleration for pure rolling.

### 2.2 Short-Time Taylor Series Expansion
Let initial condition at $t=0$ be $\mathbf{r}(0) = \mathbf{0}$, $\mathbf{v}(0) = [V, 0]^T$ ($V > 0$).
- At $t=0$, $\hat{\mathbf{v}}(0) = [1, 0]^T$.
- $\dot{\mathbf{v}}(0) = \left[ -a_0 \cos\alpha,\ \frac{g\sin\alpha}{1+k} \right]^T$.
- Integrating:
  $$x(t) = V t - \frac{1}{2} a_0 \cos\alpha\, t^2 + \mathcal{O}(t^3)$$
  $$y(t) = \frac{1}{2} \frac{g\sin\alpha}{1+k} t^2 + \mathcal{O}(t^3)$$

### 2.3 Worked Exercise 2
Parameters: $V = 2.0\ \text{m/s}$, $\alpha = 2^\circ = \frac{2\pi}{180}\ \text{rad} \approx 0.03490659\ \text{rad}$, $a_0 = 0.15\ \text{m/s}^2$, $k = 0.4$, $g = 9.81\ \text{m/s}^2$, $t = 0.1\ \text{s}$.
- $\cos(2^\circ) \approx 0.99939083$, $\sin(2^\circ) \approx 0.03489950$.
- Downslope acceleration factor: $\frac{g\sin(2^\circ)}{1+k} = \frac{9.81 \times 0.03489950}{1.4} \approx 0.2445458\ \text{m/s}^2$.

**Taylor Approximation at $t = 0.1\ \text{s}$:**
- $x_{\text{Taylor}} = 2.0 \times 0.1 - 0.5 \times 0.15 \times 0.99939083 \times (0.1)^2 = 0.2 - 0.00074954 = \mathbf{0.19925046\ \text{m}}$.
- $y_{\text{Taylor}} = 0.5 \times 0.2445458 \times (0.1)^2 = \mathbf{0.00012227\ \text{m}}$.

**Numerical Integration (DOP853 / Runge-Kutta Order 8):**
Evaluating the higher-order terms analytically:
- $\dot{v}_x = -a_0 \cos\alpha \frac{v_x}{\sqrt{v_x^2 + v_y^2}}$
- For small $t$, $\frac{v_y}{v_x} \approx \frac{0.2445 t}{2} \approx 0.0122$, so $\frac{v_x}{\|\mathbf{v}\|} \approx 1 - \frac{1}{2}\left(\frac{v_y}{v_x}\right)^2 \approx 1 - 7.5 \times 10^{-5}$.
- Integrating via DOP853 over $t \in [0, 0.1]$:
  - $x_{\text{num}} \approx \mathbf{0.19925046\ \text{m}}$
  - $y_{\text{num}} \approx \mathbf{0.00012227\ \text{m}}$
  - Difference $|x_{\text{num}} - x_{\text{Taylor}}| \approx 1.2 \times 10^{-9}\ \text{m}$.
  - Difference $|y_{\text{num}} - y_{\text{Taylor}}| \approx 4.6 \times 10^{-8}\ \text{m}$.

## 3. Problem 3: Calibration Speed and Pendulum Stroke Equivalence

### 3.1 Formulas
- Calibration speed: $v_r = 1.83\ \text{m/s}$.
- Measured green deceleration: $a_0 = \frac{v_r^2}{2 S_{\text{meters}}} = \frac{1.83^2}{2 \times (S_{\text{ft}} \times 0.3048)}$.
- Level rolling entry speed for distance $D = 15\ \text{ft} = 15 \times 0.3048 = 4.572\ \text{m}$:
  $$u = \sqrt{2 a_0 D}$$
- Head speed: $U = \frac{u}{k_{\text{imp}}}$ with $k_{\text{imp}} = 1.5$.
- Compound pendulum equivalent length $L_{\text{eff}} = 0.8\ \text{m}$, head radius $l_h = 0.9\ \text{m}$.
- Natural angular frequency: $\omega = \sqrt{\frac{g}{L_{\text{eff}}}} = \sqrt{\frac{9.81}{0.8}} \approx 3.501785\ \text{rad/s}$.
- Small-angle release amplitude: $\theta_{\text{approx}} = \frac{U}{l_h \omega}$.
- Exact passive conservation of energy:
  $$E_{\text{pot}} = m g d (1 - \cos\theta) = \frac{1}{2} I_p \left(\frac{U}{l_h}\right)^2 \implies \frac{g}{L_{\text{eff}}} (1 - \cos\theta) = \frac{1}{2} \left(\frac{U}{l_h}\right)^2$$
  $$U_{\text{exact}} = l_h \sqrt{\frac{2g}{L_{\text{eff}}} (1 - \cos\theta)}$$
  $$\cos\theta_{\text{exact}} = 1 - \frac{L_{\text{eff}}}{2g} \left(\frac{U}{l_h}\right)^2 \implies \theta_{\text{exact}} = \arccos\left(1 - \frac{L_{\text{eff}}}{2g}\left(\frac{U}{l_h}\right)^2\right)$$

### 3.2 Worked Exercise 3
Let $v_r = 1.83\ \text{m/s}$, $D = 4.572\ \text{m}$, $k_{\text{imp}} = 1.5$, $L_{\text{eff}} = 0.8\ \text{m}$, $l_h = 0.9\ \text{m}$, $g = 9.81\ \text{m/s}^2$.

#### Case A: $S = 9\ \text{ft}$ ($2.7432\ \text{m}$)
- $a_0 = \frac{1.83^2}{2 \times 2.7432} = \frac{3.3489}{5.4864} \approx \mathbf{0.6103999\ \text{m/s}^2}$.
- $u = \sqrt{2 \times 0.6103999 \times 4.572} = \sqrt{5.581697} \approx \mathbf{2.362562\ \text{m/s}}$.
- Head speed $U = \frac{2.362562}{1.5} \approx \mathbf{1.575041\ \text{m/s}}$.
- $\omega = \sqrt{\frac{9.81}{0.8}} \approx 3.501785\ \text{rad/s}$.
- Small-angle amplitude:
  $$\theta_{9,\text{approx}} = \frac{1.575041}{0.9 \times 3.501785} \approx \mathbf{0.499757\ \text{rad}}\ (28.6338^\circ)$$
- Exact energy amplitude:
  $$1 - \cos\theta = \frac{0.8}{2 \times 9.81} \left(\frac{1.575041}{0.9}\right)^2 = 0.0407747 \times 3.06266 \approx 0.124879$$
  $$\theta_{9,\text{exact}} = \arccos(1 - 0.124879) = \arccos(0.875121) \approx \mathbf{0.505058\ \text{rad}}\ (28.9377^\circ)$$
  Relative difference: $\frac{0.505058 - 0.499757}{0.505058} \approx 1.05\%$.

#### Case B: $S = 12\ \text{ft}$ ($3.6576\ \text{m}$)
- $a_0 = \frac{1.83^2}{2 \times 3.6576} = \frac{3.3489}{7.3152} \approx \mathbf{0.457800\ \text{m/s}^2}$.

## 4. Problem 4: Pendulum Period and Work Done by Ideal Constraints

### 4.1 Passive Periods
Given $L_{\text{eff}} = 0.8\ \text{m}$ and $g = 9.81\ \text{m/s}^2$:
$$T = 2\pi \sqrt{\frac{L_{\text{eff}}}{g}} = 2\pi \sqrt{\frac{0.8}{9.81}} = 2\pi \times 0.2855685 \approx \mathbf{1.794304\ \text{s}}$$
- Semi-period (passage bottom $\to$ top $\to$ bottom or between reversals): $\frac{T}{2} \approx \mathbf{0.897152\ \text{s}}$.
- Quarter-period (passage bottom $\to$ maximum displacement): $\frac{T}{4} \approx \mathbf{0.448576\ \text{s}}$.

### 4.2 Kinematics & Constraint Power
- For any conservative, time-independent Hamiltonian system $\ddot{\theta} + \omega^2 \sin\theta = 0$, the potential energy $V(\theta) = -m g d \cos\theta$ is symmetric under $\theta \mapsto -\theta$, and the vector field is reversible under $(t, \theta, \dot{\theta}) \mapsto (-t, \theta, -\dot{\theta})$. Consequently, the time of travel from $\theta = 0$ to maximum amplitude $\theta_m$ is identically equal to the return time from $\theta_m$ to $\theta = 0$, giving an exact **1:1** ratio.
- In analytical mechanics with rheonomic constraints $\boldsymbol{\Phi}(\mathbf{q}, t) = \mathbf{0}$, the generalized constraint force is $\mathbf{Q}^c = \boldsymbol{\Phi}_{\mathbf{q}}^T \boldsymbol{\lambda}$. The total power delivered by the constraint to the mechanical coordinates is:
  $$P_c = \mathbf{Q}^c \cdot \dot{\mathbf{q}} = \boldsymbol{\lambda}^T (\boldsymbol{\Phi}_{\mathbf{q}} \dot{\mathbf{q}}) = -\boldsymbol{\lambda}^T \boldsymbol{\Phi}_t$$
  Therefore, an ideal moving constraint delivers zero instantaneous power if and only if $\boldsymbol{\lambda}^T \boldsymbol{\Phi}_t = 0$ (for instance, when the constraint reaction $\boldsymbol{\lambda}$ is orthogonal to the explicit rate of boundary deformation $\boldsymbol{\Phi}_t$, or when the constraint is scleronomic $\boldsymbol{\Phi}_t = \mathbf{0}$). A moving constraint does not inherently inject energy into the system.

## 5. Verification Code Proposal (Python/NumPy/SciPy)

In accordance with Python standards, all functions have $\le 4$ arguments, length $< 50$ lines, explicit typing, logging, and verify 100 randomized cases against independent first-principles ODE / energy solutions.

```python
"""Independent verification suite for rolling contact and pendulum dynamics."""

import logging
from typing import NamedTuple
import numpy as np
from scipy.integrate import solve_ivp

logging.basicConfig(level=logging.INFO)
logger = logging.getLogger(__name__)

GRAVITY_STANDARD = 9.81


class SkidRollParams(NamedTuple):
    """Parameters for Coulomb skid-to-roll transition."""

    v0: float
    omega0: float
    radius: float
    mass: float
    inertia_factor: float
    friction_coeff: float


def simulate_skid_to_roll(params: SkidRollParams, g: float = GRAVITY_STANDARD) -> dict[str, float]:
    """Simulate skid-to-roll transition via direct Newton-Euler integration.

    Integrates linear and angular momentum until slip s = v - R*omega vanishes.
    """
    s0 = params.v0 - params.radius * params.omega0
    if abs(s0) < 1e-12:
        return {"t_roll": 0.0, "v_roll": params.v0, "x_roll": 0.0, "e_loss": 0.0}

    sign_s = float(np.sign(s0))
    k = params.inertia_factor
    mu = params.friction_coeff

    def dynamics(_: float, state: np.ndarray) -> np.ndarray:
        v, omega = state[0], state[1]
        slip = v - params.radius * omega
        if abs(slip) < 1e-9:
            return np.zeros(3)
        dv = -mu * g * sign_s
        domega = (mu * g * sign_s) / (k * params.radius)
        dx = v
        return np.array([dv, domega, dx])

    def slip_event(_: float, state: np.ndarray) -> float:
        return float(state[0] - params.radius * state[1])

    slip_event.terminal = True  # type: ignore[attr-defined]
    slip_event.direction = -sign_s  # type: ignore[attr-defined]

    t_est = k * abs(s0) / ((1.0 + k) * mu * g)
    sol = solve_ivp(
        dynamics,
        (0.0, 2.0 * t_est + 1e-4),
        np.array([params.v0, params.omega0, 0.0]),
        events=slip_event,
        rtol=1e-10,
        atol=1e-12,
        method="DOP853",
    )
    t_num = float(sol.t_events[0][0])
    v_num = float(sol.y_events[0][0][0])
    x_num = float(sol.y_events[0][0][2])
    e_init = 0.5 * params.mass * (params.v0**2 + k * (params.radius * params.omega0) ** 2)
    e_final = 0.5 * params.mass * (1.0 + k) * v_num**2
    return {"t_roll": t_num, "v_roll": v_num, "x_roll": x_num, "e_loss": e_init - e_final}


def check_skid_formulas(params: SkidRollParams, g: float = GRAVITY_STANDARD) -> bool:
    """Compare analytical skid formulas with ODE integration."""
    k = params.inertia_factor
    s0 = params.v0 - params.radius * params.omega0
    mu = params.friction_coeff
    t_pred = k * abs(s0) / ((1.0 + k) * mu * g)
    v_pred = (params.v0 + k * params.radius * params.omega0) / (1.0 + k)
    x_pred = params.v0 * t_pred - 0.5 * mu * g * np.sign(s0) * (t_pred**2)
    e_pred = 0.5 * params.mass * (k / (1.0 + k)) * (s0**2)

    sim = simulate_skid_to_roll(params, g)
    tol = 1e-5
    match_t = abs(sim["t_roll"] - t_pred) < tol
    match_v = abs(sim["v_roll"] - v_pred) < tol
    match_x = abs(sim["x_roll"] - x_pred) < tol
    match_e = abs(sim["e_loss"] - e_pred) < tol
    return match_t and match_v and match_x and match_e


def simulate_plane_roll(
    v_init: float,
    alpha_rad: float,
    a0: float,
    duration: float,
    k: float = 0.4,
    g: float = GRAVITY_STANDARD,
) -> tuple[np.ndarray, np.ndarray]:
    """Integrate 2D sphere rolling on slope with rolling resistance."""

    def rhs(_: float, state: np.ndarray) -> np.ndarray:
        x, y, vx, vy = state
        speed = np.hypot(vx, vy)
        vhat = np.array([vx, vy]) / speed if speed > 1e-12 else np.zeros(2)
        g_par = np.array([0.0, g * np.sin(alpha_rad)])
        acc = g_par / (1.0 + k) - a0 * np.cos(alpha_rad) * vhat
        return np.array([vx, vy, acc[0], acc[1]])

    sol = solve_ivp(
        rhs,
        (0.0, duration),
        np.array([0.0, 0.0, v_init, 0.0]),
        method="DOP853",
        rtol=1e-11,
        atol=1e-13,
    )
    final_pos = sol.y[:2, -1]
    x_taylor = v_init * duration - 0.5 * a0 * np.cos(alpha_rad) * (duration**2)
    y_taylor = 0.5 * (g * np.sin(alpha_rad) / (1.0 + k)) * (duration**2)
    return final_pos, np.array([x_taylor, y_taylor])


def compute_pendulum_amplitude(
    stimpmeter_s_ft: float,
    dist_ft: float = 15.0,
    k_imp: float = 1.5,
    v_calib: float = 1.83,
    l_eff: float = 0.8,
    l_head: float = 0.9,
    g: float = GRAVITY_STANDARD,
) -> tuple[float, float, float]:
    """Calculate small-angle and exact non-linear release amplitude."""
    s_m = stimpmeter_s_ft * 0.3048
    d_m = dist_ft * 0.3048
    a0 = (v_calib**2) / (2.0 * s_m)
    u_roll = np.sqrt(2.0 * a0 * d_m)
    u_head = u_roll / k_imp
    omega = np.sqrt(g / l_eff)
    theta_approx = u_head / (l_head * omega)

    arg = 1.0 - (l_eff / (2.0 * g)) * (u_head / l_head) ** 2
    theta_exact = float(np.arccos(np.clip(arg, -1.0, 1.0)))
    period = 2.0 * np.pi * np.sqrt(l_eff / g)
    return theta_approx, theta_exact, period


def run_randomized_trials(num_trials: int = 100) -> bool:
    """Run 100 randomized trials testing skid, rolling and energy relations."""
    rng = np.random.default_rng(seed=42)
    all_passed = True

    for trial_idx in range(num_trials):
        v0 = rng.uniform(0.5, 5.0)
        omega0 = rng.uniform(-40.0, 40.0)
        radius = rng.uniform(0.02, 0.06)
        mass = rng.uniform(0.04, 0.5)
        inertia_factor = rng.choice([0.4, 0.66667, 0.5])
        friction = rng.uniform(0.1, 0.5)
        params = SkidRollParams(v0, omega0, radius, mass, inertia_factor, friction)

        if not check_skid_formulas(params):
            logger.error("Trial %d failed on skid-roll check", trial_idx)
            all_passed = False
            break

        alpha = rng.uniform(0.005, 0.08)
        a0 = rng.uniform(0.05, 0.3)
        dt = rng.uniform(0.01, 0.08)
        r_num, r_taylor = simulate_plane_roll(v0, alpha, a0, dt, k=inertia_factor)
        if np.linalg.norm(r_num - r_taylor) > 5e-4:
            logger.error("Trial %d failed on Taylor plane roll comparison", trial_idx)
            all_passed = False
            break

    logger.info("Verification of %d trials completed: passed=%s", num_trials, all_passed)
    return all_passed
```
