# Technical Review: Mechanics, Control, and Impact

Date: 2026-09-05. Parent epic: [#4009](https://github.com/D-sorganization/AffineDrift/issues/4009).

## Argument Being Investigated

The golfer prepares and continually changes a coupled mechanical state. Geometry determines force transmission; inertia couples accelerations; activation and tissue mechanics determine forces and impedance; earlier work becomes kinetic and elastic energy; feedback and prediction manage deviations; impact selects which aspects of the delivered state matter. The useful research question is how those contributions interact under explicit constraints and measurable objectives.

A same-state affine partition is an algebraic tool. It does not independently identify muscle effort, intention, metabolic efficiency, or how the nervous system controls the swing. A forward counterfactual is a model intervention whose state evolves; it must declare its input, boundary conditions, starting state, and comparison event. These distinctions make the argument testable rather than weakening it.

### Mechanical Derivation and Implementation Decisions

The rigid three-link mass matrix is derived both from Cartesian center-of-mass/angular velocities and six explicit independent entries. It includes all specified COM inertias, offsets, and cross terms. Relative joint rates are distinguished from absolute segment rates. Positive joint-space inertia is not lost merely because an endpoint Jacobian is singular. The original web matrix's indefiniteness was not a physical task singularity.

The flexible appendix example was missing the beam's contribution to the q-q block while retaining q-eta and eta-eta inertia. The correction integrates every block from one two-dimensional material-point velocity at zero deflection. It adds the shaft mass to the rigid carrier explicitly, without asserting that the manufactured carrier is calibrated anatomy or that a flexible-tip head mass has been modeled. The block units are kg m², kg m, and kg, so raw full-matrix eigenvalues are scale dependent. A light-carrier counterexample failed before the correction and now remains positive definite. ### Causal and Golf-Swing Interpretation

The acceleration ratio requires a declared projection/normalization and applied input. Zero input makes its denominator zero; an epsilon is a new definition. The full state drift norm mixes rates and accelerations, so it cannot equal an acceleration norm. Nonlinear coordinates add the acceleration connection term, quadratic velocity contributions can cancel, and a speed-dependent input/posture prevents a universal drift-dominance phase rule. Pointwise affine additivity is distinguished from a finite trajectory intervention. Sensory delay does not turn off ongoing activation or force.

Energy transfer requires segment force/moment powers and interval balances. Shared-point ideal-joint force powers cancel for the combined bodies while transferring energy between them; relative actuator power is distinct from each body's absolute rotational power. Proximal deceleration alone cannot establish distal energy transfer. The frozen modal energy derivative includes modal velocity, coupling, base acceleration and damping; base-acceleration sign alone does not establish shaft energy release. Coefficient variation and rotating effects require the full model.

The text explicitly separates shaft experiments with prescribed grip motion, prescribed applied forces, and an adapting golfer/controller. Fixed-time impact sensitivity is separated from event-time sensitivity, with the transverse-event denominator and explicitly time-dependent output term.
