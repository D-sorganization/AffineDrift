## Findings and Intended Argument

The chapter should connect mechanical state, partial delayed observation,
prediction, task goals, reward and policy change. Feel is useful information,
but neither an exact state measurement nor a uniquely demonstrated neural
trajectory store. A hypothesis label in one paragraph does not qualify later
theorems, categorical explanations or exercises built on that hypothesis.

The original mixes tracking error with sensory prediction error; assumes
direct access to actual state; makes successful expected reward a large
dopamine prediction error; assigns LTD to a synapse between parallel and
climbing fibres; treats serial brain-region stages as anatomy; and turns
kinematic covariance into optimality, energy efficiency and injury protection.
The impact example confuses clubhead force with body loads and response time.
The yips explanation is an unsupported single-cause clinical claim.

Learning stages and freezing/coupling are useful historical descriptions,
not fixed neural timelines or guaranteed progressions. Endpoint-equivalent
solutions need not converge to one trajectory. A finite golf swing is not
automatically an attractor, and smaller state covariance is not necessarily
smaller task error. Schema theory, variable practice, random ordering and
differential learning need distinct definitions. Environmental constraints do
not guarantee a particular swing path, energy transfer or ball curvature.

The web edition's10000hours at10hours/week in10years is arithmetically wrong;
neither hours nor genetics establishes a universal learning rate. Practice
performance, delayed retention and transfer must be assessed separately.
External focus and quiet eye require task-specific evidence, and stable gaze
does not imply stationary head/vestibular signals. Both editions need consistent
figures, citations, worked answers and qualification of neuroscience claims.

Model diagrams are not direct physiological measurements. The chapter uses the bounded phasic-response distinction and a separately derived terminal-reward example; no universal dopamine claim.
- [Mazzoni and Krakauer2006](https://doi.org/10.1523/JNEUROSCI.5317-05.2006): publisher abstract and metadata read. Full publisher/PMC access failed; no full-method claim. Retained inference is the reported explicit compensation/implicit drift dissociation in pointing, not a universal golf mechanism. The signed45-degree example is independently manufactured.
- [Schonewille etal2011](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.neuron.2011.02.044): complete indexed abstract and metadata read. Three mutant lines target expression of PF-PC LTD through AMPA-receptor internalization; retained learning on VOR adaptation, eyeblink conditioning and Erasmus Ladder. Chapter does not generalize to all plasticity, tasks or species. Full article and figures were not read.
- [Porter and Magill2010](https://doi.org/10.1080/02640414.2010.502946): indexed abstract and metadata read. Novice putting experiment compares blocked, random and increasing schedules; the increasing schedule has superior retention. The basketball experiment's transfer result is not claimed for golf. Sample size, trial counts and effect sizes are omitted because primary full methods were not available.
- [Wulf, Lauterbach and Toole1999](https://doi.org/10.1080/02701367.1999.10608029): full indexed abstract read.22novices, arm-versus-club focus,80practice trials,30instruction-free trials next day. The retained claim concerns this pitch-shot comparison, not all technical instruction or full swings. No full-paper or neural-mechanism claim.
- [Vine, Moore and Wilson2011](https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2011.00008): complete publisher main text, methods, results and discussion through conclusion read.22skilled male golfers; training bundles gaze feedback and extra instructions. Five trained subjects' gaze records were lost. Retention group differences are not significant for every measure; expectancy and competitive-putting-metric confounds are stated. No universal fixation duration, causal interpretation of R-squared or full-swing extrapolation. Figures were not visually read or reproduced. Detailed numerical results are deliberately not republished.
- [Schöllhorn2016](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.humov.2015.11.018): commentary abstract and indexed metadata read; author is Wolfgang Immanuel, not Karl. Used only to distinguish differential learning and contextual interference, not to establish comparative efficacy.
- [Proske and Gandevia2012](https://doi.org/10.1152/physrev.00048.2011): review abstract read; supports multi-signal proprioception. No universal joint-angle acuity table retained. Related2009/2021abstracts were discovery/context leads, not sources of new numerical claims.
