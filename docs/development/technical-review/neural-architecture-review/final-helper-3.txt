### 2. Independent Mathematical Derivations & Verification

#### A. Uniform Population Vector ([Lines 51–72](file:///C:/Users/diete/Repositories/Worktrees/AffineDrift-neural-architecture-review/articles/The_Geometry_of_Motion/Volume_IV/chapters/ch03_neural_architecture.tex#L51-L72), Eq. 60–65)
- **Tuning Curve:** $r_i = b + g \cos(\theta - \phi_i)$ with preferred unit vector $d_i = [\cos \phi_i, \sin \phi_i]^T$.
- **Outer product matrix:**
  $$\sum_{i=0}^7 d_i d_i^T = \sum_{i=0}^7 \begin{bmatrix} \cos^2 \phi_i & \cos \phi_i \sin \phi_i \\ \cos \phi_i \sin \phi_i & \sin^2 \phi_i \end{bmatrix}$$
  For $N = 8$ uniformly spaced angles $\phi_i = \frac{2\pi i}{8}$:
  - $\sum_{i=0}^7 \cos^2 \phi_i = \sum_{i=0}^7 \frac{1 + \cos(2\phi_i)}{2} = \frac{8}{2} + \frac{1}{2}\sum_{i=0}^7 \cos\left(\frac{4\pi i}{8}\right) = 4 + 0 = 4$.
  - $\sum_{i=0}^7 \sin^2 \phi_i = \sum_{i=0}^7 \frac{1 - \cos(2\phi_i)}{2} = 4$.
  - $\sum_{i=0}^7 \cos \phi_i \sin \phi_i = \frac{1}{2}\sum_{i=0}^7 \sin(2\phi_i) = \frac{1}{2}\sum_{i=0}^7 \sin\left(\frac{\pi i}{2}\right) = 0$.
  Thus $\sum_{i=0}^7 d_i d_i^T = 4 I_2$.
- **Population vector $P$:**
  $$P = \sum_{i=0}^7 (r_i - b) d_i = \sum_{i=0}^7 g \cos(\theta - \phi_i) d_i = g \left(\sum_{i=0}^7 d_i d_i^T\right) \begin{bmatrix} \cos\theta \\ \sin\theta \end{bmatrix} = 4g \begin{bmatrix} \cos\theta \\ \sin\theta \end{bmatrix}$$
- **Evaluated at $b=5, g=2, \theta=30^\circ$:**
  $$P = 4(2) \begin{bmatrix} \cos 30^\circ \\ \sin 30^\circ \end{bmatrix} = 8 \begin{bmatrix} \sqrt{3}/2 \\ 1/2 \end{bmatrix} = \begin{bmatrix} 4\sqrt{3} \\ 4 \end{bmatrix}$$
  - $\|P\| = \sqrt{(4\sqrt{3})^2 + 4^2} = \sqrt{48 + 16} = \sqrt{64} = 8$.
  - $\angle P = \arctan(4 / 4\sqrt{3}) = 30^\circ$.
  - The derivation, vectors, and units verification in [lines 67–72](file:///C:/Users/diete/Repositories/Worktrees/AffineDrift-neural-architecture-review/articles/The_Geometry_of_Motion/Volume_IV/chapters/ch03_neural_architecture.tex#L67-L72) are **exact and correct**.

#### B. Nonuniform Population Vector ([Lines 73–77](file:///C:/Users/diete/Repositories/Worktrees/AffineDrift-neural-architecture-review/articles/The_Geometry_of_Motion/Volume_IV/chapters/ch03_neural_architecture.tex#L73-L77))

  $$\angle P = \arctan(0.288675) \approx 16.1021^\circ \approx 16.1^\circ$$
  Line 76 is **exact and correct**.

#### D. Kalman Filter & Scalar Exercise ([Lines 139–160](file:///C:/Users/diete/Repositories/Worktrees/AffineDrift-neural-architecture-review/articles/The_Geometry_of_Motion/Volume_IV/chapters/ch03_neural_architecture.tex#L139-L160), [Lines 191–194](file:///C:/Users/diete/Repositories/Worktrees/AffineDrift-neural-architecture-review/articles/The_Geometry_of_Motion/Volume_IV/chapters/ch03_neural_architecture.tex#L191-L194))
- State model: $x_{j+1} = A x_j + B u_j + w_j, \quad y_j = C x_j + v_j$
- Innovation: $\nu_{j+1} = y_{j+1} - C \hat{x}^-_{j+1}$
- Covariance propagation: $P^-_{j+1} = A P^+_j A^T + Q$
- Innovation covariance: $S_{j+1} = C P^-_{j+1} C^T + R$
- Kalman gain: $K_{j+1} = P^-_{j+1} C^T S_{j+1}^{-1}$
- Joseph form posterior covariance: $(I - KC) P^- (I - KC)^T + K R K^T$
- **Scalar Exercise 2 ([Lines 191–194](file:///C:/Users/diete/Repositories/Worktrees/AffineDrift-neural-architecture-review/articles/The_Geometry_of_Motion/Volume_IV/chapters/ch03_neural_architecture.tex#L191-L194)):**
  - Given: Prior variance $P^- = 4$, measurement variance $R = 1$, observation matrix $C = 1$.
  - Innovation variance: $S = C P^- C^T + R = 1 \cdot 4 \cdot 1 + 1 = 5$.
  - Gain: $K = P^- C^T S^{-1} = 4 \cdot 1 \cdot \frac{1}{5} = \frac{4}{5} = 0.8$.
  - Posterior variance:
    - Standard: $P^+ = (1 - KC) P^- = (1 - 0.8 \cdot 1) \cdot 4 = 0.2 \cdot 4 = 0.8$.
    - Joseph form: $(1 - KC)^2 P^- + K^2 R = (0.2)^2 \cdot 4 + (0.8)^2 \cdot 1 = 0.04 \cdot 4 + 0.64 \cdot 1 = 0.16 + 0.64 = 0.8$.
  - Exercise values stating "$0.8$ and $0.8$" are **exact and correct**.
