## Complete Original Findings

The introduction claims universality across games and MuJoCo, invents hardware timing comparisons wrong by a factor1000, and describes two passes before later giving three. Dense inversion is neither the required alternative to ABA nor mathematically catastrophic at30 joints. Algorithmic arithmetic counts do not establish a real-time benchmark, numerical conditioning or physical accuracy.

The central articulated-inertia definition conflates a freely articulating subtree with children rigidly attached to their parent. Rebuild from fA=IA a+pA and a=X a_parent+c+S qdd. With fixed generalized torque, U=IA S,d=S^T U,u=tau-S^T pA; eliminate qdd to obtain Ia=IA-UU^T/d and pa=pA+Ia c+Uu/d. These are carried inward as X^T Ia X and X^T pa. The bias propagation is mandatory. Ia annihilates S in the unarmatured one-DOF case; it is not a Euclidean projection of the parent's inertia onto a permitted direction.

Almost every velocity/acceleration recursion drops the frame transform. Several spatial cross products and torque projections have incompatible dimensions. The derivation omits gyroscopic wrenches and external-force signs and identifies an adjoint with a joint screw. Define angular-first motion, moment-first force, Xup mapping parent motion coordinates to child, and power-dual Xup^T for child-to-parent wrenches. Initialize pA=v cross-star I v-fext; c=v cross vJ for constant local joint subspaces, with a joint bias term for more general joints. Unknown parent acceleration is not part of c.

The fixed base generally supplies a reaction wrench. The fictitious a0=(0,-g) is a gravity bookkeeping convention, not the base's physical acceleration. A floating base requires the configuration-dependent articulated6x6 solve after all inward passes; its rigid inertia cannot be inverted once and cached. Configuration rates and generalized velocities differ on quaternion manifolds. Centroidal momentum must use a declared ordering, account for external contact couples and changing COM moment arms, and distinguish cancellation of internal torques from configuration-dependent momentum redistribution.

Viscous joint friction belongs in generalized torque tau-b qdot; subtracting b qdot after acceleration has wrong units and omits inertial coupling. A vector b qdot S is not generally the correct spatial wrench. Reflected motor inertia r^2 J requires its transmission model and consistent pivot/elimination treatment, not only a final denominator change. CRBA forms the mass matrix in O(N^2) worst case; serial-chain mass matrices are generally dense, not uniformly banded. Tree factorization has structure but does not cure a physically ill-conditioned mass matrix. Eigenvalues of mass alone do not classify dynamical stability.

The three-link example supplies COM data without the required reference shift, nonphysical off-diagonal inertia entries, arbitrary tiny rotational inertias, unused joint angles and incorrect velocity arithmetic. It doubles a distal mass and then assumes rather than derives bias and pivot values. The Python example invents a gyroscopic bias proportional to0.1 times an angular norm, silently sets singular accelerations to zero, uses quadratic list insertion, omits gravity/external loads/inward bias and applies damping after the solve. Web U@U.T is a scalar for one-dimensional NumPy arrays, not the required outer product. Replace with a complete reproducible model checked against independent Lagrange/kinetic-energy calculations and inverse dynamics.

Contact requires a signed gap, not a misleading penetration depth. Acceleration complementarity applies to a closed, zero-normal-velocity candidate contact; impact is a velocity jump with impulse units. A friction cone bounds possible forces but does not by itself specify sliding friction or uniqueness. Redundant contacts may have nonunique force distributions. A contacting foot is not automatically welded to the world, and two feet/two hands plus a rigid club create constraints beyond an unconstrained tree. Contact-space J M^-1 J^T and its solver have additional complexity. A frozen-mass velocity step does not establish full implicit Euler. Symplectic Euler is first order; velocity Verlet needs its force/coordinate hypotheses. All15 exercise topics need physically specified data and corrected premises.

Differentiation is valid for a specified smooth dynamics map with nonsingular pivots and fixed branches. It is not a claim of exact real arithmetic, a derivative through every contact event, or a guaranteed1000Hz gradient pipeline. Pinocchio provides analytical derivatives as well as AD support. Full dense Jacobians have O(N^2) output size. The implicit solve sensitivity is d(qdd)=M^-1[d(tau)-d(h)-d(M)qdd]; controller, constraints and impact-event derivatives determine which golf output is being tested.
