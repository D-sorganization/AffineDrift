## Technical Decisions

Chapter 7's original nominally general inertial regressor shared two coefficients across every joint and left additional parameter columns zero. It did not identify arbitrary multibody inertias, check rank, use velocity/damping or qualify physical feasibility. The replacement explicitly fits one downward-zero uniform thin rod: J qdd + b qd + c sin(q) = tau, J=mL²/3, c=mgL/2. It validates finite matching data and positive torque standard deviations, whitens and column-scales the design, rejects numerical rank deficiency, reports residuals/scaled singular values and flags unconstrained nonphysical coefficients without clipping. Full-rank fixed-design least squares has a unique global minimum without an initial guess. Rank and conditioning depend on excitation and declared scaling; structural ambiguity is not cured by more samples of the same motion. Uniform-rod recovery is L=3gJ/(2c), m=4c²/(3g²J); unforced motion identifies ratios, not mass scale. Known geometry makes origin mass/first-moment/inertia parameters linear, not independent mass/COM/central-inertia entries. Physical consistency requires central inertia triangle inequalities, equivalently the central second moment 0.5 tr(Ic)I-Ic PSD. The rod flag is not a general inertial certificate. Errors in differentiated kinematics violate fixed-design torque-only noise assumptions. Priors, model discrepancy and held-out prediction are distinguished. EMG, activation and force need separate observation/dynamics models; fitted torque does not uniquely allocate muscle forces.

Chapter 8's original custom class lacked Gymnasium spaces and returned obsolete reset/step values. Its reward targeted zero angle while its negative-sine gravity made zero downward and stable. The replacement runs the maintained Gymnasium 1.3.0 Pendulum-v1, installed and exercised locally. It uses proper reset pair and five-valued step, local environment seed, validated float32 actions, defensive observation copies, explicit external horizon and guaranteed close. This is a benchmark rollout, not training. The source was checked at tag v1.3.0: uniform-rod inertia, upright zero, g10, m=L1, dt.05, acceleration15sin(theta)+3tau, velocity cap8, semi-implicit step and pre-step reward -(wrappedangle²+.1omega²+.001tau²). The cap is part of the benchmark's altered mechanics. The chapter distinguishes Markov state/observation, torque/action/activation, model-free updates from model-based simulation, physical-time discount/running reward, continuous-action algorithm support, and model/reward exploitation. Termination removes the bootstrap; external truncation retains it using the final pre-reset observation. Intrinsic finite horizon requires remaining time in the Markov state. Gamma1 is permitted only with a justified formulation, not a blanket infinite-sum convergence claim. Golf objectives require a strike event, geometry/orientation and separate feasibility; policy success is a constructive model possibility, not proof of neural intent.

Chapter 9's original drawing labeled distal absolute angles as relative, called static 2D positions 3D animation, treated JJ^T as the ellipsoid itself and implied kinematic ease meant dynamic ease. The corrected figure has q1=30deg,q2=-20deg,alpha2=10deg, with q2 measured from the proximal continuation. Vectorized FK validates relative angles/positive lengths. Phase data validate matching finite arrays and index and copy columns without wrapping; a projected crossing is not a violation of full-state uniqueness. The SVD uses A=J diag(rate_scales); U directions and singular-value radii describe a normalized Euclidean rate-ball image, including padded zeros for tall tasks. Numerical rank, repeated-axis nonuniqueness, task units, box/zonotope alternatives and lack of dynamic/physiological inference are explicit. Energy now includes COM translation and central rotation in consistent frames, absolute segment rates from cumulative relative rates and cross-coupling. Work balance includes actuator/external/dissipative power and impact jumps. Conservative elastic potential belongs in total U; elastic exchange can change T+Ug while conserving T+U. Segment transfer requires all crossing forces/moments and boundaries; distal energy growth alone does not identify a supplying muscle.
