### 1. Mathematical Consistency and Rigour

1. **Equation (13.1) / `\label{eq:designspeeduncertainty}`:**
   $$\delta V \approx \sec\beta\,\delta d + V\tan\beta\,\delta\beta$$
   - **Internal check:** Differentiating $V = d\sec\beta$ with respect to $d$ and $\beta$:
     $$\mathrm{d}V = \sec\beta\,\mathrm{d}d + d\sec\beta\tan\beta\,\mathrm{d}\beta = \sec\beta\,\mathrm{d}d + V\tan\beta\,\mathrm{d}\beta.$$
   - **Assessment:** Exact first-order expansion. The qualifying remark to treat $\delta\beta$ in radians and account for covariance between $\delta d$ and $\delta\beta$ is mathematically sound.

2. **Equation (13.2) / `\label{eq:designfaceuncertainty}`:**
   $$\delta f \approx \frac{\delta\ell}{w} - \frac{1-w}{w}\delta p - \frac{\ell-p}{w^2}\delta w$$
   - **Internal check:** From $\ell = w f + (1-w) p$, solving for $f$:
     $$f = \frac{\ell - (1-w)p}{w} = \frac{\ell - p}{w} + p.$$
     Taking partial derivatives:
     $$\frac{\partial f}{\partial \ell} = \frac{1}{w}, \qquad \frac{\partial f}{\partial p} = 1 - \frac{1}{w} = -\frac{1-w}{w}, \qquad \frac{\partial f}{\partial w} = -\frac{\ell - p}{w^2}.$$
     Thus:
     $$\delta f \approx \frac{\delta\ell}{w} - \frac{1-w}{w}\delta p - \frac{\ell-p}{w^2}\delta w.$$
   - **Assessment:** Correct. The numerical examples citing amplification factors $1.15 \approx 1/0.87$ and $1.32 \approx 1/0.76$ match standard D-plane literature figures often quoted for launch direction sensitivity to face angle.
