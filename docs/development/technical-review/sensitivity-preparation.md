## Lead Questions and Candidate Checks

- Declare the parameter domain, observation map, inputs and nuisance variables. A six-output smooth local map for 12 freely varying parameters has Jacobian rank at most six, but a finite regression rank is not the Jacobian. Do not turn a local rank test into global uniqueness, or infer that every six-parameter subset is estimable. A scalar square map can be locally invertible away from zero yet globally sign-ambiguous; a cubic is injective despite a zero derivative at zero. These are proposed explanatory checks, not yet executed chapter tests.
- Use a manufactured sum/difference observation example to show influential but inseparable parameters, an identifiable combination, and how a genuinely independent measured channel can improve recovery. Include observation noise/scaling rather than promise a real strain sensor will identify stiffness and timing.
- Distinguish planar point-force nullity one from separated spatial point-force nullity one, coincident point-force nullity three, and complete two-hand-wrench nullity six. Restrictions and contact laws affect feasible allocations. Kinematic constraints, force-allocation ambiguity and parameter identifiability are different maps.
- Qualify PRCC, variance decomposition, dependence and interactions. A finite engineering ensemble does not supply a population sign probability or an automatic 50% chance reference. An intervention sign can remain stable while its mechanism remains unidentified. Zero separation removes a force-pair lever arm, not an independently applied couple.
- Separate posterior probability, confidence regions, predictive uncertainty, regularizer assumptions and observed evidence. Unidentified parameters can still support some well-determined predictions. All processing and tuning must respect held-out groups; held-out synthetic draws are not held-out participants.
- Inspect both chapter figures before deciding whether either needs correction. The sensitivity generator was read; figure pixels and the second figure's generator were not yet inspected. Preserve explanatory diagrams as illustrations, not empirical trajectories.
