## Technical Decisions and Derivations

The replacement retains the chapter's purpose: use tangent-space methods to understand coupled motion, preparation, input transmission and delivery. It removes an unsupported historical strawman about twentieth-century approximation and a supposedly new exactness viewpoint. Standard definitions and explicit counterexamples establish the distinction without inventing intellectual history.

- Fréchet differentiability at one point is separated from C1, C2 and smooth=C-infinity. The uniqueness proof divides the difference of two remainders by s without the original extra factor of s. Coordinate changes require differentiable inverses. Directional derivatives alone need not give a uniform remainder: h=x^4 y/(x^6+y^2) is continuous, has all zero ray derivatives, but along y=x^3 has residual/norm approaching 1/2. The C1 scalar |x|^(3/2) supplies a nonquadratic remainder.
- The dimensioned point-mass pendulum has I=m l^2, gravity moment m g l sin(theta), viscous torque c omega and applied torque u. A21=-(g/l)cos(theta), A22=-c/I, B2=1/I. At m=l=1,g10,c.1,theta=pi/6,u5, the exact d=.01 acceleration change is -0.08635109909331418, derivative prediction -0.08660254037844388 and residual +0.0002514412851296888. An initially hand-entered replacement decimal differed by about9e-10 and was corrected from direct computation before validation; tables use a separate cancellation-resistant trigonometric identity.
- Curve equivalence uses a chart, with addition constructed in chart coordinates, not concatenation. For H:M→N, dH maps different tangent spaces. A vector field is a section of TM; its coordinate Jacobian acquires a moving-basis term. T=Dphi(xbar(t)) gives Az=TAT^-1+TdotT^-1, Bz=TB. xdot1, z=exp(x) gives Ax0,Az1 and Wz=z^-2. The transition law uses different endpoint bases and is generally not a similarity transform.
- The integral Taylor remainder is integral_0^1(1-s)D²F(x+sd)[d,d]ds. Its bound uses an induced bilinear norm on a valid segment. A cubic leading term is also O(d²), not an exception to that bound. Directional Hessian contractions, chart scaling, finite differences, integration error and model discrepancy are separated from intrinsic curvature. Flat-line xdot=x² has nonzero residual; a zero field on a sphere has none.
- Differentiating R^TR=I yields T_R SO(3)=R so(3). For deltaR=R hat(eta), etadot=-hat(omega_b)eta+deltaomega_b. SE(3) tangents are T se(3); body/space twists have distinct products and v_s=pdot-omega_s×p. Six-vector ordering is explicit. Quaternion antipodes are a covering equivalence, while a selected half-turn logarithm has a branch cut. The compactness argument for no global SO(3) chart replaces the irrelevant hairy-ball claim.
- The two-link model uses absolute downward angles and a full mass matrix. Kinetic energy gives off-diagonal b cos(theta1-theta2), velocity bias (b sinDelta omega2²,-b sinDelta omega1²) and positive determinant m2 l1² l2²(m1+m2 sin²Delta). Relative elbow torque maps to generalized loads (tau1-tau2,tau2), established by virtual work. The abstract torus admits a constant flat metric; embedded and kinetic metrics are different choices.
- The exact first-variation identity includes Phi and transported input integral. Finite trajectory prediction has a remainder; its accumulated error is the Phi-weighted residual integral. Levi-Civita parallel transport preserves metric norm and solves a different equation. Euclidean xdot=ax distinguishes it from e^(a t) flow sensitivity and from Euler's (1+a h) numerical step.
- DDP's local Q Hessian includes sum_a V_xa (f_a)_zz; usual iLQR omits that dynamics-Hessian term. Both approximate local value/cost behavior; nonlinear rollout, regularization, globalization and explicit constraint treatment remain necessary. Neither a Riccati solve nor a small residual proves global optimality or physiological behavior.
- Driftless bracket-generated local controllability is qualified by reversible controls and smoothness. xdot1,ydot=u is accessible with full spanning drift/input but cannot move backward in x, disproving a blanket small-time controllability claim.
- Static friction is set-valued inside its admissible interval; a one-sided quadratic penalty is not an everywhere smooth force. Prescribed switches, state-triggered events, chattering, sliding and Zeno are distinct. Guard differentiation and common-time comparison derive Xi=R_x+(Fplus-R_xFminus-R_t)g_x/(g_t+g_xFminus). The scalar speed1→2 crossing has Xi2 although R_x1. Grazing/event-sequence assumptions are explicit.
- Golf interpretation uses complete state, task output, feasible perturbations, input convention and event time. High drift can have zero residual and low drift nonzero residual. A stable triangular matrix [-1,10;0,-1] gives transition eigenvalues e^-1 but maximum singular value3.71522166. Endpoint metrics, task Jacobians and covariance correlations determine the practical sensitivity question. None uniquely identifies muscle input history or licenses an automatic technique recommendation.

## Exercise Derivations and Answers

1. At(1,pi), Df=[[2pi,1],[-pi,-1]]. Along(d,d), the first residual component is (pi+2)d²+d³. The second is -sin((pi+1)d+d²)+(pi+1)d, so residual/d² tends to (pi+2,-1). Dividing the residual norm by the squared perturbation norm gives sqrt((pi+2)²+1)/2, not the vector's norm without the factor2. The helper table must receive the actual perturbation vectors.
2. Leading residuals are d²/2 for exp, -d³/6 for sin, d³/3 for tan, and |d|^(3/2) for the last example. The first three have local quadratic bounds, while the last does not. C2 with a vanishing Hessian implies o(d²), not necessarily O(d³); |d|^(5/2) is a C2 counterexample with zero second derivative at zero. Numerical derivatives can introduce a fixed coefficient error times d, overtaking the true remainder as d decreases.
3. The inequality2|x³y|≤x6+y² yields |h|≤|x|/2. On a ray with b≠0, h(sa,sb)/s=s²a4b/(s4a6+b²) tends to0; b0 gives0 identically. On y=x³, h=x/2 and the positive-x norm ratio tends to1/2. Thus even a continuous function with a linear zero directional-derivative map need not have the uniform Fréchet remainder.
4. Moment balance gives I=m l², ubar=m g l sin(theta), A21=-(g/l)cos(theta), A22=-c/I and B2=1/I. For m2,l3,g10,c.6 at pi/6, ubar30, I18, A21=-(10/3)cos(pi/6), A22=-.6/18, B2=1/18. Defining normalized input a_u=u/I instead changes its units to angular acceleration and its input derivative to1; it is not the same numerical torque variable.
5. The first two states are equilibria; (0,1) has field(1,0) and is moving. A=[[0,1],[-cos(theta),0]] gives eigenvalues±i at theta0 and±1 at pi. The downward undamped equilibrium's nonlinear stability can be established by its strict local energy minimum, but the imaginary linear eigenvalues alone are not a general nonlinear stability certificate. The upright equilibrium is a saddle. The moving state's future Jacobian changes along its trajectory, requiring Phi and a defined comparison/metric.
6. Differentiate orthogonality to show R^T deltaR is skew; write deltaR=R hat(eta). Equating the variation of Rdot=R hat(omega_b) with the time derivative of deltaR produces hat(etadot)=[hat(eta),hat(omega_b)]+hat(deltaomega_b), hence etadot=-hat(omega_b)eta+deltaomega_b. The algebra has three independent components; RS is not generally skew as an ambient matrix.
7. Matrix multiplication gives vb=R^T pdot and vs=pdot-omega_s×p. For a fixed body origin pdot0, vb0 but vs=-omega_s×p. The velocity field evaluated at p is vs+omega_s×p=0, resolving the apparent translation. Six-vector ordering and the reference point must agree when applying adjoints or computing wrench power.
8. Constant metric coefficients in angular charts give zero local Christoffel symbols and curvature; periodic identifications do not alter that local result. Differentiating the mass endpoints gives the printed K. Euler–Lagrange yields M and c; symbolic differentiation independently checks the two equations. Virtual actuator work is tau1 delta(theta1)+tau2 delta(theta2-theta1), yielding (tau1-tau2,tau2). For no applied/dissipative load, mechanical energy is conserved, but neither individual segment energy nor a chosen acceleration component must be constant.
9. T=e^x, Tdot=e^x along xdot1, so Az0+1=1. Phiz=e^(t-t0) while Phix1. Transforming the physical ruler gives Wz=e^-2x, preserving the variation norm. Endpoint transformation matrices differ. An unbounded chart derivative can change naive long-time Euclidean exponent calculations, which is why coordinate-boundedness assumptions matter.
10. Euclidean parallel transport is identity; the flow derivative is e^(a delta t); Euler propagation is(1+a h)^N. The first two are distinct exact mathematical maps. Computing exponentials, quadrature or a numerical flow introduces implementation/roundoff effects; Euler has truncation error even though the field is exactly linear. This disproves the original claim that every error is a curvature/transport cost.
11. Chain-rule differentiation yields ell_zz+fz^T Vxx fz+sum_a Vxa(fa)_zz. Standard iLQR omits the final dynamics-Hessian contraction. Q_uu positive definite makes the local unconstrained quadratic minimizer well defined; constraints, finite trust region, nonlinear rollout and multiple local minima remain. Local stationarity or convergence of a surrogate is not global optimality and does not validate biological assumptions.
12. Relative to the initial point, the within-T reachable set is {0≤delta x≤T, |delta y|≤delta x}; it has interior for T>0 but no negative-x point. At exactly T it is the vertical segment delta x=T, |delta y|≤T. Stating whether time is fixed or bounded matters. Full drift/input span does not make the drift reversible.
13. A3N required sticking force is feasible inside the5N bound; its sign is whatever balances the other tangential dynamics. The speed switch has g_x1,g_t0,R_x1,R_t0,Fminus1,Fplus2, so Xi=1+(2-1)=2. Direct common-time trajectory differences independently give2. An identity reset derivative alone misses the changed residence time.
14. Phi=e^-1[[1,10],[0,1]] has both eigenvalues e^-1. Its singular values are approximately3.71522166 and.03642724, whose product is e^-2. Output first-coordinate gain from Euclidean unit initial error is the row norm sqrt101/e≈3.69718. These are different from the whole-state maximum gain. Use endpoint/task scale matrices and actual covariance; cross-correlations can change output variance even when marginal variances are fixed.
15. A possible protocol chooses pre-impact clubface orientation in a declared target frame; includes joint configuration/rates, shaft modes/rates and activation; perturbs a feasible initial wrist coordinate with consistent contact state; and specifies either applied torque or excitation. Derive the smooth flow and event-time correction, then compare decreasing finite perturbations at matched events, several numerical resolutions and estimated model/measurement uncertainty. Independent measured perturbations or held-out swings test physical prediction. Drift ratios, residual convergence, bounded task gain and measured outcomes are reported separately.
