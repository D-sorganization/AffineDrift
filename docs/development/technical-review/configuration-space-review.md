## Findings and Replacement Derivations

The original circle maps x on y > -epsilon and -x on y < epsilon are not injective. Replace them with north/south stereographic coordinates u=x/(1-y), v=x/(1+y), inverses (2u/(1+u²),(u²-1)/(1+u²)) and (2v/(1+v²),(1-v²)/(1+v²)). Each image is R; on the overlap u and v are nonzero, v=1/u and vdot=-udot/u². Compactness rules out one global chart on the circle; noncontractibility alone does not rule out every one-chart manifold. Open subsets of R^n can be noncontractible. Poles are ordinary sphere points with failed longitude charts, not manifold exceptions. Tangent spaces have a base point; TQ is not generally a product. The 2n state statement applies to unconstrained second-order mechanical states, not arbitrary activation, memory, nonholonomic or hybrid models.

DOF is local dimension, not a promise of global unique angles. Independent regular constraints yield dimension D-rank(Dphi); rank loss may enlarge the linearized kernel without an equal increase in finite mobility. phi=x²+y²=0 illustrates this failure at the isolated origin. Gruebler counts assume independent joint constraints. Four-bar closure has assembly branches; input angle determines other angles only locally where the selected closure sub-Jacobian is nonsingular. The original diagram shows an extra irrelevant ground vertex/pin and an unqualified uniqueness caption. Stewart UPS counts must consistently distinguish moving links from total bodies and separate platform mobility from actuator-map rank. Human forearm pronation/supination is not a wrist ball joint; a declared seven-coordinate model can use shoulder 3, elbow flexion 1, forearm rotation 1 and wrist 2, without claiming exact anatomy or universal redundancy.

The general Jacobian's bottom-left entry must differentiate f_m with respect to q_1. Planar (x,y,theta) pose is SE(2), not spatial 3D pose; angular-coordinate rates and physical angular velocity differ. 2R inverse kinematics requires cos(q2)=(r²-L1²-L2²)/(2L1L2), both atan2 branches and reachable-distance checks. Equal links at the origin have a continuous shoulder family, so a generic two-branch formula needs an exception. Newton's quadratic convergence requires a smooth square nonsingular root and suitable full steps; pseudoinverse, damping, constraints and distant starts need separate statements.

Reachable position workspace for unlimited 2R joints is |L1-L2| <= r <= L1+L2, a disk when lengths coincide. Dexterous workspace uses forall required orientations, exists admissible configuration; one full-rank configuration does not establish it. A planar 2R equal-link arm can have every final-link angle at the folded origin despite singular position Jacobians, while a regular generic endpoint admits only discrete orientations. Thus neither full rank nor rank deficiency defines dexterous workspace. |det J|=L1 L2 |sin q2| is area scaling under a stated coordinate metric, not distance to singularity or a condition number. Joint limits/collisions change admissibility. A sample cloud illustrates the analytic reachable set but cannot prove coverage.

Frobenius needs a smooth constant-rank distribution and closure under brackets. Nonintegrability alone does not imply full reachability: on R4, X1=partial_x and X2=partial_y+x partial_z generate partial_z but leave w fixed. For an independently driven unicycle X1=(cos theta,sin theta,0), X2=(0,0,1), the convention [X,Y]=DY X-DX Y gives [X1,X2]=(sin theta,-cos theta,0). Full bracket rank plus reversible driftless controls gives the appropriate local controllability result; finite time, obstacles and actuator limits still matter. A car/bicycle has steering and wheelbase constraints and cannot freely rotate in place. The original coin equation has the wrong dimensions and loses rolling sign; an upright wheel uses xdot=r phidot cos theta and ydot=r phidot sin theta. A ball's no-slip relation is v_C+omega cross r_CP=v_surface at the actual contact, with frames/reference points explicit; a fixed plane gives v_C=r omega cross n. Friction feasibility is additional. Pfaffian rows are covectors; Euclidean orthogonality wording needs a chosen metric. Time-dependent holonomic constraints induce affine velocity constraints.

Every original exercise needs its premises checked, including slider-crank joint types, singular velocity directions, disk exception, rolling-ball reference, and explicit Stewart leg lengths. The golf synthesis must distinguish configuration choices, instantaneous task freedom, admissible contact motion, feasible torques and delivery over time; geometry alone cannot identify muscle causes or optimal swing sequencing.

For exercise 15, write r_i=R b_i, s_i=p+r_i-a_i and u_i=s_i/||s_i||, assuming nonzero leg length and all vectors in the base frame. Since rdot_i=omega cross r_i, the leg rate is u_i dot v + (r_i cross u_i) dot omega. The row in translational/angular ordering is [u_i^T, (r_i cross u_i)^T].
