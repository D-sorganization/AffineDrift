### 3. Critique of Attack-Angle Statement When Horizontal Velocity Changes
* **Text statement**:
  > *"An upward change in velocity increases attack angle, but need not make an already upward motion 'shallower.' "*
* **Critique & Concrete Issue**:
  * Attack angle is the vertical elevation angle: $\alpha = \arctan\left(\frac{v_y}{\|\vect{v}_{xz}\|}\right) = \arcsin\left(\frac{v_y}{\|\vect{v}\|}\right)$.
  * An upward change means $\Delta v_y > 0$.
  * **Qualification/Error**: If horizontal velocity $\vect{v}_{xz}$ simultaneously increases, $\alpha$ can **decrease** even when $v_y$ increases (e.g., $v_y$ increases by $1\,\text{m/s}$ while $v_x$ doubles).
  * Furthermore, for an *already upward motion* ($v_y > 0$), increasing attack angle makes it **steeper**, never shallower; "shallower" corresponds to decreasing magnitude towards horizontal ($\alpha \to 0^+$). The text's phrasing conflates colloquial terminology with coordinate signs.
  * **Shortest qualification**: *"An upward velocity change ($\Delta v_y > 0$) increases attack angle only if horizontal speed $\|\vect{v}_{xz}\|$ remains fixed or does not outpace $\Delta v_y$."*

### 4. Contact Relative-Velocity Formula: Rigid Kinematics vs. Deformational Velocity
* **Formula in text**:
  $$
  \vect v_{\rm rel} = \vect v_P - \left[\vect v_B + \boldsymbol\omega_B \times (\vect p_Q - \vect p_B)\right]
  $$
* **Does it require rigid kinematics or additional deformational velocity?**
  * **Kinematic status**: The formula as written represents purely **rigid-body relative velocity** between the boundary material points $P$ and $Q$.
  * **Collision / Contact mechanics requirement**:
    * During impact, golf ball and clubface undergo finite elastoplastic/viscoelastic local deformation.
    * If $P$ and $Q$ track the instantaneous deformed contact surface, the true relative material velocity is:
      $$
      \vect{v}_{\text{rel, total}} = \vect{v}_{\text{rel, rigid}} + \dot{\vect{u}}_P^{\text{def}} - \dot{\vect{u}}_Q^{\text{def}}
      $$
    * For rigid-body contact models (e.g., Poisson/Stronge coefficient of restitution formulation), $\vect{v}_{\rm rel}$ evaluates the pre-impact boundary conditions of rigid proxy points.
  * **Concrete identification / Shortest qualification**:
    * The formula is **strictly rigid-body relative velocity**.
    * **Shortest qualification**: Add a qualification that $\vect{v}_{\rm rel}$ denotes the *undeformed rigid-body velocity at contact points prior to local deformation/compliance rates*.
