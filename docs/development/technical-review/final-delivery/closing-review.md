## Scope and Technical Argument

The accepted scope covers 407 heterogeneous historical source rows, 27
supplementary sources and eight books' 131 direct input occurrences. These are
not 407 unique articles or a measure of empirical validity. The 38 immutable
upstream publication rows retain their provider authority and reading limits.

The corrected arguments connect geometry and inertial coupling, declared inputs,
constraints and contact, energy bookkeeping, and measurement and inference.
Configuration and contact affect the modeled dynamics and available input
effects; power and work describe energy transfer. The interpretation remains
conditional on the model, coordinates, inputs, parameters and intervention.
Neither a decomposition nor a successful simulation uniquely identifies muscle
forces, intention, human causal mechanisms or a coaching prescription.

The reader guide distinguishes a proof under assumptions from the model's
applicability to a golfer. Under its stated National Academies convention,
rerunning an analysis on original observations is computational reproducibility;
empirical replication requires new observations and uncertainty-aware agreement.
The [guide adjudication](evidence-wording-review.md) preserves the primary source,
accepted and rejected helper findings, and the bounded implementation decision.
