## Arguments and Independent Derivations

Ideomotor planning: TEC describes action-effect/event coding, not a uniquely specified neural controller. Desired $r$, predicted $\hat{y}$, and observed $y$ differ. Exactly predicted misses can have zero innovation and nonzero task error. Prior innovation is $y - H x^-$, not a posterior residual. $P^- = 4, R = 1$ gives $K = 0.8 / P^+ = 0.8$; $P^- = 0.25, R = 1$ gives $K = 0.2$. $R$ is precision-inverse, not gain. Explicit $R > 0$ and $P^- \succeq 0$ (positive semidefinite) make $S$ invertible. A perfect predictor for $x_1 = u$ makes innovation-plus-effort choose $u = 0$ even when target is 1; tracking gives $u = 1 / (1 + \lambda)$. MPC is a horizon/dynamics/information/receding-action construction, not every quadratic objective. Kept variational free-energy identity distinct from the action law and renamed the old internal equation label after checking references. Efference copy goes to the predictor, and the applied command goes through plant/sensors. No universal feedback latency or neural algorithm is inferred from movement duration.

Rate model: normalized $x, v, y, u$; $\tau > 0$, and $\beta, w, u \ge 0$. Equilibrium $u / (1 + \beta + w)$. Sum and difference blocks were independently derived; difference trace is $(w - 1)/\tau_1 - 1/\tau_2$ and determinant is $(1 + \beta - w) / (\tau_1 \tau_2)$. Strict positive trace and determinant establish local instability of this equilibrium, not a unique global cycle or generic Hopf bifurcation theorem. Exact symmetric initial states remain symmetric. At $w > 1 + \beta$, a winner-take-all equilibrium is possible: active $x = u / (1 + \beta)$, suppressed $x = u - w u / (1 + \beta) < 0$, with its adaptation at 0. Positive homogeneity scales drive and initial states together without rescaling time; common $\tau$ scaling rescales time. No universal drive-frequency law. Local phase construction uses $\psi_{ij} = \alpha_j - \alpha_i$; a positive symmetric connected graph gives $\dot{\eta} = -L \eta$ and common phase neutral. Two-node $\delta = \phi_2 - \phi_1$ gives $\dot{\delta} = \Delta\omega - 2K \sin(\delta)$; $|\Delta\omega| \le 2K$ for existence, $\cos(\delta) > 0$ for local attraction, and equality is nonhyperbolic. Explicit LF/RF/LH/RH indexing fixes trot/pace/bound labels; phases alone do not certify a gait. Velocity-peak sequencing does not establish oscillators or energy transfer.

Learning: pretrial $x$, posttrial $x_{\text{next}}$, and supplied error are explicit. Veridical $\lambda = a - b$, $x_\infty = b p / (1 - a + b)$, $e_\infty = (1 - a) p / (1 - a + b)$, and $x_k = x_\infty + (x_0 - x_\infty) \lambda^k$. Defaults of $30^\circ$, $a = 0.98$, and $b = 0.05$ yield $150/7^\circ$ and $60/7^\circ$. First washout error is the negative post-adaptation state; washout decay is $\lambda$, and zero-error clamp decay is $a$. The scalar example does not distinguish explicit strategy from implicit adaptation. `lfilter([1], [1, -pole], forcing, zi=[pole * x0])` returns posttrial states; prepend $x_0$ and drop the final value for the pretrial array. Final state enables exact block continuation. Two-rate state matrix $\operatorname{diag}(a) - b \mathbf{1}^T$ governs feedback stability; retention alone is insufficient. Opposing fast/slow states cancel output, but clamping reveals $c (a_s^j - a_f^j)$; all states resetting means no history advantage in a deterministic fixed-parameter model. Anterograde = task A impairs task B learning; retrograde = task B impairs task A retention/expression. Structural learning concerns task-family relationships, not repetition bias or consolidation. Orthonormal $S$ restriction residual $(I - S S^T)(\theta^* - \theta_0)$ shows possible off-family bias. Club mass/compliance, displayed aim, and wind change different parts of the system.
