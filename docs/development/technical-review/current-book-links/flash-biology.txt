## 2. Proposed Link & Delivery Contract

### A. Clear Partitioning: Corrected Manuscript vs. Historical Scaffolds
1. **Canonical Manuscript Source & PDF (`.tex`, `.pdf`)**:
   - Must point to the latest reviewed and accepted manuscript commit (`fbee4da94fd699e9710f4c29b42a73a5113f08d4`) or the canonical release branch (`main`), ensuring readers access the corrected mathematical formulations and updated scoping.
2. **Computational Scaffolds (`.ipynb`, Colab)**:
   - Remain strictly pinned to their original scaffold baseline (`f287ce6ab2cd26079bbace6b8f362a1f0a729acd`).
   - Do not claim validation or parity with the updated text.

### B. Minimal Editorial Preamble Adjustments

#### Volume III (`books/biomechanics-biology-to-systems.qmd`)
- **Current text**:
  > *"The PDF includes the corrected introduction and Chapters 1-3: model choice, musculoskeletal conventions, and muscle models. Other chapters retain their separate review status; rebuilding the book does not validate the complete volume."*
- **Recommended adjustment**:
  > *"This edition reflects the technically reviewed and corrected manuscript through accepted revision `fbee4da` (all canonical chapters). Rebuilding the book incorporates these formal editorial and mathematical corrections, but does not constitute empirical or clinical validation. Accompanying Jupyter notebooks remain historical, unvalidated computational scaffolds."*

#### Volume IV (`books/human-motor-control.qmd`)
- **Recommended adjustment**:
  - Resolve the duplicate `Source .tex` links for Chapters 1 and 4 so current readers are not directed back to `f287ce6...` while assuming they are reading the accepted review text.
  - Clarify in the opening notes that notebook scaffolds remain pinned to initial baseline references and are not validated against current neuro-computational models.
