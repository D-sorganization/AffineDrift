## Questions Requiring Correction or Evidence

1. The quoted Bosch “core thesis” has no exact page/source. Do not retain quotation marks without verifying verbatim language. A practitioner's organizing framework is not mathematically identical to a specific controlled dynamical system. Expert performance does not imply u tends to zero, and a vector field does not itself “do work”; define forces, velocities, power, task and input channel.
2. Repeated patterns are not automatically invariant attracting sets. A finite downswing cannot be declared an asymptotically attracting limit cycle. Ordinary full-state contraction and orbital/transverse contraction differ: a nontrivial autonomous cycle has a neutral phase direction. State the return-map event, reduced coordinates and stability conditions, including contact/reset assumptions for a passive walker. Drift, closed-loop feedback dynamics and mechanical passivity are distinct.
3. Differential learning is not proven universally superior by adding noise. A covariance lower bound requires an identified stochastic process, forcing covariance, dynamics and observability assumptions. Behavioral variation, anatomical freedom, model uncertainty, neural gain and stiffness cannot be interchanged. High delayed gain need not improve stability or certainty; forward prediction does not automatically lower control bandwidth.
4. Passive walkers establish existence under specific mechanical, slope and initial-condition assumptions; they do not establish generic human walking or golf passivity. Avoid unstable product claims about Atlas generations and actuation. Check McGeer and actuated passive-walker primary sources for the exact bounded demonstration.
5. Tendon stretch does not require eccentric muscle-fascicle contraction, and isometric fascicles do not imply zero muscle power at every boundary or zero activation cost. A universal load/freeze/release sequence is not established. Verify the Ker 1987 attribution and the stated 35% energy fraction: distinguish foot arch from Achilles and define denominator/task. Transfer to wrist golf loading needs separate evidence.
6. The displayed algebraic tendon balance and the Python ODE describe different systems. With fixed external endpoint zero, algebra gives x_t = k_m x_m/(k_m+k_t), hence m x_m'' + b x_m' + k_eff x_m = F, k_eff = k_m k_t/(k_m+k_t). The code instead assigns x_t' = [k_m(x_m-x_t)-k_t x_t]/(b_m+0.001), introducing a separate grounded damping pathway. Its storage derivative is F x_m' - b_m (x_m')² - b_t (x_t')² if b_t is explicitly defined; it is not the stated algebraic system. The initial x_t = 0.01 m violates the algebraic constraint for x_m = 0.02 m and k_m/k_t = 1000/5000 (consistent x_t = 1/300 m). Select and declare one model, units, effective mass, initial preload, actuator work and integration across piecewise force jumps. Do not label arbitrary values “wrist-club tendon” measurements.
7. Multi-segment mechanical coupling is not automatically an off-diagonal input-map phenomenon. Inverting coupled inertia spreads even diagonal generalized inputs; fascia can affect constitutive forces, constraints, geometry and retained state. The input/state/model choice controls the decomposition. Do not imply that shifting an unmeasured effect into M or D is necessarily a validated conservative treatment. The summary table currently presents speculative mappings as exact equivalences despite prose caveats.
8. Audit frontmatter, summary, caveats, cross-volume table and related-link descriptions as well as body equations. They currently promise an established mathematical mapping and universal constraint-relaxation account beyond the evidence. EMG/kinematic correlation alone does not identify tendon energy, intrinsic impedance, load sharing or causality.
