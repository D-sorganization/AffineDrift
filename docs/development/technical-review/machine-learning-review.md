Distinguish state, partial observations, goals, histories, input ports, supervised inverse models, feedback policies and forward transition models. Approximation is not identification, training success or a control guarantee. POMDPs retain a Markov latent state; observations need not be Markov. Remove universal superiority over classical control and passive-dynamics explanations unsupported by experiments. Reconcile the web's explicit forward-model exclusion with the print's PINN, Neural ODE and learned-mechanics scope.
- **P1 — Bellman and Policy Gradients:** the policy-evaluation Bellman equation is said to yield an optimal policy. Separate evaluation, improvement and Bellman optimality; declare finite/infinite horizon, integrability, discount, terminals and continuous-action integrals. Derive the trajectory likelihood and return-to-go estimator for a stated objective, including discount/time weighting, instead of differentiating an unspecified state distribution as constant. Baselines need action independence and stopped derivatives; lower variance is not automatic. The n-step bias/variance direction is reversed. Clarify TD advantage approximation, detached bootstrap/advantage targets, terminal versus truncation handling, and limits of nonlinear actor-critic convergence.
- **P1 — PPO and Algorithm Guarantees:** clipping a sampled surrogate is not a hard probability-ratio or KL bound and does not guarantee avoidance of policy collapse. Derive both advantage-sign branches, old-policy sampling, detached estimates, maximize/minimize signs and stochastic bounded-action likelihoods. The deterministic pendulum policy shown does not by itself define a PPO sampling density. DDPG's actor does not exactly solve a global Q maximization; DQN target/replay reduce particular instabilities without preventing all oscillation. Remove universal sample-efficiency/stability rankings and categorical continuous-action intractability. Model-based methods can use a known model; model-free methods can use physics-informed representations/rewards and are not universally more data-efficient. Do not attribute replay and entropy mechanisms to every listed algorithm.
- **P1 — Pendulum Objective and Evidence:** angle is measured from hanging with upright at pi, yet the reward penalizes theta squared and is claimed to reward pi. Declare a periodic upright error, dimensional dynamics, torque scale and bounds, finite sampling and episode conventions. Squared action is an effort regularizer, not measured mechanical energy or metabolism. No training run supports the claimed learned swing-up sequence. Provide reproducible mechanics/objective/gradient checks and distinguish an untrained network, a supervised regression demonstration and a validated controller.
- **P1 — Learned Physics and Adjoint:** Burgers' operator has the wrong stated sign and omits a declared viscosity/domain/boundary problem. Derivative regression alone is not a known-physics residual; use vector norms and consistent coordinate versus full-state dimensions, force ports, contacts and losses. A residual penalty at sample points is not global enforcement or guaranteed generalization. Define Neural ODE adjoint dynamics, terminal conditions, parameter and initial-state terms, and continuous-versus-discrete gradient accuracy. Backward state reconstruction can be unstable and memory savings depend on solver/checkpoint strategy. HNNs conserve the learned autonomous Hamiltonian in exact flow, not necessarily physical energy or a numerical trajectory. A general learned Lagrangian can have velocity-dependent/singular Hessian; it does not automatically reduce to an SPD natural mechanical mass matrix. Friction/forcing/contacts need explicit terms. Universal approximation has domain/continuity/activation and output-coefficient conditions; it does not cover every arbitrary control policy or promise training. ReLU/GELU, momentum and Adam claims need qualified domains rather than universal convergence or gradient-flow superiority. Backprop computes gradients; the optimizer updates weights. ### Mechanics, Learning and the Golf Argument

The declared smooth-mode mechanics are qdot=N(q)v and M(q)vdot+c+g=B(q)u+J(q)^T lambda+f_other. Configuration, velocity, actuator/tissue/storage state and observation are distinguished. The network's parameter theta is not the pendulum angle q. A forward derivative model has different units and meaning from a sampled transition; inverse allocation can be infeasible or nonunique; a policy depends on available information and task. Earlier action can change velocity, activation and stored elastic energy at impact. A low late command does not erase those retained states or prior work. Classical control can also exploit these dynamics. Golf claims require matched interventions, declared input ports, physical measurements and uncertainty, not a physiological label assigned to a learned trajectory.

Universal approximation is stated for continuous functions on the unit cube with a continuous sigmoidal activation, adjustable finite width and unconstrained output coefficients. This existence result is not training, identification or arbitrary discontinuous-policy approximation. Affine layers include biases. Sigmoid derivative reaches 1/4 at zero; the parameter-gradient conclusion depends on weight and activation Jacobian products. ReLU's derivative convention at zero and GELU's actual derivative are explicit. MSE estimates a conditional mean under its usual conditions; hidden-state ambiguity can make that mean an inappropriate action. Huber thresholds have residual units. Data splits separate trajectories/sessions/participants as appropriate.

Manual column-vector backprop is derived before describing the transposed row-array storage in the runnable module. For half mean-square loss, the output residual is divided by the actual batch size. All gradients use the same parameter values before an update. SGD, momentum and Adam are described as update rules with explicit conventions, not general convergence guarantees.

The finite-horizon objective is J=E[sum gamma^t r_t], with rewards indexed by the generating decision. V^pi evaluates a policy; V* uses optimal action selection. Differentiating trajectory likelihood accounts for action-dependent state visitation. Removing past rewards gives sum gamma^t score_t G_t; uniform practical time weighting can optimize a differently weighted objective. Action-independent baselines have zero expected score product, subject to sampling and stopped-derivative conditions; variance reduction is not automatic. Longer n-step targets typically reduce reliance on an imperfect bootstrap and use more random rewards, rather than the opposite categorical claim. True terminals, rollout truncations and task deadlines receive different continuation handling.

PPO's sample objective is min(ratio*A,clip(ratio)*A). Positive A gives A*min(ratio,1+epsilon); negative A gives A*max(ratio,1-epsilon). Flat regions do not enforce a hard ratio or KL limit, guarantee performance, or prevent policy collapse. Bounded stochastic actions require an actual density: a fixed scaled tanh transform of a Gaussian has a log-Jacobian term. That term cancels in a same-transform old/new ratio but remains relevant to density and entropy. Clipping an unbounded Gaussian instead introduces boundary probability masses. DQN replay/targets, DDPG critic gradients, TD3 minimum targets and SAC's entropy objective have distinct assumptions and limitations; no universal algorithm ranking is asserted.

For the hanging-angle point-mass pendulum, I qddot=u-b qdot-mgl sin(q), and Edot=u qdot-b qdot^2. A wrapped upright error minimizes at q=pi, unlike the original downward-angle square. Input-square regularization is not work or metabolism. At m=l=1, g=9.81, b=0, q=pi/2, qdot=1 and u=2, acceleration is -7.81 rad/s^2, input power 2 W and the resting upright/downward energy gap 19.62 J. No PPO swing-up training or human performance experiment is reported.

The PINN example now declares the authors' nondimensional viscous Burgers problem, viscosity 0.01/pi, domain, initial data and boundary values; fitting derivative labels alone is not a known-physics residual. Finite collocation penalties are not global error certificates. The Neural ODE column adjoint has the terminal loss gradient, negative transpose state Jacobian, direct parameter-loss term and initial-state sensitivity term. The original negative integral with reversed limits was not itself a sign error. Running losses, observation jumps, event sensitivities and discrete-solver differences need their own terms. Backward reconstruction can be unstable.

HNN exact flow conserves its learned autonomous Hamiltonian, not automatically measured energy or its numerical discretization. A general LNN's velocity Hessian can depend on velocity and be singular or indefinite. Its forced acceleration is Hvv^-1(tau+Lq-Hvq*v), when invertible, and autonomous energy rate is v^T tau. A natural-mechanics variant with M=R R^T+epsilon I and scalar potential imposes stronger structure. Primary inspection found that original DeLaN learns a separate gravity-force network; scalar-potential integrability must not be attributed to every original implementation. Explicit friction, muscle activation, contact and force ports remain necessary.

Unforced pendulum angle data identify g/l, not separate g and l; scaling both equally preserves the dynamics. Equilibrium data may identify neither. Calibrated inputs or independent measurements can add information under adequate excitation and known parameters. Domain randomization requires plausible correlated physical distributions. Potential-based reward shaping has a telescoping return with initial and terminal terms; a finite-horizon terminal potential needs explicit treatment. Penalties alone are not hard constraints.
