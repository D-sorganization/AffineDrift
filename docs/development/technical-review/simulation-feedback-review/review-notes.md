## Corrected Mechanics and Algorithms

Chapter 4 originally equated inverse dynamics with inverse kinematics, called RNEA a time integrator and described its two passes as alternative forward/inverse algorithms. Its correctly derived two-link mass, Coriolis and gravity expressions were mislabeled as RNEA; unsupported dimensions silently returned zero acceleration. The replacement uses the canonical capstone uniform-rod model, so simulation and prescribed-motion analysis share masses, lengths, COM/inertia conventions and coordinate definitions. It explicitly solves M a = tau - C v - g and actually integrates with DOP853. Fixed-base planar xy coordinates, q1 from +x and subsequent relative angles, gravity -y, q/v shape six, torque shape three, finite data, increasing output times starting at zero and failed-solver handling are explicit. Continuous callback evaluation at internal stages is distinguished from sampled held input. No contact, muscles, passive damping or shaft mechanics are implied. Finite-difference gravity/Christoffel error remains distinct from integration error. Holding torque +grad U is distinguished from applied gravity force -grad U. The same-state ID/FD round trip is algebraic consistency, supplemented by independent lever-arm gravity, equilibrium and work balance.

Chapter 5's original iLQR value-gradient and Hessian updates omitted cross terms and had the wrong curvature sign. Its alleged line search always accepted alpha=1, and its cost history did not describe the last returned trajectory. Duplicate swing-up headings and unsupported end-to-end/optimal claims are removed. The replacement derives the full Q quadratic and conditional control minimization, including Schur-complement curvature with a minus sign and value offset. The executable quadratic_step validates finite dimensions, full Hessian symmetry and positive-definite Huu, with no requirement that the full Hessian be positive definite. A scalar case yields k=-2, K=-1/2, Vx=1, Vxx=3/2 and offset=-4. This is explicitly a minimization core, not a complete nonlinear optimizer.

The text specifies nonlinear rollout, actual objective/feasibility acceptance, rejection handling, stationarity/termination reporting, and the difference between iLQR first-order dynamics and full DDP second-derivative contractions. Regularized gains require evaluating the appropriate modified quadratic or retaining all original cross terms. A squared-torque objective is distinguished from net work, positive work and metabolic energy. Pendulum wrapping, the zero-gradient downward initialization, duration and bounds are explicit. Golf optimization requires strike geometry, face/orientation/direction, actuator feasibility and a defined impact event; speed alone does not specify ball launch or human intent.

Chapter 6 separates constant-setpoint PD plus exact gravity from arbitrary moving-target tracking. The energy proof uses Mdot-2C skew-symmetry, SPD gains, full actuation, regular dynamics and compact forward invariance. A trajectory remaining at Vdot=0 must be at equilibrium; a momentary zero velocity does not suffice. The unit-mass sin(t) counterexample has PD torque zero at t=pi/2 but desired acceleration -1. Exact-model computed torque gives the stated linear error dynamics with gains of different units. The controller listing validates finite state/gravity and defensively copies equal-size SPD gains; gravity is mandatory and its physical correctness remains the caller's responsibility.

Contraction uses the metric itself, not its inverse. A convex forward-invariant Euclidean coordinate domain plus aI<=W<=bI permits transporting the initial straight path and gives the sqrt(b/a) exponential distance bound. General domains need a connecting-path bound. The target must be a closed-loop solution. Funnels bound finite-time reachable motion under declared disturbance sets; they do not alone prove convergence. The strict inward boundary condition is quantified for all disturbances, with smoothness/regular-boundary assumptions. Joint SOS synthesis is generally nonconvex and time samples alone do not certify all time. Contact resets and actuator/task limits must be included for golf claims.
