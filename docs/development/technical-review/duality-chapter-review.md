## Findings

- **P1 — Value identities and hypotheses:** the discrete derivation drops the control-cost term then adds it back by assertion. Positivity, stabilizability, detectability and terminal-metric requirements are missing or inconsistent. The continuous and discrete algebraic Riccati equations are interchanged; squared-value and norm decay rates differ by factors of two or a square root. The bound proof cites the wrong norm inequality. Nonlinear value Hessians are neither universally positive nor coordinate-invariant metrics; for V=xTSx the Hessian is2S. Finite-horizon value decrease is not an infinite-horizon stability theorem.
- **P1 — Numerical edition disagreement:** print uses a computed DARE example, but web retains the superseded incorrect Riccati matrix, unstable feedback labeled stable and an invented trajectory table. The declared pendulum A and B mix discretization orders and must be identified as an illustrative discrete model or replaced consistently. Compare matrix entries only with stated units/scales, retain reproducibility, distinguish exact dynamics, bounds and rounded displays, and compare a separate solver.
- **P1 — Robustness and dissipation:** gain-margin proofs contain wrong Riccati signs and dimensionally invalid products. A norm-ball perturbation is confused with scalar gain scaling; Nyquist return-difference exclusion is confused with closed-loop pole location and disk or structured-singular-value margins. LQR does not automatically certify sensor noise, saturation, delay or general uncertainty. Dissipativity is not equivalent to stability for arbitrary supply rates. Derive the conditional completion-of-squares and SISO return-difference statements with precise feedback and loop assumptions.
- **P1 — Robust control and estimation:** the H-infinity output omits the intended control term and its Riccati normalization is inconsistent. A bounded L2 gain does not imply negative storage derivative under every disturbance or simultaneously minimal energy. Continuous white noise requires intensity/SDE conventions. Time-varying estimation/control duality needs time reversal. EKF and observer Jacobians omit DG times u and state-dependent innovation-gain terms. Generic nonlinear observer/controller transpose duality and a minimum-rate separation theorem are unsupported. Distinguish covariance from a contraction metric and derive the actual augmented dynamics.
- **P2 — Geometry, conditioning and golf evidence:** optimal LQR paths are not generally Riccati geodesics, geodesics are not automatically attracting or globally shortest, and metric ellipses need not align with closed-loop eigenvectors. Condition number is scale- and weight-dependent, not a controllability test; an uncontrollable stable system can have a perfectly regular Riccati solution. Fixed numerical thresholds and increasing Q do not certify improvement. Muscle attachments do not establish sensor observability. Define measured output, noise, state scales and feasible intervention before interpreting a swing estimate or control objective.
