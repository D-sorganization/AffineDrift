# Article Idea: Measured or Modelled? Camera Versus Radar Launch Monitors at Impact

**GitHub:** [#5393](https://github.com/D-sorganization/AffineDrift/issues/5393)  
**Status:** Idea only — needs the publication gate (see the issue) and Dieter's
approval before drafting.

## Working Title

Measured or modelled? Camera versus radar launch monitors, and what each one can
actually see at impact.

## Hook

Foresight Sports markets its dual-camera GC2 with the line
"Cameras see. Radar estimates." (product page:
[GC2](https://www.foresightsports.com/pages/gc2)).

## Angle

Separate what camera-based and radar-based launch monitors **directly observe**
at and just after impact from what they **infer or model** (for example spin
axis, club delivery, impact location), and tie this to how UpstreamDrift swing
models would consume launch-monitor data.
