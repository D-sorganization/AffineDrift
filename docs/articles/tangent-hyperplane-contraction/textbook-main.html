Contraction Metrics and Tangent-Space Optimal Control

A Geometry-First Textbook Draft for High-Dimensional Nonlinear Control

AffineDrift Working Notes

2026-02-18

Abstract

This manuscript develops a unified framework for nonlinear control based on tangent-space dynamics, contraction metrics, and Riccati-based local optimal control. The central claim is that local stability certificates and local optimality recursions act on the same infinitesimal objects. The document is structured for textbook development: every major section includes qualitative framing, non-technical explanation, geometric interpretation, and mathematical details.

Roadmap and Scope

Introduction and Qualitative Purpose

The purpose is to establish a mathematically coherent and physically interpretable bridge between contraction analysis and trajectory optimization. The practical objective is not only proof-level correctness but usable design guidance for complex, many-dimensional systems.

In Laymans Terms

We want controllers that do two things at once: make motion good and make motion reliable. This draft shows that both goals can be addressed through one set of local error equations.

Geometric Intuition

The dynamics evolve on a manifold, but perturbations evolve in tangent spaces. A metric defines local distances in those tangent spaces. Contraction and optimal control each shape how these tangent vectors evolve.

Contraction Theory Primer

Introduction and Qualitative Purpose

Contraction theory asks whether nearby trajectories converge to each other exponentially. This trajectory-to-trajectory perspective is especially useful for time-varying motions and underactuated tasks where equilibrium-only analysis is too narrow (Lohmiller and Slotine 1998; Forni and Sepulchre 2014).

In Laymans Terms

If you run the same movement twice with tiny differences, contraction says those differences should fade quickly.

Geometric Intuition

Contraction is local shrinking of tangent vectors under a metric, integrated along trajectories into global distance decay.

Differential Condition

For

\[ \dot x = f(x,t), \]

with variational dynamics

\[ \dot{\delta x} = A(x,t)\,\delta x, \qquad A(x,t)=\frac{\partial f}{\partial x}(x,t), \]

a metric \(M(x,t)\succ0\) is contracting with rate \(\lambda>0\) if

\[ A^\top M + MA + \dot M \preceq -2\lambda M. \]

A rigorous trajectory-distance result also requires forward invariance, geodesic regularity, and uniform bounds on \(M\).

Tangent-Space Dynamics and Local Linearization

Introduction and Qualitative Purpose

Local linearization is the exact infinitesimal description of deviation dynamics, not merely a computational trick. It separates nominal motion from perturbation behavior.

In Laymans Terms

At each instant, we zoom in enough that nonlinear behavior looks linear for tiny errors.

Geometric Intuition

Linearization defines a map between neighboring tangent spaces along a nominal trajectory.

Continuous-to-Discrete Convention

For sampled-data control, define

\[ x_{k+1}=f_d(x_k,u_k)=\Phi_h(x_k,u_k), \]

where \(\Phi_h\) is the time-\(h\) flow map. Linearization along \((x_k^\star,u_k^\star)\) yields

\[ \delta x_{k+1}=A_k\delta x_k+B_k\delta u_k, \]

with Jacobians of \(f_d\).

Local Optimal Control: LQR and DDP

Introduction and Qualitative Purpose

LQR and DDP use tangent dynamics to compute feedback corrections that balance state error and control effort.

In Laymans Terms

Take a hard nonlinear problem, solve a local linear-quadratic version, update, and repeat.

Geometric Intuition

The Riccati matrix is local curvature of cost-to-go in tangent space.

Canonical Riccati Recursion

Given \(\delta x_{k+1}=A_k\delta x_k+B_k\delta u_k\), minimize

\[ J=\frac{1}{2}\delta x_T^\top Q_T\delta x_T + \frac{1}{2}\sum_{k=0}^{T-1} \left(\delta x_k^\top Q_k\delta x_k + \delta u_k^\top R_k\delta u_k\right). \]

The backward recursion is

\[ \begin{aligned} S_T &= Q_T,\\ K_k &= (R_k+B_k^\top S_{k+1}B_k)^{-1}B_k^\top S_{k+1}A_k,\\ S_k &= Q_k + A_k^\top S_{k+1}A_k - A_k^\top S_{k+1}B_k(R_k+B_k^\top S_{k+1}B_k)^{-1}B_k^\top S_{k+1}A_k. \end{aligned} \]

Stability-Optimality Duality

Introduction and Qualitative Purpose

In linear-quadratic settings, the Riccati matrix can be interpreted both as value-function curvature and as a contraction metric candidate for closed-loop tangent dynamics.

In Laymans Terms

The same matrix that tells you future cost can also prove errors shrink.

Geometric Intuition

Optimization and contraction both specify a quadratic geometry on perturbations; duality occurs when those geometries coincide.

Decay Statement

For closed-loop perturbations \(\delta x_{k+1}=(A_k-B_kK_k)\delta x_k\) and \(V_k=\delta x_k^\top S_k\delta x_k\),

\[ V_{k+1}-V_k = -\delta x_k^\top(Q_k+K_k^\top R_kK_k)\delta x_k. \]

Under uniform bounds

\[ Q_k+K_k^\top R_kK_k\succeq \alpha I,\quad \underline s I\preceq S_k\preceq \overline s I, \]

we obtain exponential decay in both \(V_k\) and Euclidean norm.

Coordinate-Free and Riemannian Formulation

Introduction and Qualitative Purpose

High-dimensional mechanical and robotic systems often evolve on manifolds. Coordinate-free statements avoid misleading chart artifacts.

In Laymans Terms

Some state spaces are curved, and we need rulers that respect that curvature.

Geometric Intuition

A metric assigns an inner product to each tangent space; pullbacks transfer task-space priorities into configuration space.

Pullback Metric Caveat

For task map \(y=h(x)\),

\[ M(x)=J(x)^\top W(h(x))J(x). \]

This is positive definite only if \(J\) has full column rank. Otherwise, use regularization or nullspace completion to obtain a true metric.

Contraction-Constrained Computation

Introduction and Qualitative Purpose

Controller synthesis can be framed as optimization under contraction inequalities, turning robustness goals into explicit constraints.

In Laymans Terms

Do not only optimize performance. Also require a mathematical shrinking guarantee.

Geometric Intuition

The optimizer selects both control action and local measurement geometry that bends perturbations toward the target trajectory.

Representative Programs

  • LTI metric-gain synthesis via LMIs (Boyd et al. 1994).
  • Time-varying trajectory synthesis with stage-wise constraints.
  • Sampled nonlinear metric search with autodiff and constrained optimization.

High-Dimensional Applications

Introduction and Qualitative Purpose

Applications in biomechanics and robotics motivate the framework by coupling strong performance demands with disturbance rejection requirements.

In Laymans Terms

In complex motions, you need speed and precision without losing repeatability.

Geometric Intuition

Many-dimensional motion is a curve in a large state manifold. Metrics encode which deviation directions matter most for success.

Example Mechanical Form

\[ H(q)\ddot q + C(q,\dot q)\dot q + g(q) = \tau + J_c(q)^\top\lambda. \]

This structure supports physically grounded tangent-space analysis and task-relevant metric shaping.

Implementation and Validation Checklist

Introduction and Qualitative Purpose

A textbook treatment must be reproducible. This section defines minimum standards for derivation quality, numerics, and verification.

In Laymans Terms

Every new equation should answer: what does it mean physically, and how would we test it?

Geometric Intuition

For each contribution, identify the tangent object, the metric, and the claimed decay/optimality property.

Checklist

  1. Verify linearization against finite differences.
  2. Track conditioning of Riccati factors.
  3. Log contraction residual spectra along trajectories.
  4. Stress-test under model mismatch and contact variation.
  5. State assumptions explicitly before every theorem-level claim.

Conclusion

Introduction and Qualitative Purpose

This draft establishes the core architecture for a full textbook on nonlinear control via tangent-space and contraction perspectives.

In Laymans Terms

The same local error geometry can guide design and certify stability.

Geometric Intuition

Trajectory, tangent perturbations, and metric are the minimal geometric triad for unifying nonlinear stability and optimality.
