The Big Picture

This article explains how a golf swing works from a physics perspective, specifically looking at what parts of the swing you actively control versus what happens automatically due to momentum and gravity.

Think of pushing a child on a swing. You give pushes (active control), but the swing also moves on its own between pushes (passive/drift motion).

Drift vs. Control

"Drift" is the natural movement that happens without any muscle effort - like how a pendulum swings back and forth. "Control" is when your muscles actually do work to change the motion.

Understanding what you can and cannot control helps golfers work with physics instead of against it.
