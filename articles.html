The Drifter Manifesto

Theory Part 1: Introduction, System Definition, and Control-Affine Derivation

Establishes the mathematical framework for modeling the golfer–club–shaft system as a control-affine system. Introduces the state space, generalized coordinates, and derives the fundamental control-affine structure.

Theory Part 2: Drift-Input Decomposition, ZTCF, and ZVCF

Formalizes the separation of passive (drift) and active (input) contributions to dynamics. Introduces the Zero Torque Counterfactual (ZTCF) and Zero Velocity Counterfactual (ZVCF) as mathematical tools for isolating passive dynamics and torque effectiveness.

Theory Part 3: Drift Invariance, Taxonomy, Limitations, and Conclusion

Discusses drift invariance, presents a force taxonomy based on the affine mapping, summarizes theoretical limitations, and concludes with directions for future work.

Theory Part 4: Appendices A-C

Mathematical derivations, modal approximation methods, and pendulum examples demonstrating the control-affine structure.

Theory Part 5: Appendices D-F

Simulink implementations, numerical computation methods, and additional technical details.
